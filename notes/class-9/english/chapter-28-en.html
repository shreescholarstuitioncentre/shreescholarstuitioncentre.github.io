The Last Leaf

by O. Henry | Class 9 English | UP Board

1. About the Author

O. Henry (William Sydney Porter, 1862-1910) was an American short-story writer, famous for surprise endings and stories of ordinary poor people of New York. Other works: The Gift of the Magi, The Cop and the Anthem.

2. Setting

  • Place: Greenwich Village, New York, a quarter of artists.
  • Time: Cold November; pneumonia is spreading.
  • Home: Sue and Johnsy share a top-floor studio; Behrman lives on the ground floor.

3. Animated Diagram (Labelled)

Explanation: The last ivy leaf (2) clings to the vine (3) on the brick wall (4) outside Johnsy's window (1). It sways in the wind (animation) but never falls. Johnsy thinks her life is tied to it. In truth, Behrman painted it on the wall in the stormy night.

4. Story Summary

  • Friends: Sue (Maine) and Johnsy (California) meet at Delmonico's and set up a studio in May.
  • Illness: In November "Mr. Pneumonia", a cold unseen stranger, strikes Johnsy.
  • Doctor: Her chance is one in ten. Only her will to live can help.
  • Belief: She counts the falling ivy leaves. When the last falls, she will die.
  • Behrman: Sue tells old Behrman. He scoffs but agrees to pose for her.
  • Storm: Rain and wind all night. In the morning one leaf is still there!
  • Recovery: Johnsy sees that wishing for death is a sin. She takes soup and recovers.
  • Twist: Behrman dies of pneumonia. He had painted the leaf: his masterpiece.

6. Theme and Message

  • Sacrifice: Behrman gave his life for Johnsy.
  • Hope: Will power matters as much as medicine.
  • Friendship: Sue's care saves Johnsy.
  • True art: Art that saves a life is the greatest art.

8. Very Short Q&A

Q1. Who wrote The Last Leaf?

O. Henry.

Q2. Where did Sue and Johnsy live?

In a top-floor studio in Greenwich Village, New York.

Q3. What disease did Johnsy have?

Pneumonia.

Q4. What was Behrman's masterpiece?

The leaf he painted on the wall.

Q5. How did Behrman die?

9. Short Answer Questions (40-50 words)

Q1. What did the doctor tell Sue?

The doctor said Johnsy had only a one-in-ten chance. Medicine could help only if she wished to live. She had decided not to recover, so Sue must keep her cheerful and give her hope.

Q2. Why did Johnsy count the leaves?

Johnsy believed her life was linked to the ivy leaves. She thought she would die when the last one fell. Counting them shows her hopelessness and weak mind.

Q3. Describe Behrman.

Behrman was over sixty, a failed painter who always talked of a masterpiece but never began it. He was short-tempered and fond of gin, yet he protected Sue and Johnsy like a watchdog.

Q4. Why did Johnsy change her mind?

The last leaf survived the stormy night. She felt it was a sin to wish for death. She asked for soup and a mirror and began to hope.

10. Long Answer Questions (100-120 words)

Q1. How did Behrman prove to be a true artist?

Behrman always said he would paint a masterpiece but never did. When he learnt that Johnsy would die with the last leaf, he understood her despair. On the cold, stormy night he climbed a ladder with a lantern, brushes, and green and yellow paint, and painted a leaf on the wall. It looked so real that Johnsy believed it was alive. The leaf saved her life. But Behrman caught pneumonia and died. His sacrifice made his painting a true masterpiece.

Q2. Justify the title "The Last Leaf".

The title is symbolic. The last leaf is the centre of the story because Johnsy ties her life to it. It stands for hope. The real leaves fell, but the painted leaf never fell, and it gave her strength to live. It also shows Behrman's love, art, and sacrifice. So the title suits the story fully.

Q3. Write a character sketch of Sue.

Sue is a young, kind artist from Maine and Johnsy's loyal friend. She hides her fear and talks cheerfully to Johnsy, sketches to earn money, and nurses her. She takes Behrman into confidence and acts with patience. Her love and care help Johnsy recover.
