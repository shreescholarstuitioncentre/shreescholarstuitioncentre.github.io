🎭 If I Were You

by Douglas James · A one-act play · Class 9 English (Beehive)

1. About the Play

"If I Were You" is a short one-act play (a thriller) by Douglas James. It has just two main characters and one setting. It shows how a clever, calm person can defeat a violent criminal. Message: intelligence and presence of mind are stronger than weapons.

5. Summary (Detailed)

Gerrard, an escaped criminal who is being chased, secretly enters the room of a man called Duncan. He has a revolver and wants to kill Duncan and take his place, because the two look alike. In this way, he plans to escape the police and start a new life.

Duncan, however, is not afraid. He stays calm and talks cleverly. He explains that taking his place will not help Gerrard, because he is wanted by the police for some serious matter, and the police are about to come. Gerrard begins to doubt his plan and gets confused.

At the end, the police arrive. Instead of Duncan, they find Gerrard, the real wanted man, and arrest him. Duncan's presence of mind has defeated the armed criminal. The title comes from Gerrard's thinking, "If I were you..." (he wants to become Duncan), and from Duncan's advice not to try.

6. Themes & Literary Points

  • Presence of mind beats force.
  • Crime does not pay; the criminal is caught.
  • Irony: Gerrard wants Duncan's life but loses his own freedom.
  • Suspense: short scenes, a telephone and a gun create thrill.
  • Genre: one-act, two-character thriller with a twist ending.

9. Long Questions & Answers

Q1. Give a character sketch of Gerrard.

Gerrard is an escaped criminal, cruel and cunning. He is ready to kill to save himself. But he is overconfident and not very wise. He thinks that the gun makes him powerful, so he is easily fooled by Duncan. In the end his greed for another man's identity becomes his downfall.

Q2. Give a character sketch of Duncan.

Duncan is intelligent, brave and calm. Even when a gun is pointed at him, he keeps his presence of mind, talks cleverly and makes Gerrard doubt his own plan. He saves his life without fighting.

Q3. How does the title suit the play?

"If I were you" is advice or a wish: Gerrard wishes to be Duncan; Duncan advises Gerrard not to do it. The phrase carries irony because Gerrard's attempt to be someone else leads to his arrest.

Q4. What lesson does the play teach?

Crime never pays; wisdom and calm thinking are stronger than weapons. A person must keep his head in danger.

Q5. Explain the importance of suspense in the play.

The gun, the phone and the talk between the two men keep the audience guessing. The twist ending surprises us and makes the play memorable.
