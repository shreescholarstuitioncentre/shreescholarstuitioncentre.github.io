The Fun They Had – Class 9 English Notes (Beehive, Ch.1)

Author: Isaac Asimov (1920–1992) – Russian-born American writer & biochemistry professor; wrote 500+ books (I, Robot, Foundation). Genre: Science Fiction. Year of story: 2157. Book: Beehive (NCERT / UP Board).

1. Story in Short (Summary)

In the year 2157, 11-year-old Margie writes in her diary that her 13-year-old friend Tommy found a real printed book in his attic. It is about school long ago, when a human teacher taught children in a school building. Margie's own school is a mechanical teacher – a big computer-like machine in a room next to her bedroom.

Margie hates school: the teacher gave her many geography tests and she kept doing badly. Her mother called the County Inspector, who fixed the machine, slowed down the geography sector and said Margie was a little behind. He said Margie was not to blame. Margie and Tommy are amazed that books had words that stood still, not changing on a screen.

They read about old schools, where children of the same age studied together, laughed, helped each other and learnt the same things. Margie wonders what fun they had. She is jealous and thinks: "What fun they had!"

2. Mechanical Teacher – Labelled Diagram (animated)

Explanation: The screen shows lessons & questions. Margie puts homework (punch-code) in the slot. The machine marks it instantly. Geography sector was set too fast, so Margie failed.

4. Themes / Message

① Technology cannot replace human teachers' warmth. ② Learning together with friends is joyful. ③ Books are precious. ④ Be grateful for what you have – grass is greener on the other side.

6. Short Questions & Answers

Q1. Who is Margie?

✍ Margie is an 11-year-old girl of the year 2157 who writes a diary.

Q2. What did Tommy find?

✍ He found a real old printed book in his attic.

Q3. Why was the book funny to Margie?

✍ Its words stood still and did not move on a screen as in telebooks.

Q4. What did the Inspector do?

✍ He repaired the teacher and slowed the geography sector.

Q5. Where was Margie's school?

✍ In a room next to her bedroom.

Q6. What did Margie think about old schools?

✍ She felt children had great fun learning together.

Q7. Why did Margie hate school?

✍ The machine kept giving her geography tests and she did badly.

Q8. What was Tommy's age?

✍ Tommy was 13 years old.

7. Long Questions & Answers

Q1. Compare Margie's school with the old school.

✍ Margie's school: one student, mechanical teacher, in a room at home, set program, tests by punch-code, lonely. Old school: special building, human teachers, many children of same age, studied together, helped each other, laughed. Margie felt old school was more joyful.

Q2. Why did Margie envy children of the past?

✍ She learnt alone with a machine that never understood her feelings. Old-time children had friends, human teachers and shared lessons. So she wondered about the fun they had.

Q3. What message does Asimov give in the story?

✍ Technology can help but not replace human relationships. Real learning needs friends and teachers; one should value books and simple joys.

Q4. Describe the mechanical teacher.

✍ It was a large black-screen machine with a slot for homework and tests. It showed lessons and questions, was set for each child by age and ability, and could go wrong, needing a County Inspector.
