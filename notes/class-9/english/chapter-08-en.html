⛰️ Reach for the Top

Two parts: Santosh Yadav (mountaineer) and Maria Sharapova (tennis star) · Class 9 English (Beehive)

1. Main Idea of the Chapter

The chapter has two real-life success stories. Both women worked very hard and fought difficulties to reach the top of their fields. Message: courage, dedication and hard work can make dreams come true.

3. Part I – Santosh Yadav (Detailed Notes)

  • Born in 1969 in a village in Rewari, Haryana; she was the only girl among six children (five brothers).
  • Her family was well-off but traditional; they wanted her to marry early. She firmly refused and wanted to study.
  • She studied in Delhi and Jaipur, where she saw hills and mountaineers and got inspired.
  • She trained at the Nehru Institute of Mountaineering, Uttarkashi, and joined mountaineering expeditions.
  • 1992: she climbed Everest at about 20, one of the youngest women to do so. 1993: she climbed it again, becoming the first woman to climb Everest twice.
  • On the way she saved a fellow climber in distress, showing her courage and humanity.
  • She was awarded the Padma Shri and joined the Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP).

Qualities: brave, determined, hard-working, helpful, independent, patriotic.

5. Part II – Maria Sharapova (Detailed Notes)

  • Born on 19 April 1987 in Nyagan, Siberia (Russia). Her family later moved to Sochi, by the Black Sea.
  • At age 6, she attended a tennis clinic in Moscow where the great Martina Navratilova saw her talent and advised her to train in the USA.
  • At 9, she went to Florida with her father, Yuri, with very little money. Her mother stayed back in Russia for about two years because of visa problems.
  • Her father did odd jobs to pay for training; she trained at a tennis academy and often felt lonely, yet never gave up.
  • In 2004, aged 17, she won the Wimbledon title, beating Serena Williams. She later became the world number one.
  • She said that she was shaped by hard work, family support and a love for the game.

Qualities: talented, hard-working, patient, focused, grateful to her family.

8. Short Questions & Answers

Q1. Where was Santosh Yadav born?

In a village in Rewari district, Haryana.

Q2. What was her family's attitude to her education?

They were traditional and wanted her to marry early, but she insisted on studying.

Q3. What record did Santosh Yadav make?

She became the first woman to climb Mount Everest twice (1992 and 1993).

Q4. How did she show humanity on Everest?

She helped a fellow climber who was in trouble, even at great risk.

Q5. Who spotted Sharapova's talent?

The tennis star Martina Navratilova at a clinic in Moscow.

Q6. Why did Sharapova go to America?

To get better tennis training and chances to become a champion.

Q7. When did Sharapova win Wimbledon?

In 2004, at the age of 17, beating Serena Williams.

Q8. How high is Mount Everest?

About 8,848 metres.

9. Long Questions & Answers

Q1. How did Santosh Yadav fight the odds to reach Everest?

Santosh came from a traditional family that wished to marry her early. She refused, studied further and discovered mountaineering. She trained hard, climbed in extreme cold and thin air, and reached Everest twice. Her success shows that a strong will can defeat social pressure.

Q2. Describe Sharapova's struggle to become a champion.

She moved to Florida at nine with her father, with little money. The family was separated and the father did small jobs to afford training. She felt lonely but practised daily. Her talent, discipline and her family's sacrifice led to her Wimbledon win in 2004.

Q3. What common qualities do both women share?

Both are hard-working, courageous, focused and determined. Each faced hardship, yet continued. Both got support from family or trainers and became role models for young people.

Q4. What message does the lesson give?

The title says: aim high. With effort, courage and patience, anyone, boy or girl, rich or poor, can reach the top.
