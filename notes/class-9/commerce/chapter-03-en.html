Journal (Class 9 Commerce)

1. Meaning

Journal is the book of original (first) entry in which every transaction is recorded date-wise (chronologically) with its debit and credit accounts, before posting to the Ledger. Recording in it is called Journalising, and each record is a Journal Entry. It is also called the Book of Prime Entry.

2. Features & Importance

  • Records transactions in date order.
  • Shows both Dr and Cr accounts of each transaction.
  • Has a short explanation called Narration.
  • Helps find errors easily; acts as the base of the Ledger.
  • Gives a permanent, legal record.

3. Format of Journal

L.F. = Ledger Folio (page no. of ledger where posted). Narration = line starting with "Being...". Dr is written at the left, To is written with the credited account, slightly right.

4. Steps of Journalising

  1. Identify the transaction.
  2. Find the two accounts involved.
  3. Decide the type of each account (Personal/Real/Nominal).
  4. Apply the Golden Rules to find Dr and Cr.
  5. Write date, accounts, amounts.
  6. Write narration.

5. Types of Entries

Simple entry: one debit and one credit account.
Compound entry: more than two accounts (e.g., goods sold for part cash, part credit).

6. Merits & Limits

Merits: chronological record, less chance of error, narration explains the transaction, helps ledger posting.
Limits: does not show account-wise balance; takes time; repeated writing of the same account.

8. Solved Example (step by step)

Q. Journalise: (i) Ram started business with ₹60,000. (ii) Bought goods ₹20,000 on credit from Shyam. (iii) Paid Shyam ₹20,000.
  1. (i) Cash comes in (Real → Dr); Capital is giver (Personal → Cr).
    Cash A/c Dr 60,000 / To Capital A/c 60,000 (Being business started)
  2. (ii) Purchases come in (Dr); Shyam is the giver (Cr).
    Purchases A/c Dr 20,000 / To Shyam A/c 20,000 (Being goods bought on credit)
  3. (iii) Shyam is the receiver (Dr); Cash goes out (Cr).
    Shyam A/c Dr 20,000 / To Cash A/c 20,000 (Being Shyam paid)
  4. Check: Dr 100,000 = Cr 100,000 ✔

9. Short & Long Questions

Q1. Define Journal.

The book where transactions are first recorded in date order.

Q2. What is journalising?

The act of recording transactions in the journal.

Q3. What is narration?

A brief explanation of the entry, starting with "Being".

Q4. What is L.F.?

Ledger Folio – the ledger page number where the entry is posted.

Q5. Why is Journal called book of original entry?

Because every transaction is first written here before going to any other book.

Q6. What is a compound entry?

An entry having more than two accounts.

Q7. Explain the steps of journalising. (Long)

Identify the transaction, find the two accounts, decide their types, apply the Golden Rules, write date, accounts and amounts, then add narration.

Q8. Explain the importance of journal. (Long)

It gives a date-wise record, shows both aspects, helps in finding errors, forms the base for ledger posting and gives legal proof.
