Organisation & Routine of Business Office (Class 9 Commerce)

1. Meaning of Office

An office is the place where records and papers of a business are kept and clerical work (writing, filing, accounting, correspondence) is done. It is called the nerve centre of the business because information flows through it.

2. Functions of an Office

  • Receiving information – letters, orders, calls.
  • Recording – books, registers, files.
  • Arranging and processing – sorting, calculating.
  • Supplying information to management and others.
  • Safeguarding assets – cash, documents, property.
  • Coordination among departments.

3. Office Organisation

Office organisation means arranging men, material, machines and methods so that office work is done smoothly and economically. Principles: division of work, authority and responsibility, unity of command, coordination, right person for right job, flexibility, economy.

4. Departments of an Office

DepartmentWork

5. Office Routine

Office routine means the fixed daily sequence of office work. Daily steps: opening office, checking mail, inward mail handling, writing letters, accounts entries, filing, outward mail handling, cash checking, locking safe, closing office.

7. Filing

Filing means keeping papers in order for easy search. Methods: alphabetical, numerical, chronological (date-wise), subject-wise, geographical. Merits: quick retrieval, safety, evidence, saves time.

9. Solved Example (step by step)

Q. A quotation request letter arrives from Ram Traders on 5 May. Show how it is handled and replied to.
  1. Step 1: Mail clerk receives and sorts the post.
  2. Step 2: Opens it, stamps the date 5 May, checks enclosures.
  3. Step 3: Enters it in the Inward Mail Register (No. 1, date, sender, subject).
  4. Step 4: Sends it to the Purchase/Sales department.
  5. Step 5: Reply with prices is drafted, signed and a copy kept in file.
  6. Step 6: Entered in Outward Mail Register and despatched ✔

10. Short & Long Questions

Q1. What is an office?

Place where records are kept and clerical work is done.

Q2. Why is the office called the nerve centre?

Information of the whole business flows through it.

Q3. State two functions of an office.

Recording information and safeguarding assets.

Q4. What is office routine?

Fixed daily sequence of office work.

Q5. What is filing?

Arranging papers systematically for easy search.

Q6. Name two filing methods.

Alphabetical and chronological.

Q7. Explain functions of an office. (Long)

Receiving, recording, arranging, supplying information, safeguarding assets and coordinating departments.

Q8. Explain mail handling. (Long)

See section 6: inward mail is received, opened, stamped, registered, distributed and replied; outward mail is drafted, signed, registered and despatched.
