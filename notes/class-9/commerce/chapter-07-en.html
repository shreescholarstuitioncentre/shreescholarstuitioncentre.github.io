Trial Balance (Class 9 Commerce)

1. Meaning

Trial Balance is a statement of all ledger account balances (debit and credit) prepared on a particular date to check the arithmetical accuracy of the books under double entry. If Total Debit = Total Credit, the books are arithmetically correct. It is a statement, not an account.

2. Objectives

  • Check arithmetical accuracy of ledger posting.
  • Help locate errors.
  • Base for preparing Final Accounts.
  • Gives all ledger balances in one place.

3. Features

  • Prepared from ledger balances at a date (usually year-end).
  • Has two columns: Debit and Credit.
  • Possible only in double entry system.
  • Agreement is not conclusive proof of accuracy.

5. Methods of Preparation

MethodHow
Balance method (common)Write each account's balance on its side.
Total methodWrite total of debit side and credit side of each account.
Total-cum-BalanceCombination of both.

6. Where Each Account Goes

Debit column: Assets (cash, furniture, debtors), Expenses and Losses (purchases, rent, salary), Drawings.
Credit column: Liabilities (creditors), Capital, Incomes and Gains (sales, commission).

7. Errors

Disclosed by Trial Balance: posting to one side only; wrong totalling; wrong balancing; wrong carrying forward.
Not disclosed: error of omission; error of commission (wrong account, same amount); error of principle (capital treated as revenue); compensating errors; wrong amount in both journal and ledger.
If totals differ, the difference is put in a Suspense Account temporarily.

8. Merits & Limitations

Merits: checks accuracy, helps prepare final accounts, summarises balances.
Limitations: does not prove complete accuracy; several errors stay hidden.

10. Solved Example (step by step)

Q. Prepare a Trial Balance from these balances: Cash ₹33,000; Purchases ₹20,000; Rent ₹2,000; Furniture ₹10,000; Capital ₹50,000; Sales ₹15,000.
  1. Step 1: Classify. Debit: Cash, Purchases, Rent, Furniture (assets and expenses).
  2. Step 2: Credit: Capital, Sales (capital and income).
  3. Step 3: Debit total = 33,000 + 20,000 + 2,000 + 10,000 = ₹65,000.
  4. Step 4: Credit total = 50,000 + 15,000 = ₹65,000.
  5. Step 5: Dr ₹65,000 = Cr ₹65,000, so the Trial Balance agrees ✔.

11. Short & Long Questions

Q1. Define trial balance.

A statement of ledger balances prepared on a date to check arithmetical accuracy.

Q2. Is trial balance an account?

No, it is a statement.

Q3. State two objectives.

Check arithmetical accuracy and help prepare final accounts.

Q4. Name methods of preparing trial balance.

Balance method, total method and total-cum-balance method.

Q5. What is suspense account?

A temporary account where the difference of trial balance is placed until errors are found.

Q6. Give one error not shown by trial balance.

Error of omission.

Q7. Explain objectives and features. (Long)

See sections 2 and 3: accuracy check, error location, base for final accounts; prepared at a date with Dr and Cr columns.

Q8. Explain errors disclosed and not disclosed. (Long)

See section 7: one-sided posting and wrong totals are caught; omission, commission, principle and compensating errors are not.
