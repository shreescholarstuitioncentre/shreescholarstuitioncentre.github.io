1. Meaning of Scope

Scope means the area or subject matter that a science studies. The scope of economics tells us what economics studies and how far its study extends: the wants of people, the production of goods, exchange, the sharing of income and the role of the government.

3. Explanation of Each Division

DivisionWhat it studiesExample
ConsumptionWants, utility, how consumers use income.A family buying food and clothes.
ProductionCreation of utility using land, labour, capital and organisation.A farmer growing wheat, a factory making shirts.
ExchangeBuying and selling, price fixing, markets, trade.Selling wheat in a mandi.
DistributionSharing of output among factors: rent, wages, interest, profit.Worker gets wages; landlord gets rent.
Public financeGovernment income (taxes), expenditure, borrowing, budget.GST collection, spending on roads.

4. Other Parts of the Scope

Microeconomics: individual units – consumer, firm, price of one good.
Macroeconomics: the economy as a whole – national income, inflation, unemployment, growth.
Positive economics: “what is” – e.g., India’s inflation is X%.
Normative economics: “what ought to be” – e.g., the government should reduce taxes.
Science and art: a science because it follows laws and cause-effect; an art because it gives practical guidance (planning, budgeting).

5. Importance of Studying Economics

It helps consumers spend wisely, producers plan output, the government frame policies (tax, budget), and the nation fight poverty and unemployment and achieve growth.

6. Solved Examples (Step by Step)

Easy: National income is ₹1,000 crore: rent ₹100, wages ₹500, interest ₹100, profit ₹300 (crore). Check that distribution adds up.

To find: total of shares.
Step 1: 100 + 500 + 100 + 300.
Step 2: = 1,000.
Step 3: Total = ₹1,000 crore = national income. Verified.

Intermediate: In the above case find the share of wages as a percentage.

Step 1: Share = (500 / 1,000) × 100.
Step 2: = 50.
Step 3: Wages are 50% of national income.

Hard: Income is ₹10,000 and consumption is ₹7,500. Prove that APC + APS = 1.

To prove: APC (average propensity to consume) = C/Y; APS = S/Y; S = Y − C.
Step 1: APC = 7,500 / 10,000 = 0.75.
Step 2: Saving = 10,000 − 7,500 = 2,500, so APS = 2,500 / 10,000 = 0.25.
Step 3: APC + APS = 0.75 + 0.25 = 1. Hence proved.

B. Short Answers

7. What is the scope of economics?

The subject matter economics studies: consumption, production, exchange, distribution and public finance.

8. What is public finance?

The study of the government’s revenue, expenditure and debt.

9. What is consumption?

The use of goods and services to satisfy wants.

10. What is distribution?

Division of national income among factors of production.

11. Is economics a science?

Yes, because it follows cause-and-effect laws, though they are less exact than in physical sciences.

12. Give one example each of micro and macro topics.

Micro: price of wheat. Macro: national income.
