Double Entry System (Class 9 Commerce)

1. Meaning

Double Entry System is the system where every transaction has two aspects – one account is debited and another is credited by an equal amount. Founder: Luca Pacioli (Italy, 1494), the "Father of Accounting". Principle: For every debit there is an equal credit.

2. Features

  • Two aspects of every transaction recorded.
  • Total debit = total credit always.
  • Records Personal, Real and Nominal accounts.
  • Trial balance can be prepared to check accuracy.
  • Gives complete and scientific records.

3. Types of Accounts

TypeMeaningExamples
PersonalPersons, firms, companiesRam, Bank, Capital
RealAssets, thingsCash, Machinery, Furniture
NominalExpenses, losses, incomes, gainsRent, Salary, Commission

4. Golden Rules

AccountDebitCredit
PersonalThe receiverThe giver
RealWhat comes inWhat goes out
NominalExpenses and lossesIncomes and gains
Modern rule: Assets & Expenses ↑ = Debit; Liabilities, Capital & Income ↑ = Credit.
Equation: Assets = Liabilities + Capital.

5. Merits & Demerits

Merits: complete record; trial balance check; easy to find errors; profit/loss and balance sheet possible; legal acceptance; prevents fraud.
Demerits: needs trained staff; costly for tiny shops; some errors (e.g., wrong amount both sides) are not caught by trial balance.

7. Solved Example (step by step)

Q. Journalise: (i) Purchased furniture for cash ₹10,000. (ii) Paid rent ₹2,000. (iii) Received commission ₹1,500.
  1. (i) Furniture = Real (comes in → Dr); Cash = Real (goes out → Cr).
    Furniture A/c Dr ₹10,000 / To Cash A/c ₹10,000
  2. (ii) Rent = Nominal expense (Dr); Cash goes out (Cr).
    Rent A/c Dr ₹2,000 / To Cash A/c ₹2,000
  3. (iii) Cash comes in (Dr); Commission = Nominal income (Cr).
    Cash A/c Dr ₹1,500 / To Commission A/c ₹1,500
  4. Check: Dr ₹13,500 = Cr ₹13,500 ✔

8. Short & Long Questions

Q1. Define double entry system.

Every transaction is recorded with two aspects: a debit and an equal credit.

Q2. Who is the father of accounting?

Luca Pacioli.

Q3. Name three types of accounts.

Personal, Real, Nominal.

Q4. Rule for personal account?

Debit the receiver, credit the giver.

Q5. Rule for real account?

Debit what comes in, credit what goes out.

Q6. Rule for nominal account?

Debit expenses and losses, credit incomes and gains.

Q7. What is the accounting equation?

Assets = Liabilities + Capital.

Q8. Explain features of double entry. (Long)

Two aspects are recorded; total debits equal total credits; all three account types are covered; trial balance can be made; the record is complete and scientific.

Q9. Explain merits and demerits. (Long)

See section 5: it is complete and checkable, but needs skill and some errors stay hidden.
