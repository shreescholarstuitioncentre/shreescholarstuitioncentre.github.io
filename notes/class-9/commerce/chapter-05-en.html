Ledger (Class 9 Commerce)

1. Meaning

Ledger is the principal book of accounts in which all accounts (Personal, Real, Nominal) are kept separately with the entries posted from the Journal. Transferring entries from journal to ledger is called Posting. Finding the difference of the two sides is called Balancing. It is called the Book of Final Entry.

2. Features & Importance

  • Every account has a separate page.
  • Gives account-wise balance quickly.
  • Base for the Trial Balance and Final Accounts.
  • Shows total debtors and creditors.
  • Helps control expenses and income.

3. Format (Two-sided)

Dr — Cash Account — Cr
Date / Particulars (To)J.F.₹Date / Particulars (By) J.F. ₹
To Capital A/c150,000By Purchases A/c 2 20,000
Debit side uses "To"; credit side uses "By". J.F. = Journal Folio (journal page number).

4. Rules of Posting

  1. Open a separate account for each name.
  2. The account debited in journal is written on the debit side; the other account name is written in particulars with "To".
  3. The account credited is written on the credit side with "By" and the other account name.
  4. Write the same amount and J.F.
  5. At the end of the period, balance each account.

5. Balancing an Account

  1. Total both sides separately.
  2. Find the difference.
  3. Write it on the smaller side as Balance c/d (carried down).
  4. Write equal totals on both sides.
  5. Bring it down on the opposite side as Balance b/d on the next day.
If Dr total is bigger → Debit balance; if Cr total is bigger → Credit balance.

7. Merits

Account-wise information; easy balance finding; helps prepare trial balance; helps control and decision making.

9. Solved Example (step by step)

Q. Post to Cash A/c: (i) Started business with cash ₹50,000. (ii) Sold goods for cash ₹15,000. (iii) Bought goods for cash ₹20,000. (iv) Paid rent ₹3,000. Find the balance.
  1. Step 1: Cash coming in → Debit: Capital 50,000, Sales 15,000.
  2. Step 2: Cash going out → Credit: Purchases 20,000, Rent 3,000.
  3. Step 3: Debit total = 50,000 + 15,000 = 65,000. Credit total = 20,000 + 3,000 = 23,000.
  4. Step 4: Difference = 65,000 − 23,000 = 42,000, put on credit side as By Balance c/d 42,000. Both totals become 65,000.
  5. Step 5: Next day: To Balance b/d 42,000 on debit side. Cash in hand = ₹42,000 (debit balance) ✔

10. Short & Long Questions

Q1. Define ledger.

The principal book in which accounts are kept separately with entries posted from the journal.

Q2. What is posting?

Transferring entries from journal to ledger.

Q3. What is balancing?

Finding the difference of the two sides of an account.

Q4. What does c/d mean?

Carried down.

Q5. What does b/d mean?

Brought down.

Q6. Why is the ledger called the book of final entry?

Because transactions reach their final account-wise place here from the journal.

Q7. Differentiate journal and ledger. (Long)

See section 6: nature, record style, narration and balancing differ.

Q8. Explain the steps of balancing. (Long)

Total both sides, find the difference, write it on the smaller side as balance c/d, equalise totals, then bring it down as balance b/d on the opposite side.

11. MCQs

MCQ1. Ledger is (a) Book of original entry (b) Book of final entry

(b) Book of final entry

MCQ2. Debit side uses (a) To (b) By

(a) To

MCQ3. Credit side uses (a) To (b) By

(b) By

MCQ4. J.F. means (a) Journal Folio (b) Ledger Folio

(a) Journal Folio

MCQ5. Cash account always has (a) Debit balance (b) Credit balance

(a) Debit balance
