Correspondence Regarding Enquiries, Quotations & Orders (Class 9 Commerce)

1. Overview

A trade deal normally runs in this order: Enquiry (buyer) → Quotation (seller) → Order (buyer) → Execution of order (seller) → Payment (buyer). Each step is done through business letters.

2. Enquiry (Inquiry) Letter

Enquiry is a letter by a prospective buyer to a seller asking for information about goods – price, quality, discount, delivery and payment terms.
Kinds: General (asks for catalogue and price list) and Specific (asks for details of particular goods).
Contents: how the buyer got the seller's name; goods needed with quantity and quality; request for price, discount, terms, delivery time and samples; thanks and hope for early reply.
Qualities: short, clear, polite; no obligation to buy.

3. Quotation Letter

Quotation is the seller's reply to an enquiry, giving price and terms. Contents: thanks for enquiry; description of goods; price; trade and cash discount; packing/transport charges; delivery time; payment terms; validity period of the offer; request for order.
Types: Quotation (reply to a specific buyer), Price list (general), Tender (offer in response to public notice), Estimate (approximate).
A quotation is an offer; when the buyer accepts it by order, a contract is formed.

4. Order Letter

Order is a letter from the buyer asking the seller to supply goods as agreed. Contents: reference to quotation; description, quantity, price; date and mode of delivery; payment mode; packing instructions; invoice details.
Kinds: Trial (first small order), Repeat (same goods again), Open (supplier chooses goods), Specific (exact details given), Standing (regular supply).

5. Execution of Order

The seller acknowledges the order, supplies goods, and sends invoice / bill, challan and transport receipt. If the seller cannot supply, he writes a letter of regret with reasons.

Shyam Stores, Lucknow, 5 May 2026. The Manager, Ram Traders, Delhi. Dear Sir, Sub: Enquiry for notebooks. We deal in stationery and need 100 notebooks (200 pages). Kindly send your lowest price, discount, delivery time and payment terms with a sample. Yours faithfully, Shyam Lal.

9. Solved Example (step by step)

Q. Ram Traders quotes 100 notebooks at ₹20 each, trade discount 10%, packing ₹100, cash discount 2% (on net price of goods) if paid within 10 days. Find the amount Shyam Stores pays if paid in 10 days.
  1. Step 1 – List price: 100 × 20 = ₹2,000.
  2. Step 2 – Trade discount: 10% of 2,000 = ₹200.
  3. Step 3 – Net price of goods: 2,000 − 200 = ₹1,800.
  4. Step 4 – Add packing: 1,800 + 100 = ₹1,900.
  5. Step 5 – Cash discount: 2% of 1,800 = ₹36.
  6. Step 6 – Amount payable: 1,900 − 36 = ₹1,864 ✔
  7. Check: If paid after 10 days, no cash discount: ₹1,900.

10. Short & Long Questions

Q1. What is an enquiry letter?

A letter from a buyer asking a seller for information about goods.

Q2. What is a quotation?

Seller's reply giving price and terms; an offer.

Q3. Name kinds of enquiry.

General and specific.

Q4. What is an order letter?

Buyer's request to supply goods as agreed.

Q5. What is a trial order?

First small order to test the supplier.

Q6. What is a letter of regret?

Seller's letter saying he cannot execute the order, with reasons.

Q7. Explain contents of a quotation. (Long)

Thanks, description of goods, price, trade and cash discount, packing charges, delivery time, payment terms, validity and request for order.

Q8. Differentiate enquiry, quotation and order. (Long)

See section 6: they differ in sender, purpose and binding nature.
