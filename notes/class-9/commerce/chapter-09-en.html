Business: Meaning, Kinds & Establishment (Class 9 Commerce)

1. Meaning

Business means all economic activities of producing or buying and selling goods and services regularly with the aim of earning profit. Example: a shop, a factory, a bank.

2. Features

  • Economic activity – done for money.
  • Production or exchange of goods/services.
  • Profit motive.
  • Regularity of dealings.
  • Risk and uncertainty.
  • Customer satisfaction and social responsibility.

3. Objectives

Earning profit; creating customers; survival and growth; providing quality goods at fair prices; providing employment; fulfilling social responsibility.

5. Kinds of Business Activities

A. Industry: production of goods.
• Primary: Extractive (farming, mining, fishing) and Genetic (nurseries, poultry, cattle breeding).
• Secondary: Manufacturing (analytical, synthetic, processing, assembling) and Construction (roads, buildings).
B. Commerce: activities that move goods to consumers.
• Trade: buying and selling (internal – wholesale, retail; external – import, export, entrepot).
• Aids to Trade: banking, insurance, transport, warehousing, advertising.

6. Establishing a Business (Steps)

  1. Choice of business – interest, skill, demand, profit chances.
  2. Size of business – small, medium, large.
  3. Form of organisation – sole proprietorship, partnership, company.
  4. Location – nearness to market, raw material, labour, transport, power.
  5. Finance – fixed and working capital.
  6. Licences and registration – GST, shop licence etc.
  7. Plant and machinery, staff, raw material.
  8. Business plan and start.

7. Merits & Risks

Merits: profit, employment, goods and services for society, growth of economy.
Risks: loss, competition, changes in demand, natural calamities.

9. Solved Example (step by step)

Q. Ram plans a grocery shop. Fixed capital: fittings ₹40,000, furniture ₹20,000, equipment ₹10,000. Working capital: stock ₹80,000, cash ₹20,000, salary reserve ₹30,000. Find total capital required and classify the activity.
  1. Step 1: Fixed capital = 40,000 + 20,000 + 10,000 = ₹70,000.
  2. Step 2: Working capital = 80,000 + 20,000 + 30,000 = ₹1,30,000.
  3. Step 3: Total capital = 70,000 + 1,30,000 = ₹2,00,000.
  4. Step 4: Buying from wholesalers and selling to consumers is Commerce → Trade → Retail (internal).
  5. Step 5: Also choose form (sole proprietorship), location near customers, and get GST and shop licence ✔

10. Short & Long Questions

Q1. Define business.

Regular economic activity of producing or exchanging goods and services to earn profit.

Q2. State two features of business.

Profit motive and risk.

Q3. What is industry?

Activities concerned with production of goods.

Q4. What is commerce?

Activities that help goods reach consumers: trade and aids to trade.

Q5. Name aids to trade.

Banking, insurance, transport, warehousing, advertising.

Q6. What is working capital?

Capital needed for day-to-day running such as stock and cash.

Q7. Differentiate business, profession and employment. (Long)

See section 4: reward (profit/fees/salary), capital, risk and examples differ.

Q8. Explain steps of establishing a business. (Long)

Choose business, decide size, select form, choose location, arrange finance, take licences, arrange plant and staff, prepare plan and start.
