Commercial Correspondence – Meaning, Importance & Qualities (Class 9 Commerce)

1. Meaning

Commercial (Business) Correspondence means exchange of information by letters, e-mails etc. between a business and its customers, suppliers, banks and others, for business purposes. Examples: inquiry, quotation, order, complaint, reminder.

2. Types

Internal: within the firm (memos, circulars to staff).
External: with outsiders (customers, suppliers, banks, insurers).

3. Importance

  • Link between buyers and sellers who are far apart.
  • Written proof of dealings (evidence).
  • Builds goodwill and relations.
  • Helps in promoting sales.
  • Economical compared to personal visits.
  • Useful for reference in future.

4. Qualities (7 Cs and more)

QualityMeaning
ClearnessMessage easy to understand; simple words
ConcisenessShort, no extra words
CourtesyPolite and respectful tone
CorrectnessRight facts, figures, grammar
CompletenessAll required information given
ConsiderationReader's point of view ("You" attitude)
ConcretenessSpecific facts, not vague
Neatness & PromptnessGood layout; quick reply

5. Parts of a Business Letter

  1. Letterhead – name, address, phone, e-mail.
  2. Reference no. and Date.
  3. Inside address – receiver's name and address.
  4. Salutation – Dear Sir/Madam.
  5. Subject line.
  6. Body – introduction, main matter, conclusion.
  7. Complimentary close – Yours faithfully.
  8. Signature and designation.
  9. Enclosures (if any).

6. Types of Business Letters

Inquiry, quotation, order, execution of order, complaint, adjustment, credit, collection/reminder, sales letters, bank and insurance letters.

7. Do's and Don'ts

Do: be polite, brief, clear, check facts, reply quickly, keep a copy.
Don't: use harsh words, slang, long sentences, vague terms, spelling mistakes.

9. Solved Example (step by step)

Q. A letter says: "Send goods immediately!!! Else we will cancel. Our order was sometime back." Find the qualities violated and rewrite it.
  1. Step 1 – Tone: "immediately!!!" and "Else we will cancel" are rude → Courtesy missing.
  2. Step 2 – Detail: "sometime back" and no order number → Concreteness and Completeness missing.
  3. Step 3 – Reader: No reason or request → Consideration missing.
  4. Step 4 – Rewrite: "Dear Sir, Our order No. 45 dated 10 April for 100 notebooks has not reached us. Kindly send the goods by 20 May, as our stock is finishing. We shall be thankful for your early help. Yours faithfully, ..."
  5. Step 5 – Check: Polite ✔, specific ✔, complete ✔, short ✔.

10. Short & Long Questions

Q1. What is commercial correspondence?

Exchange of information through letters etc. for business purposes.

Q2. Name two types.

Internal and external.

Q3. State two importances.

Written proof and goodwill building.

Q4. What is courtesy?

Polite and respectful tone in writing.

Q5. What is salutation?

Greeting such as Dear Sir.

Q6. When is Yours faithfully used?

When the letter starts with Dear Sir/Madam.

Q7. Explain importance of commercial correspondence. (Long)

It links distant parties, gives written evidence, builds goodwill, promotes sales, is economical and useful for reference.

Q8. Explain qualities of a good business letter. (Long)

Clearness, conciseness, courtesy, correctness, completeness, consideration, concreteness, neatness and promptness; see section 4.
