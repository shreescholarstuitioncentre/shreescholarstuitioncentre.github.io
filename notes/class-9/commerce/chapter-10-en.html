Record of Inward & Outward Correspondence (Class 9 Commerce)

1. Meaning

Correspondence means exchange of letters, e-mails and messages between the business and outsiders. Inward correspondence = letters received by the office. Outward correspondence = letters sent out from the office. Keeping a written record of both is necessary.

2. Importance of Records

  • No letter is lost or forgotten.
  • Proof of receipt and despatch (evidence).
  • Easy tracing and reminders.
  • Control over postage expenses.
  • Helps in quick reply and follow-up.

Also called Outward Mail Book / Despatch Register. Shows postage spent and mode of sending.

5. Procedure

Inward: receive → sort → open → stamp date → enter in register → distribute → obtain receipt in peon book.
Outward: draft → sign → keep copy → enter in register → seal, stamp → despatch.

6. Related Records

Peon Book: proof of delivery inside office. Postage Book: record of stamps used. Remittance Book: records cheques/money received by post. Reminder/Follow-up: tracks unanswered letters.

9. Solved Example (step by step)

Q. Enter in registers: (a) 2 May – letter dated 30 Apr from Ram Traders, asking quotation, sent to Purchase. (b) 3 May – letter dated 1 May from Shyam & Co., order, to Sales. (c) 4 May – reply sent to Ram Traders by speed post, postage ₹10.
  1. Step 1 (a): Inward Register: S.No. 1 | 2 May | Ram Traders | 30 Apr | Quotation | Purchase.
  2. Step 2 (b): Inward Register: S.No. 2 | 3 May | Shyam & Co. | 1 May | Order | Sales.
  3. Step 3: Check: serial numbers run 1, 2 without gaps and dates are in order.
  4. Step 4 (c): Outward Register: S.No. 1 | 4 May | Ram Traders | Reply to quotation | Speed post | ₹10.
  5. Step 5: Write "Replied" in remarks of Inward entry 1 and keep a copy in file ✔
  6. Postage total: ₹10.

10. Short & Long Questions

Q1. What is inward correspondence?

Letters received by the business.

Q2. What is outward correspondence?

Letters sent from the business.

Q3. Why keep a record of correspondence?

For evidence, tracing, follow-up and control.

Q4. What is Inward Mail Register?

Register recording every letter received.

Q5. What is Peon Book?

Book where the receiver signs as proof of delivery within the office.

Q6. What is Postage Book?

Record of postage stamps used.

Q7. Differentiate inward and outward registers. (Long)

See section 7: meaning, key columns and copy kept differ.

Q8. Explain the procedure of recording inward mail. (Long)

Receive and sort, open, stamp date, enter in register, distribute and take receipt in peon book.
