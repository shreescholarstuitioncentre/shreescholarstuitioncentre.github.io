Constituents of a Business Letter (Class 9 Commerce)

1. Meaning

Constituents are the parts or components that together make a complete business letter. A well-arranged letter creates a good impression and avoids confusion.

2. Essential Constituents

No.PartDetails
1Heading / LetterheadFirm name, address, phone, e-mail, GST no., logo; printed at top
2Reference No.File/serial number for easy tracing (e.g., SS/25/101)
3DateDate of writing, e.g., 5 May 2026
4Inside AddressName, designation and address of receiver
5SalutationGreeting: Dear Sir / Dear Madam / Dear Sirs
6Subject LineOne-line summary of the matter
7BodyOpening, main matter, closing paragraph
8Complimentary CloseYours faithfully / Yours sincerely
9Signature & DesignationSignature, printed name, post

3. Optional Constituents

  • Attention Line – to a particular person/department.
  • Enclosure – papers sent along (Encl.: Cheque).
  • Copy to (cc) – others getting a copy.
  • Postscript (P.S.) – afterthought.
  • Identification marks – initials of dictator/typist (SL/AK).

4. Parts of the Body

Opening: refers to earlier letter or states purpose.
Main matter: facts, details, request, prices etc. in short paragraphs.
Closing: thanks, request for action, hope for reply.

5. Salutation and Close Pairing

SalutationComplimentary Close
Dear Sir / Madam / SirsYours faithfully
Dear Mr. SharmaYours sincerely

6. Layout Styles

StyleFeature
IndentedEach line of inside address and paragraphs starts a little further right
BlockAll parts start at left; date, close and signature at right
Semi-blockParagraphs indented, other parts as in block
Full blockEverything starts at the left margin

7. Merits of Proper Format

Good impression; easy reading; easy filing and tracing; looks professional; avoids mistakes.

9. Solved Example (step by step)

Q. Shyam Stores, Hazratganj, Lucknow wants to order 100 notebooks from Ram Traders, Chandni Chowk, Delhi on 5 May 2026 as per their quotation dated 1 May, and send a cheque. Write the letter constituent by constituent.
  1. Step 1 – Letterhead: SHYAM STORES, Hazratganj, Lucknow – 226001.
  2. Step 2 – Ref. & Date: Ref: SS/25/101, Date: 5 May 2026.
  3. Step 3 – Inside address: The Manager, Ram Traders, Chandni Chowk, Delhi.
  4. Step 4 – Salutation: Dear Sir,
  5. Step 5 – Subject: Sub: Order for 100 notebooks.
  6. Step 6 – Body: Opening: "With reference to your quotation dated 1 May…"; Main: "please send 100 notebooks at quoted price"; Closing: "Kindly deliver by 20 May. Thank you."
  7. Step 7 – Close and Signature: Yours faithfully, (Shyam Lal), Proprietor.
  8. Step 8 – Encl./cc: Encl.: Cheque No. 456; cc: Accounts Dept. ✔ (Dear Sir pairs with Yours faithfully ✔)

10. Short & Long Questions

Q1. What are constituents of a business letter?

The parts that together make a complete letter.

Q2. What is a letterhead?

Printed top part showing firm name, address and contact details.

Q3. Why is reference number given?

For easy tracing and filing.

Q4. What is inside address?

Name and address of the receiver written on the letter.

Q5. What is subject line?

One-line summary of the letter.

Q6. What is enclosure?

Papers sent along with the letter.

Q7. Explain the essential constituents. (Long)

Letterhead, reference number, date, inside address, salutation, subject line, body, complimentary close and signature with designation; see section 2.

Q8. Explain layout styles. (Long)

Indented, block, semi-block and full block differ in where each part starts on the page; see section 6.
