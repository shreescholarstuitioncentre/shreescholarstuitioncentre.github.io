Class 9 Drawing & Painting – Ch.9: Memory Drawing (Smriti Chitran)

UP Board • Notes in handwritten style

1. What is Memory Drawing?

Memory drawing is making a picture of something we have seen, heard or experienced earlier, without the object or scene in front of us. The artist depends on memory, observation and imagination.

Difference: in object/still-life drawing the thing is in front of us; in memory drawing it is recalled from the mind. In imaginative drawing the scene may never have been seen at all.

2. Importance

✦ Strengthens memory and concentration.
✦ Develops imagination and originality.
✦ Improves observation of daily life.
✦ Helps in composition and story-telling through pictures.

3. Common Subjects

Fair (mela), village scene, market, Diwali, Holi, rainy day, playground, wedding procession, farmers in the field, railway station, a festival of your town.

4. How to Improve Memory for Drawing

1. Observe carefully: shapes, colours, actions.
2. Make small quick sketches of people, animals, trees, vehicles.
3. Note key points in words (e.g., "red umbrella, wet road").
4. Practise daily and recall scenes before sleeping.
5. Remember proportion: a person is about 7–8 heads tall.

5. Composition Rules

RuleMeaning
Main figurePlace the main subject near the centre or on a third; make it largest and brightest.
OverlappingObjects overlap for depth.
No empty spaceFill ground and sky with suitable details.
Colour schemeChoose colours matching the mood of the event.

7. Worked Examples

Example 1: List things to draw for "Diwali".
Step 1: Think of the scene: home and street at night.
Step 2: Main figure: a family with lamps.
Step 3: Add diyas, rangoli, sweets, fireworks, lights on houses.
Step 4: Colours: dark blue sky with yellow-orange lights.
Example 2: Where to place the main figure on a 30 cm wide sheet?
Given: width = 30 cm.
Step 1: One third = 30 ÷ 3 = 10 cm.
Step 2: Place the main figure at 10 cm (or 20 cm) from the left edge.
Answer: 10 cm or 20 cm.
Example 3: Choose colours for a rainy day.
Step 1: Sky: blue + a little black = grey-blue.
Step 2: Ground and trees: deep greens.
Step 3: Umbrella: red or yellow for contrast.
Answer: cool grey-blue background with a bright umbrella.
