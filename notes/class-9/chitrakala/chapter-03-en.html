1. Meaning of Rekhankan (Drawing)

Drawing is the art of creating pictures with lines using pencil, pen, charcoal or crayon. It is the foundation of painting; a good painting always stands on a good drawing.

Definition: Drawing is the representation of an object or idea on a surface mainly with the help of lines and tones.

2. Qualities of a Good Line

✦ Confident – drawn in one smooth stroke, not scratchy.
✦ Expressive – shows the character of the object.
✦ Controlled thickness – thick for near/dark parts, thin for far/light parts.
✦ Flowing – has rhythm and direction.

3. Types of Drawing

TypeMeaning
Freehand drawingMade with the hand only, without scale or compass; builds observation.
Geometrical drawingMade with instruments (scale, compass, set-square); exact and measured.
Object drawingDrawn by looking at a real object (pot, fruit, leaf).
Memory drawingDrawn from imagination or memory of things seen.

4. Drawing Materials

PencilUse
H, 2HHard, light lines; construction lines
HBMedium; general outlines
2B, 4B, 6BSoft, dark; shading

Also: drawing paper, kneaded eraser, sharpener, drawing board, charcoal, pen and ink.

5. Techniques of Shading

TechniqueHow
HatchingParallel lines close together; closer = darker.
Cross-hatchingSecond set of lines crossing the first; very dark tone.
StipplingDots; more dots = darker.
Blending / smudgingRubbing with finger or tissue for smooth tone.

6. Steps of Making a Drawing

1. Observe the object carefully.
2. Draw a light centre line and basic shapes (construction).
3. Check proportion of parts.
4. Draw the final outline.
5. Add details.
6. Fill light and shade and clean extra lines.

7. Precautions

Keep the pencil sharp, begin with light lines, do not rub the paper too much, keep the sheet clean, and keep your eye on the object while drawing.
