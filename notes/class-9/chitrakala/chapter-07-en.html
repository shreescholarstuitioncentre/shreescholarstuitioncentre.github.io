Class 9 Drawing & Painting – Ch.7: Landscape Drawing (Prakritik Drishya Chitran)

UP Board • Notes in handwritten style

1. What is Landscape Drawing?

A picture of natural scenery – sky, hills, trees, rivers, fields, clouds and the sun – is called a landscape. Drawing it from observation or memory is landscape drawing.

It teaches observation, sense of distance, colour harmony and the beauty of nature.

2. Materials

Drawing sheet, HB and 2B pencils, eraser, water colours or poster colours, round and flat brushes, palette, water pot, cloth.

3. Parts of a Landscape

PartMeaning
Foreground (Agrabhumi)Nearest part: big trees, grass, stones; strong, dark, clear.
Middle ground (Madhyabhumi)Middle part: river, fields, smaller trees, houses.
Background (Prishthabhumi)Farthest part: hills, sky; pale, bluish, soft.
Horizon lineLine where the earth seems to meet the sky.

4. Perspective (Depth)

Linear: far objects look smaller; parallel lines (road, river banks) meet at a point on the horizon. Aerial (colour) perspective: far objects look lighter, bluish and less detailed; near objects look darker, brighter and clear.

5. Colours in Landscape

Sky – blue with white clouds (sunset: yellow, orange, red, violet). Trees – many greens made by mixing yellow and blue with a little red or white. Mountains – blue-grey. Water – blue with reflections. Earth – brown, ochre.

6. Steps to Make a Landscape

1. Observe the scene and plan.
2. Draw the horizon line lightly.
3. Sketch hills, then river and trees, then foreground objects.
4. Paint the sky first with light washes.
5. Paint background, middle ground and foreground in order (light to dark).
6. Add details, shadows and highlights; clean the sheet.

7. Worked Examples

Example 1: Where to place the horizon? Sheet height = 21 cm.
Step 1: Divide into thirds: 21 ÷ 3 = 7 cm.
Step 2: For a big sky, draw horizon at 14 cm from top (sky = 2/3). For more land, at 7 cm from top.
Step 3: Avoid exactly the middle (10.5 cm); it looks dull. Answer: 7 cm or 14 cm.
Example 2: Three equal trees at different distances.
Step 1: Nearest tree = 8 cm tall.
Step 2: Middle tree smaller = 5 cm.
Step 3: Farthest tree = 3 cm and paint it lighter, bluish-green. Answer: 8, 5, 3 cm; fading colour.
Example 3: Colouring a sunset sky.
Step 1: Wet the sheet lightly.
Step 2: Yellow near the horizon, then orange, then red.
Step 3: Violet-blue at the top; blend edges. Answer: graded sunset sky.
