Chapter 14 : Balanced Diet

Class 9 · Home Science · UP Board (Notes)

1. What is a balanced diet?

A diet that contains all nutrients (carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, water) and fibre in proper amounts and proportion as needed by the person is a balanced diet.

Balanced diet = right nutrients + right amount + right proportion

2. Importance

  • Proper physical and mental growth.
  • Higher immunity.
  • Protects from malnutrition and deficiency diseases.
  • Better efficiency and long life.

3. Calorie and BMR

Calorie: unit of energy. 1 kilocalorie (kcal) = heat needed to raise 1 kg of water by 1°C.

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate): minimum energy needed at rest for breathing, heartbeat, body temperature etc. (about 1 kcal per kg per hour). It depends on age, sex and body size.

4. Factors affecting dietary needs

  • Age: children and adolescents need more for growth.
  • Sex: men generally need more energy.
  • Type of work: light, moderate, heavy.
  • Climate: more energy in cold.
  • Health/condition: pregnancy, lactation, illness.
  • Body size.

6. Solved example: calculating energy

Given: A food has 50 g carbohydrate, 10 g protein, 5 g fat.
To find: Total energy.
Formula: carbohydrate 4, protein 4, fat 9 kcal/g.
Solution:
From carbohydrate = 50 × 4 = 200 kcal
From protein = 10 × 4 = 40 kcal
From fat = 5 × 9 = 45 kcal
Total = 200 + 40 + 45 = 285 kcal
Example 2: Percentage of energy from fat in the above food?
= (45 ÷ 285) × 100 ≈ 15.8%

7. Principles of meal planning

  • Include all five food groups; give variety.
  • Quantity as per age, sex, work, health.
  • Budget, seasonal and local cheap foods (groundnut, soya, jaggery, bajra, seasonal fruits).
  • Family likes, religion, culture, food habits.
  • Attractive colour, taste, aroma and texture.
  • Save time, fuel and effort.

8. Raising nutrition cheaply

  • Eat cereal + pulse together (proteins supplement each other).
  • Sprouted grains/pulses (vitamins increase).
  • Fermentation (idli, dhokla) – better digestion and vitamin B.
  • Green leafy vegetables, seasonal fruits; jaggery-groundnut.

9. Cooking methods that save nutrients

  • Wash vegetables before cutting, not after.
  • Do not throw rice water; cook in little water.
  • Cook covered; steaming is best.
  • Do not add baking soda (destroys vitamins B and C).
  • Peel thinly; do not over-fry.

10. Diets for different conditions

  • Infants: only mother’s milk till 6 months; then weaning foods (dal water, mashed khichdi, fruit).
  • Children/adolescents: more protein, calcium, iron.
  • Pregnant: extra protein, iron, calcium, folic acid.
  • Lactating mother: extra energy, protein, fluids.
  • Elderly: light, low-fat, easily digestible, fibre-rich.

Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ)

1. A balanced diet contains —
(a) only protein (b) all nutrients in right amounts (c) only fat (d) only fruit

Answer: (b)

2. Energy from 1 g carbohydrate —
(a) 4 kcal (b) 9 kcal (c) 2 kcal (d) 7 kcal

Answer: (a) 4 kcal

3. Energy from 1 g fat —
(a) 4 (b) 9 (c) 6 (d) 2 kcal

Answer: (b) 9 kcal

4. BMR means —
(a) minimum energy at rest (b) energy in sport (c) food quantity (d) weight

Answer: (a)

5. Who needs most energy?
(a) infant (b) heavy-work man (c) elderly (d) desk worker

Answer: (b) man doing heavy work

6. An infant should get till 6 months —
(a) cow’s milk (b) only mother’s milk (c) dal (d) fruit

Answer: (b) only mother’s milk

7. Why eat cereal + pulse together?
(a) taste (b) protein supplementation (c) colour (d) cheap

Answer: (b) protein supplementation

8. Sprouting increases —
(a) vitamins (b) fat (c) starch (d) lowers fibre

Answer: (a) vitamins

9. Vegetables should be washed —
(a) after cutting (b) before cutting (c) soaked long (d) never

Answer: (b) before cutting

10. Pregnant woman needs extra —
(a) only fat (b) protein, iron, calcium (c) only sugar (d) salt

Answer: (b)

11. Calorie is a unit of —
(a) energy (b) weight (c) length (d) time

Answer: (a) energy

12. Energy from 50 g carbohydrate —
(a) 200 kcal (b) 450 (c) 100 (d) 250

Answer: (a) 50 × 4 = 200 kcal

Short Answer Questions

1. What is a balanced diet?

A diet with all nutrients in proper amount and proportion as needed.

2. What is BMR?

Minimum energy needed at rest for vital body functions.

3. Write factors affecting dietary needs.

Age, sex, work, climate, health/condition, body size.

4. Why are sprouted grains useful?

Their vitamin content and digestibility increase.

5. How to save nutrients while cooking vegetables?

Wash before cutting, cook covered, no soda, steam.

6. Examples of weaning foods.

Mashed khichdi, dal water, mashed banana, porridge.

7. Cheap protein sources.

Pulses, groundnut, soyabean, gram.

8. What to increase in a pregnant woman’s diet?

Protein, iron, calcium, folic acid and fluids.

Long Answer Questions

1. Write meaning, importance and factors affecting a balanced diet.

Meaning: all nutrients in proper proportion. Importance: growth, immunity, protection from malnutrition. Factors: age, sex, work, climate, health, pregnancy/lactation, body size.

2. Explain principles of meal planning.

All food groups, variety, quantity as per need, budget and seasonal foods, likes and culture, attractive colour and taste, saving time and fuel.

3. How can nutrient loss during cooking be prevented?

Wash before cutting, do not discard rice water, cook covered in little water, steam, avoid soda, avoid over-frying, peel thinly.

4. Plan a one-day menu for a 14-year-old girl.

Breakfast: porridge-milk-fruit; mid-morning: sprouted gram; lunch: roti, dal, green vegetable, curd, salad; evening: groundnut-jaggery, milk; dinner: roti, palak paneer/dal, vegetable.
