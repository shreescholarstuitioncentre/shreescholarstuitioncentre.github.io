Chapter 15 : Main Principles of First Aid

Class 9 · Home Science · UP Board (Notes)

1. What is first aid?

The immediate help given to an injured or suddenly ill person before a doctor or hospital is available is called first aid. It is not the final treatment, only early help.

Aims: (1) preserve life (2) prevent condition from worsening (3) promote recovery

2. Qualities of a first aider

  • Calm and patient, does not panic.
  • Quick and able to decide.
  • Sympathetic and gentle-spoken.
  • Observant and confident.
  • Resourceful (uses available things).
  • Clean and skilful.

3. Main principles of first aid

  1. Stay calm and check the scene: first ensure safety (fire, electricity, traffic) for yourself and the victim.
  2. Call for help: doctor/ambulance (India emergency number 112; ambulance 108 in many states).
  3. Check consciousness, breathing and pulse; keep airway open.
  4. Stop bleeding: direct pressure with clean cloth.
  5. Give priority to life-threatening problems (stopped breathing, severe bleeding, shock).
  6. Do not move the victim unnecessarily, especially with spine or neck injury; keep comfortable.
  7. Give fresh air, keep crowd away; loosen tight clothes.
  8. Reassure the victim.
  9. Give nothing by mouth to an unconscious person or in serious abdominal injury.
  10. Keep body warm (prevents shock) but not overheated.
  11. Do not remove embedded objects.
  12. Shift to hospital carefully and tell the doctor what was done.

4. First aid box

Keep it at an accessible place in home, school, vehicle; check regularly and keep medicines fresh.

  • Cotton, sterile gauze, roller bandage, triangular bandage, adhesive plaster.
  • Scissors, tweezers, safety pins, gloves.
  • Antiseptic lotion, burn cream.
  • Thermometer, ORS packets, torch.
  • Pain-relief medicine as advised by a doctor.

5. Types of bandages

  • Roller bandage: limbs, fingers.
  • Triangular bandage: sling, head and shoulder.
  • Adhesive plaster: small wounds.

A bandage should be neither too tight nor too loose; finish with a reef knot.

7. Artificial respiration and CPR (brief)

If breathing or heart stops, artificial respiration/CPR can save life. In adults: fast firm chest compressions with both hands in the centre of chest (about 100–120 per minute) with breaths in between (30 compressions : 2 breaths). Do it only after training, and call 112 at the same time.

8. Solved example: finger cut

Given: A boy cut his finger and is bleeding. To find: first aid.
Solution (steps):
1. Stay calm, seat the boy and reassure him.
2. Wash your hands/wear gloves.
3. Press clean gauze/cloth directly on the wound; raise the hand.
4. When bleeding stops, wash with clean running water, apply antiseptic.
5. Apply a clean bandage (not too tight).
6. If deep or bleeding does not stop, go to a doctor; check tetanus vaccination.

9. Solved example: burn

Given: Hot tea spilled on a hand causing a burn. To find: treatment.
Solution: 1. Hold the burn under cool running water 10–20 min. 2. Remove rings/watch (before swelling). 3. Cover loosely with clean dry cloth. 4. Do not burst blisters or apply home remedies. 5. Large/deep burn: go to hospital at once.

Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ)

1. First aid is given —
(a) after doctor (b) before doctor (c) never (d) only in hospital

Answer: (b)

2. Which is NOT an aim of first aid?
(a) preserve life (b) prevent worsening (c) give complete cure (d) promote recovery

Answer: (c)

3. What should not be given to an unconscious person?
(a) air (b) water by mouth (c) rest (d) sympathy

Answer: (b) water by mouth

4. First step in a burn —
(a) butter (b) cool running water (c) burst blisters (d) rub ice

Answer: (b)

5. In nosebleed the head should be —
(a) back (b) forward (c) up (d) none

Answer: (b) leaning forward

6. R in RICE —
(a) Rest (b) Run (c) Rub (d) Roll

Answer: (a) Rest

7. In electric shock first —
(a) touch victim (b) switch off power (c) pour water (d) run

Answer: (b)

8. India’s emergency number —
(a) 112 (b) only 100 (c) 555 (d) 911

Answer: (a) 112

9. In snake bite do NOT —
(a) keep calm (b) keep limb still (c) cut the wound (d) go to hospital

Answer: (c) cut the wound

10. Not found in a first aid box —
(a) cotton (b) bandage (c) scissors (d) surgical instruments

Answer: (d)

11. Triangular bandage is used as —
(a) sling (b) food (c) water (d) utensil

Answer: (a) sling

12. To stop bleeding —
(a) direct pressure (b) massage (c) give water (d) shake

Answer: (a) direct pressure with clean cloth

Short Answer Questions

1. What is first aid?

Immediate help given to a victim before a doctor arrives.

2. Write the aims of first aid.

Preserve life, prevent worsening, promote recovery.

3. Write four qualities of a first aider.

Calm, quick, sympathetic, confident.

4. List contents of a first aid box.

Cotton, gauze, bandages, scissors, tweezers, antiseptic, thermometer, ORS.

5. What not to do in a burn?

Do not burst blisters or apply butter/paste.

6. Expand RICE.

Rest, Ice, Compression, Elevation – for sprains.

7. First step in electric shock?

Switch off the power, then move victim with a non-conductor.

8. Treatment of dog bite?

Wash with soap and water for 15 minutes and see a doctor at once for rabies vaccine.

Long Answer Questions

1. Write the main principles of first aid.

Stay calm and check safety; call help; check consciousness, breathing, pulse; stop bleeding; prioritise serious conditions; avoid unnecessary movement; fresh air and no crowd; reassure; nothing by mouth to unconscious; keep warm; shift to hospital. (Figure 2)

2. Describe the qualities of a first aider.

Calm, patient, quick, decisive, sympathetic, observant, resourceful, confident and clean.

3. Describe the contents and care of a first aid box.

Cotton, gauze, bandages, plaster, scissors, tweezers, pins, gloves, antiseptic, burn cream, thermometer, ORS, torch. Keep accessible, check regularly, renew medicines. (Figure 1)

4. Write first aid for burns, cuts and sprains.

Burn: cool running water 10–20 min, cover. Cut: direct pressure, wash, bandage. Sprain: RICE.
