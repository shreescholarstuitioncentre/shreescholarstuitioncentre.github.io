Chapter 5: Meaning and Definition of Health; Care and Protection of Personal Health

1. Meaning of Health

Health does not mean only the absence of disease. It means being well and happy in body, mind and society.

WHO definition (1948): Health is a state of complete physical, mental and social well-being, and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity.

2. Dimensions of Health (diagram)

  • Physical: all organs work well; one feels active and fit.
  • Mental: control over stress, fear and anger; positive thinking.
  • Social: good relations with others; spirit of cooperation.

3. Signs of Good Health

  • Glowing skin and hair, clear eyes
  • Good appetite and sound sleep
  • Right weight and height for age
  • Cheerful, enthusiastic nature
  • Falls ill rarely

4. Factors Affecting Health

Heredity, environment (clean air and water), balanced food, exercise, sleep and rest, habits, economic condition, health services.

5. Care of Personal Health

Keeping the body clean and following good habits to stay healthy is personal hygiene.

  • Skin: bathe daily, use a clean towel, change sweaty clothes.
  • Hair: wash 2–3 times a week, keep combs clean, never share combs (prevents lice and dandruff).
  • Teeth: brush twice a day, rinse after meals, eat less sweets.
  • Eyes: wash with cool clean water, read in good light, watch TV or phone from a distance and for less time, avoid touching with dirty hands.
  • Ears: keep the outer part clean, never insert sharp objects.
  • Nails: keep short and clean (prevents worm infection).
  • Hands: wash with soap before eating and after using the toilet.
  • Clothes and shoes: clean, sun-dried, suited to the weather.

6. Ways to Protect Health

Balanced diet on time; exercise or yoga at least 30 minutes daily; sleep about 8–10 hours for children and 7–8 hours for adults; correct posture; staying away from bad habits (tobacco, alcohol); vaccination and regular check-ups; keeping the mind cheerful.

Example: Reena walks every morning, eats clean food and sleeps 8 hours. All three of her dimensions of health stay good, so she falls ill rarely.

MCQs

2. How many dimensions of health are stated? Ans: three – physical, mental, social.

3. How often should we brush teeth? Ans: twice a day.

4. Why keep nails short? Ans: to avoid worms and infection.

5. What to do before eating? Ans: wash hands with soap.
