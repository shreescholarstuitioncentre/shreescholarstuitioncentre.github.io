Chapter 16 : Common Household Accidents and Their Prevention

Class 9 · Home Science · UP Board (Notes)

1. What is an accident?

A sudden, unwanted event causing injury, loss or death is an accident. Most household accidents can be prevented with a little care.

Prevention is better than cure.

2. Causes of accidents

  • Carelessness and haste.
  • Lack of knowledge.
  • Slippery floors, poor light, scattered things.
  • Faulty wiring/appliances, damaged gas pipes.
  • Children’s curiosity and lack of supervision.
  • Overcrowding and poor house design.

3. Common household accidents

Falls, burns and scalds, cuts, electric shock, poisoning, fire, gas leak, drowning, choking, bites of snakes, dogs and insects.

4. Falls

Causes: wet or oily floor, soap in bathroom, poor light, loose carpets, stairs without railing, climbing on unstable stools, toys on floor. Children and elderly are most at risk.

Prevention: keep floors dry, anti-skid mats in bathroom, railings and light on stairs, fix carpets, use sturdy ladders, keep paths clear.

5. Burns and scalds

Burn: by dry heat (fire, hot vessel); scald: by hot liquid or steam.

Prevention: keep handles turned inward; keep children away from kitchen; hold hot vessels with cloth/tongs; wear cotton clothes with fitted sleeves in kitchen (not loose or synthetic); open a pressure cooker only after the whistle stops; handle stove and kerosene carefully.

6. Cuts and injuries

From knives, blades, broken glass. Prevention: store sharp objects safely; knife edge downward; wrap broken glass in paper before discarding; use the right tool.

7. Electric shock

Causes: touching switches with wet hands, frayed wires, faulty sockets, overloaded socket, children putting fingers in sockets.

Prevention:

  • ISI-marked appliances and switches, proper earthing, MCB/ELCB.
  • Socket covers (for children).
  • Do not touch electrical items with wet hands; switch off before plugging in or out.
  • Repairs only by a trained electrician.
  • Do not overload a socket.

8. Poisoning

Causes: medicines, kerosene, insecticides, phenyl, cleaning chemicals; stale or contaminated food; coal angithi in a closed room (CO).

Prevention: keep all chemicals and medicines separate and out of children’s reach; label them; never store in cold-drink bottles; check expiry dates; cover fresh food; ventilate rooms.

In poisoning, rush to hospital with the container; do not induce vomiting unless a doctor says so.

9. Fire and gas leak

Causes: short circuit, LPG leak, open match/candle/diya, bidi-cigarette.

Fire triangle

Fire needs three things: fuel, heat, oxygen. Removing any one puts out the fire.

  • Ordinary fire: water (reduces heat).
  • Oil/electrical fire: no water; use sand, CO₂/dry chemical extinguisher, thick wet blanket.

If clothes catch fire — Stop, Drop, Roll

Do not run; lie on the ground and roll, or wrap in a blanket.

LPG care

Turn off regulator at night, replace rubber tube on time, keep cylinder upright, never strike a match when burner is off.

10. Drowning, choking and bites

  • Drowning: cover buckets/tanks; never leave a child alone near water.
  • Choking: keep small objects, coins, polythene away from children; chew food well.
  • Bites: keep house clean, cover food, mosquito nets, torch at night; care in snake areas.

11. Solved example: gas leak

Given: Smell of LPG in the kitchen. To find: What to do?
Solution (steps):
1. Do not panic.
2. Do not light a match/lighter; do not switch electrical items or fan on/off.
3. Turn off the regulator.
4. Open doors and windows.
5. Take everybody outside.
6. Inform gas agency/emergency service; use again only after checking.

12. Solved example: clothes on fire

Given: A dupatta catches fire in the kitchen. Solution: 1. Do not run. 2. Stop. 3. Drop to the ground. 4. Roll (or wrap in thick blanket). 5. When fire is out, hold burnt area under cool water 10–20 min and go to hospital.

13. Solved example: frayed wire

Given: The rubber of an iron’s cord has peeled off. Hazard: electric shock/short circuit. Solution: remove plug (dry hands), stop use, get cord replaced, keep children away.

14. Child safety

  • Keep sockets, medicines, chemicals, matches out of reach.
  • Gates on stairs; grills on windows.
  • Keep small things (coins, buttons) away.
  • Never leave a child alone near water, kitchen or roof.

Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ)

1. Main cause of household accidents —
(a) carelessness (b) education (c) cleanliness (d) exercise

Answer: (a) carelessness

2. A scald is caused by —
(a) hot liquid/steam (b) fire (c) electricity (d) chemical

Answer: (a)

3. Not part of the fire triangle —
(a) fuel (b) heat (c) oxygen (d) water

Answer: (d) water

4. Do not use which on oil fire?
(a) sand (b) water (c) CO₂ extinguisher (d) blanket

Answer: (b) water

5. If clothes catch fire —
(a) run (b) stop, drop, roll (c) fan (d) drink water

Answer: (b)

6. In LPG leak do NOT —
(a) turn off regulator (b) open window (c) strike a match (d) go outside

Answer: (c) strike a match

7. Sockets reachable by children should be —
(a) open (b) covered (c) low (d) broken

Answer: (b) covered

8. Medicines should be kept —
(a) out of children’s reach (b) in kitchen low (c) on open table (d) in bottle

Answer: (a)

9. To prevent slipping —
(a) anti-skid mat (b) wet floor (c) loose carpet (d) dim light

Answer: (a)

10. Chemicals should NOT be stored in —
(a) labelled bottle (b) cold-drink bottle (c) closed container (d) high cupboard

Answer: (b)

11. While handling electrical items hands should be —
(a) wet (b) dry (c) soapy (d) any

12. Knives should be kept —

Short Answer Questions

1. What is an accident?

A sudden unwanted event causing injury or loss.

2. Write causes of household accidents.

Carelessness, ignorance, slippery floor, poor light, faulty wires, children’s curiosity.

3. Difference between burn and scald.

Burn is by dry heat; scald by hot liquid or steam.

4. What is the fire triangle?

Fuel, heat and oxygen — all three are needed for fire.

5. Two ways to prevent electric shock.

Touch switches with dry hands; proper earthing and MCB.

6. How to prevent poisoning?

Keep medicines/chemicals labelled, away from children, never in food bottles.

7. How to prevent falls?

Dry floors, mats, light, railings, clear paths.

8. What to do in an LPG leak?

Turn off regulator, open windows, avoid electrical switches and flame, go outside.

Long Answer Questions

1. Write main causes of household accidents and how to prevent them.

Causes: carelessness, ignorance, slippery floors, poor light, faulty wires/gas pipes, lack of supervision of children. Prevention: care, mats, railings, light, ISI appliances, socket covers, medicines out of reach, checking LPG. (Figure 1)

2. Write causes and prevention of electric shock.

Causes: wet hands, frayed wires, faulty sockets, overload. Prevention: ISI appliances, earthing, MCB/ELCB, dry hands, repairs by electrician, socket covers.

3. Explain causes of fire, the fire triangle and methods of extinguishing.

Causes: short circuit, LPG leak, open flame. Triangle: fuel, heat, oxygen. Ordinary fire: water; oil/electrical fire: sand, CO₂ extinguisher, blanket. (Figure 2)

4. Write precautions to avoid accidents in the kitchen.

Handles inward, children away, hold hot vessels with tongs, fitted cotton clothes, LPG off at night, check tube, safe storage of sharp objects, dry floor.
