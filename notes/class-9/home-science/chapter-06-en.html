Chapter 6: Local Health Institutions and Services

1. Introduction

Government, semi-government and voluntary bodies that protect people of an area from disease, give treatment and spread health knowledge are called local health institutions.

Why needed? Timely treatment, disease prevention, safety of mother and child, population control, clean surroundings and health education.

2. Ladder of Rural Health Institutions (diagram)

A serious patient is referred from a lower institution to a higher one.

3. Main Institutions and Services

  • Sub-centre: first contact in the village; immunisation, check-up of pregnant women, family-planning advice, common medicines.
  • PHC: outpatient treatment, delivery, immunisation, laboratory tests, disease control.
  • CHC: minor operations, specialist services, indoor beds, referral centre.
  • District hospital: all major facilities, emergency care, surgery.
  • Anganwadi (ICDS): supplementary nutrition, immunisation and pre-school education for children up to 6 years, pregnant and nursing mothers.
  • ASHA worker: a village woman health worker who helps people reach the hospital.
  • Urban bodies: dispensaries, municipal hospitals, maternity and child welfare centres.
  • Voluntary bodies: Red Cross, Rotary, Lions Club – blood donation, eye camps, relief.

4. Types of Health Services

Preventive: vaccines, sanitation, nutrition.
Curative: treating patients, medicines, operations.
Promotive: health education, posters, camps.
Rehabilitative: helping the disabled or recovering patients become capable again.

5. Role of Local Self-Government

Gram Panchayat and Nagar Palika protect health through water supply, drains, garbage disposal, mosquito control, birth and death registration, and cleanliness drives.

Example: ASHA didi took pregnant Seema to the sub-centre. The ANM examined her. Because of a complication she was sent to the PHC and then the CHC – this is the referral system.

6. Questions & Answers

MCQs

1. First unit of health service in a village? Ans: sub-centre.

2. Who works at the sub-centre? Ans: ANM.

3. One PHC covers about how many people in plains? Ans: about 30,000.

4. Anganwadi belongs to which scheme? Ans: ICDS.

5. The Red Cross is what kind of body? Ans: voluntary.

Short answers

6. What are local health institutions? A. Bodies that give health services to people of an area.

7. Two functions of a sub-centre. A. Immunisation; check-up of pregnant women.

8. One function of a CHC. A. Treatment by specialist doctors and referral service.

9. Who is an ASHA worker? A. A village woman who assists in health matters.

10. Two services of Anganwadi. A. Supplementary nutrition; pre-school education.
