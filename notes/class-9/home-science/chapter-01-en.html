📒 Home Science – Class 9
Chapter 1: Elements and Scope of Home Science

1. Meaning of Home Science

Home Science teaches how to use the available resources wisely for a healthy, happy and well-managed home and family. It blends science, art and social science.

Definition: Home Science is the scientific study of food, clothing, shelter, child development and management of resources to improve family life.

Founder: Ellen Swallow Richards (USA) is called the mother of Home Science. In India, Lady Irwin College was set up in Delhi in 1932.

2. Objectives

  • Keep the family healthy and happy
  • Use time, money and energy wisely
  • Give knowledge of balanced diet
  • Help all-round development of children
  • Make self-reliant, aware citizens

3. Elements / Branches (diagram)

  • Food & Nutrition: balanced diet, nutrients, cooking, food preservation, diet in illness.
  • Resource Management: managing time, money, energy; home decoration; budgeting.
  • Human Development: physical, mental and social growth from infancy to old age; family relations.
  • Textiles & Clothing: fibres, weaving, washing, dyeing, stitching, choosing clothes.
  • Extension & Communication: carrying knowledge to villages and society through demonstrations, posters, campaigns.

4. Scope of Home Science

(a) Personal & family: home management, care of children and elders, hygiene.
(b) Educational: teaching and research in schools and colleges.
(c) Professional: dietitian, fashion designer, interior designer, preschool manager, teacher, extension worker, hotel and catering manager, entrepreneur.
(d) Social: helps fight poverty, malnutrition and illiteracy.

5. Importance

  • Better family health
  • Saves money and time
  • Makes women self-reliant
  • Creates jobs
  • Helps national development

6. Questions & Answers

MCQs

1. Who is called the mother of Home Science? Ans: (b) Ellen Swallow Richards – her Lake Placid conferences (1899) gave the subject its identity.

2. How many branches does Home Science have? Ans: Five.

3. Budget making belongs to which branch? Ans: Resource Management, since money is a resource.

4. Study of fibres belongs to? Ans: Textiles & Clothing.

5. Lady Irwin College was founded in? Ans: 1932.

Very short & short answers

6. What is Home Science? A. The scientific study of managing home and family life well.

7. Name some resources. A. Time, money, energy, skills, household equipment.

8. Give one function of extension education. A. Teaching village women about nutrition and hygiene.

9. Write two objectives. A. Keep the family healthy; teach wise use of resources.

10. What is studied in Human Development? A. Growth of a person at every stage of life.

Long answers
