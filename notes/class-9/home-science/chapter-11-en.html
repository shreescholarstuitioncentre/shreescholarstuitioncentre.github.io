Chapter 11 : Personal Grooming – Proper Dress

Class 9 · Home Science · UP Board (Notes)

1. What is personal grooming?

Keeping the body, skin, hair, teeth, nails and clothes clean, tidy and pleasant is called personal grooming. It covers cleanliness, health and tasteful dressing.

Neat look = health + confidence + good impression

2. Importance of grooming

  • Builds self-confidence.
  • Creates a good impression and earns respect.
  • Cleanliness keeps diseases away.
  • Improves personality; helps in jobs, interviews and relationships.

3. Parts of personal grooming

(a) Skin care

Daily bath, clean face, moisturiser, sunscreen outdoors, balanced diet and enough water.

(b) Hair care

Wash 2–3 times a week, oil massage, clean comb (never share), regular trimming, treat dandruff and lice.

(c) Teeth and mouth

Brush twice a day (morning and night), rinse after meals, less sweets, dental check-ups.

(d) Nails, hands and feet

Trim nails weekly, keep them clean, do not bite; wash hands before eating and after toilet; wear clean socks.

(e) Body odour

Bathe daily, wear clean clothes, use deodorant if needed.

(f) Posture and habits

Sit and walk straight, sleep well, exercise, speak politely, good manners.

4. What is proper dress?

Clothing suited to the occasion, season, age, body, budget and personality, which is comfortable, clean and decent.

5. Factors in selecting clothes

  • Age, climate, occasion, profession, body figure, complexion, budget, fashion, comfort, fabric, ease of care.

6. Clothes by age

Age groupSuitable clothes
InfantsSoft cotton, loose, front-opening, no pins or hard buttons
ChildrenDurable, washable, comfortable for play, bright colours
AdolescentsDecent, fashionable yet comfortable
AdultsAccording to profession and occasion
ElderlyLight, loose, easy to wear, soft colours

7. By season and occasion

  • Summer: light colours, cotton, loose.
  • Winter: dark colours, woollen, in layers.
  • Rainy: quick-drying synthetics, raincoat.
  • Occasion: uniform for school, rich silk for weddings, plain white/black for mourning, track suit for sports.

8. By body figure (effect of lines)

FigureSuitableAvoid
Tall/thinHorizontal lines, large prints, light bright coloursVertical lines
Short/stoutVertical lines, small prints, dark coloursHorizontal lines, large prints, shiny cloth
Vertical lines add height; horizontal lines add width. Light colours make one look bigger, dark colours smaller.

9. By complexion

  • Fair: most colours; deep and soft shades look good.
  • Dark/wheatish: light, bright colours; avoid very dark or dull shades.

10. Care of clothes and footwear

  • Wash regularly, iron, mend tears, sew buttons.
  • Right fit: neither too tight nor too loose.
  • Shoes clean, polished and of right size.
  • Accessories simple and matching.

Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ)

1. Main benefit of grooming —
(a) confidence (b) money (c) food (d) sleep

Answer: (a) confidence and good impression

2. How often should teeth be brushed?
(a) once (b) twice (c) four times (d) never

Answer: (b) twice a day

3. Suitable clothes in summer —
(a) dark woollen (b) light cotton (c) dark nylon (d) leather

Answer: (b) light cotton

4. Infant clothes should be —
(a) tight (b) soft and loose (c) with pins (d) heavy

Answer: (b) soft, loose and front-opening

5. Vertical lines make a person look —
(a) wider (b) taller (c) fatter (d) shorter

Answer: (b) taller

6. Suitable for a stout person —
(a) horizontal lines (b) vertical lines, dark colours (c) large prints (d) shiny colours

Answer: (b) vertical lines, dark colours

7. Colours suitable in winter —
(a) dark (b) light (c) white (d) none

Answer: (a) dark, as they absorb heat.

8. Nails should be cut —
(a) monthly (b) weekly (c) yearly (d) never

Answer: (b) weekly

9. Proper dress does NOT depend on —
(a) age (b) season (c) occasion (d) neighbour’s colours

Answer: (d)

10. Suitable in rainy season —
(a) quick-drying (b) heavy woollen (c) velvet (d) silk

Answer: (a) quick-drying synthetics

11. Dress for mourning —
(a) bright red (b) plain white/black (c) yellow (d) any

Answer: (b) plain

12. Which should NOT be done for hair care?
(a) share combs (b) oil (c) wash (d) trim

Answer: (a) never share combs

Short Answer Questions

1. What is personal grooming?

Keeping body, hair, teeth, clothes etc. clean and pleasant.

2. Why is grooming necessary?

It gives health, confidence and a good impression.

3. What is proper dress?

Dress suited to age, season, occasion and body, comfortable and decent.

4. How should clothes be in summer?

Light-coloured, loose and cotton, to absorb sweat.

5. Effect of vertical lines?

The person looks taller and slimmer.

6. Two ways of hair care.

Regular washing and oiling; use a clean comb.

7. Two features of infant clothes.

Soft cotton, loose and easy to open.

8. How to care for shoes?

Keep clean and polished, wear the right size, keep socks clean.

Long Answer Questions

1. Describe the various parts of personal grooming.

Skin (bath, sunscreen), hair (wash, oil), teeth (brush twice), nails and hands-feet (trimmed, clean), body odour (bath, clean clothes), posture and habits (walk straight, sleep, exercise). (Figure 1)

2. What points should be considered while selecting clothes?

Age, season, occasion, profession, figure, complexion, budget, fashion, comfort, fabric and ease of care.

3. Explain selection of clothes according to body figure.

Tall and thin: horizontal lines, large prints, light colours. Short and stout: vertical lines, small prints, dark colours. (Figure 2)

4. Describe proper dress for different ages.

Infants: soft cotton, loose; children: durable, washable; adolescents: decent, fashionable; adults: as per occasion; elderly: light, loose, easy to wear.
