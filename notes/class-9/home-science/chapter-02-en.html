📒 Home Science – Class 9
Chapter 2: Home Management in the Family Context

1. Meaning of Home Management

Using the family's available resources thoughtfully and in the right order to reach family goals is called home management.

Definition: Home management is the process of using family resources to achieve family goals.

Example: A mother divides the monthly budget among groceries, school fees and savings – that is management.

2. Why the family needs it

  • Resources are limited but wants are many, so choices are needed
  • Saves time, money and energy
  • Brings cooperation among members
  • Raises the standard of living

3. Process of Management (diagram)

  • Planning: fix the goal, think of options, choose the best.
  • Controlling: carry out the plan, share work, check and make changes.
  • Evaluation: see how far the goal was reached and where it fell short.

4. Family Resources

(a) Human: time, energy, skills, knowledge, interests, attitudes.
(b) Non-human (material): money, house, goods, equipment, community facilities (school, hospital, park).

Characteristics of resources: utility, limited supply, can substitute for each other (money can hire help), accessibility.

5. Goals, Values and Decisions

Values are beliefs that guide our choices (e.g. education, health). Goals grow out of values. Steps in decision making: identify the problem → find alternatives → compare them → choose → evaluate the result.

6. Qualities of a Home Manager

  • Foresight and patience
  • Ability to decide
  • Honesty and cooperation
  • Good health and discipline

7. Questions & Answers

MCQs

1. First step of management? Ans: Planning – control is impossible without a plan.

2. Money is which type of resource? Ans: Non-human.

3. Time belongs to? Ans: Human resources.

4. Last step of management? Ans: Evaluation.

5. Parks and hospitals are? Ans: Community facilities (non-human).

Short answers

6. What is home management? A. Planned use of resources to reach family goals.

7. Types of resources? A. Two – human and non-human.

8. Difference between value and goal? A. A value is a belief; a goal is an achievable aim built from it.

9. What happens in controlling? A. Work is shared, checked and adjusted.

10. Why is evaluation needed? A. To find weaknesses and improve next time.
