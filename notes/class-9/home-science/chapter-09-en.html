Chapter 9 : Diseases Caused by Impure Air

Class 9 · Home Science · UP Board (Notes)

1. Introduction

Impure air contains dust, smoke, poisonous gases and germs (bacteria, viruses). On breathing, they reach the respiratory system (nose, windpipe, lungs) and cause illness. Such diseases are called air-borne diseases.

2. How do diseases spread? (Droplet infection)

When a patient coughs, sneezes or talks, tiny droplets with germs spread in air. A healthy person breathes them in. This is droplet infection.

Crowds, closed rooms and poor ventilation make it spread faster.

4. Dust-related occupational diseases

  • Silicosis: silica dust from stone or mines.
  • Anthracosis: coal dust (coal mines).
  • Asbestosis: asbestos fibres.

They harden the lungs. Wearing masks and good ventilation are essential.

5. Effect of poisonous gases

  • Carbon monoxide (CO): binds haemoglobin and stops oxygen transport; causes dizziness, unconsciousness, even death. Never burn coal or an angithi in a closed room.
  • SO₂ and smoke: irritate eyes, throat and lungs; worsen asthma.

6. Prevention

  • Cover mouth with handkerchief or elbow while coughing or sneezing.
  • Do not spit in public.
  • Keep good ventilation; avoid crowds.
  • Get vaccinated on time.
  • Isolate patient; keep utensils and clothes separate.
  • Nutritious food, exercise and sleep raise immunity.
  • Plant trees, use clean fuel (LPG), do not burn waste.
  • Wear a mask in dusty work.

Short Answer Questions

1. What are air-borne diseases?

Diseases whose germs spread through air and enter by breathing, e.g. TB, flu, measles.

2. What is droplet infection?

Germ-laden droplets from a patient’s cough or sneeze are inhaled by a healthy person, spreading disease.

3. Write four symptoms of TB.

Cough over two weeks, blood in sputum, weight loss, evening fever.

4. What is pneumonia?

Infection and inflammation of the lungs causing fever, chest pain and breathlessness.

5. Give two causes of asthma.

Allergy to dust and smoke, pollen, cold air, pollution.

6. How can diphtheria be prevented?

By taking the DPT vaccine.

7. Differentiate silicosis and anthracosis.

Silicosis is due to silica dust (stone mines); anthracosis is due to coal dust.

8. What care should be taken while coughing?

Cover mouth with handkerchief or elbow, do not spit, wash hands.

Long Answer Questions

1. Write cause, symptoms and prevention of any four diseases caused by impure air.

TB: bacterium; long cough, bloody sputum, weight loss; BCG, DOTS. Pneumonia: bacteria/virus; fever, chest pain; early treatment. Diphtheria: bacteria; throat membrane; DPT. Measles: virus; fever, red rash; vaccine and isolation.

2. Explain droplet infection and ways to prevent it.

Tiny droplets from a patient’s cough or sneeze float in air and a healthy person breathes them in (Figure 2). Prevention: cover mouth, no spitting, ventilation, avoid crowds, isolate patient, vaccination, masks.

3. Write a detailed note on tuberculosis.

Cause: Mycobacterium tuberculosis. Spread: droplet infection. Symptoms: cough, bloody sputum, weight loss, weakness, evening fever. Treatment: full DOTS course. Prevention: BCG vaccine, ventilation, nutritious food, no spitting.

4. Suggest measures to prevent diseases caused by dust and smoke.

Wear masks, water spraying and ventilation in mines, clean fuel, tree plantation, not burning waste, chimneys and filters in factories, regular health check-ups.
