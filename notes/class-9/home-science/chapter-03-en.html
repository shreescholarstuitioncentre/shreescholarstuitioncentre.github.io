📒 Home Science – Class 9
Chapter 3: Work Management

1. Meaning

Doing household tasks in a planned order, shared sensibly and in the right way, so that they take less time, less energy and cause less fatigue.

Aims: save time and energy, reduce fatigue, improve quality of work, leave time for rest and recreation.

2. Steps in Work Management

  • Planning: list all the day's tasks.
  • Sequencing: heavy and urgent jobs first, light ones later.
  • Sharing: give work by age and ability of members.
  • Doing and checking: carry out and see if it was done on time.

3. Time Plan

Arranging tasks according to time. Types: daily (routine jobs), weekly (washing clothes, cleaning), monthly (ration, bills), yearly/seasonal (airing quilts, making pickles).
Qualities of a good time plan: flexible, simple, made with everyone's agreement, includes rest.

Peak load: the time of day with the most work (morning breakfast and school rush). Reduce it by preparing the night before.

4. Work Curve and Fatigue (diagram)

Fatigue: fall in the body's or mind's working capacity after continuous work.
Types: physical (aching muscles) and mental (loss of concentration).
Causes: long hours at one task, wrong posture, poor nutrition, lack of sleep, unsuitable tools.
Remedies: rest breaks, correct posture, changing tasks, balanced diet, enough sleep.

5. Work Simplification

Using methods that remove unnecessary motions and make work easier.
Mundel's classes of change: (1) the way of doing the work, (2) layout of the work area, (3) tools and equipment, (4) the final product, (5) raw material.

Solved example: In a kitchen you walk to the cupboard 40 times a day. Fix: keep the knife, board and bowls near the cutting spot → less walking → saved time and less tiredness.

Ways to simplify: work surface at waist level, things stored near where they are used, use both hands, sit while working, suitable tools, correct work order.

6. Correct Posture

While standing: spine straight, feet slightly apart, weight on both feet. Avoid bending. Keep the work surface about 5–8 cm below the elbow.

7. Questions & Answers

MCQs

1. Main aim of work management? Ans: saving time and energy.

2. Time with the most work is called? Ans: peak load.

3. How many types of fatigue? Ans: two – physical and mental.

4. Washing clothes fits which plan? Ans: weekly.

5. Work simplification reduces? Ans: unnecessary motions and fatigue.

Short answers

6. What is work management? A. Doing household tasks in a planned way with less time and energy.

7. Two ways to remove fatigue. A. Rest in between; balanced diet and enough sleep.

8. One way to reduce peak load. A. Prepare the night before (cut vegetables, set out clothes).

9. Two qualities of a good time plan. A. Flexible and made with everyone's agreement.

10. What is work simplification? A. Methods that cut motion, time and tiredness.

Long answers

11. Write the causes and remedies of fatigue. A. Use point 4 with the work-curve diagram.

12. Explain work simplification. A. Use points 5 and 6: meaning, Mundel's classes, ways, posture and the example.
