Chapter 7: Effect of Environmental Pollution on Life

1. Environment and Pollution

The environment is the air, water, soil, plants and animals around us. When harmful substances mix into it and make it unsafe for life, that is pollution.

Pollutants: substances that cause pollution – smoke, gases, garbage, chemicals, dirty water, noise.

2. Types of Pollution (diagram)

3. Air Pollution

Causes: smoke of vehicles and factories (carbon monoxide, sulphur dioxide), burning wood and coal, stubble burning, dust, CFCs.
Effects: asthma, bronchitis, lung cancer, eye irritation, headache; acid rain; global warming (from CO₂); ozone layer depletion (from CFCs).
Control: plant trees, use public transport or CNG, fit filters, do not burn waste.

4. Water Pollution

Causes: household sewage, industrial waste, farm pesticides and fertilisers, garbage and idol immersion in rivers, washing clothes in ponds, dumping dead animals.
Effects: cholera, typhoid, dysentery, jaundice, diarrhoea; death of aquatic life; shortage of drinking water.
Control: treat sewage, boil or filter drinking water, effluent plants for industries, no dumping in rivers.

5. Soil Pollution

Causes: plastic and garbage, excess chemical fertilisers and pesticides, industrial waste.
Effects: loss of fertility, toxic substances in crops, diseases in humans.
Control: organic manure, less plastic, recycling of waste.

6. Noise Pollution

Causes: horns, loudspeakers, DJs, machines, firecrackers.
Effects: deafness (sound above about 85 decibels for long periods is harmful), irritability, sleeplessness, high blood pressure, disturbed studies.
Control: blow horns and play loudspeakers less, plant trees, use ear protection.

7. Overall Effect on Life and Government Steps

Poor health, lower working capacity, higher medical expenses, lower crop yield, climate change. Laws: Water Act 1974, Air Act 1981, Environment Protection Act 1986. Remember: the Bhopal gas tragedy (1984) was a major accident caused by an industrial gas leak.

Example: A village pond was dirtied by washing clothes and garbage → people drank that water → diarrhoea and cholera spread. Solution: boil water before drinking, clean the pond, stop dumping waste.

3. What destroys the ozone layer? Ans: CFCs.

4. Year of the Air Act? Ans: 1981.

5. Unit of sound? Ans: decibel.
