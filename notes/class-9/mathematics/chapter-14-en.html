Class 9 Maths – Chapter 14: Surface Areas and Volumes

UP Board · Handwritten-style notes (π = 22/7)

📘 Notes

1. Cuboid and Cube

Surface area = total area of all faces (unit: cm², m²). Volume = space occupied (unit: cm³, m³). Lateral (curved) surface area excludes top and bottom.

Cuboid (l, b, h):

  • Total surface area = 2(lb + bh + hl)
  • Lateral surface area = 2h(l + b)
  • Volume = l × b × h; diagonal = √(l² + b² + h²)

Cube (edge a):

  • TSA = 6a², LSA = 4a², Volume = a³, diagonal = a√3

2. Units and key ideas

  • Use π = 22/7 unless told otherwise.
  • Convert everything to the same unit before calculating.
  • 1 m = 100 cm; 1 m³ = 10,00,000 cm³; 1 litre = 1000 cm³; 1 m³ = 1000 litres.
  • When a solid is melted and recast, volume stays the same.
  • Number of small solids = Volume of big ÷ Volume of one small.

3. Right circular cylinder

Radius r, height h.

  • Curved surface area = 2πrh (a rectangle 2πr × h when opened)
  • Total surface area = 2πr(r + h) (CSA + two circles 2πr²)
  • Volume = πr²h

A hollow pipe needs both inner and outer radius: volume of material = πh(R² − r²).

4. Cone, sphere and hemisphere

Cone (r, h, slant height l = √(r² + h²)):

  • CSA = πrl; TSA = πr(l + r); Volume = ⅓πr²h

Sphere: SA = 4πr²; Volume = 4/3 πr³.

Hemisphere: CSA = 2πr²; TSA = 3πr² (curved + base); Volume = 2/3 πr³.

Volume of cone = ⅓ of cylinder with same base and height.

✏️ Solved Examples

EasyQ1. Find the total surface area and volume of a cuboid 12 cm × 8 cm × 5 cm.

Given: Find the total surface area and volume of a cuboid 12 cm × 8 cm × 5 cm.

To prove / find: TSA and volume

Solution:
l = 12, b = 8, h = 5.
TSA = 2(lb + bh + hl) = 2(96 + 40 + 60) = 2 × 196 = 392 cm².
Volume = 12 × 8 × 5 = 480 cm³.

EasyQ2. Find the surface area and volume of a cube of edge 6 cm.

Given: Find the surface area and volume of a cube of edge 6 cm.

To prove / find: TSA and volume

Solution:
TSA = 6a² = 6 × 36 = 216 cm².
Volume = a³ = 6 × 6 × 6 = 216 cm³.

IntermediateQ3. A cylinder has radius 7 cm and height 15 cm. Find CSA, TSA and volume.

Given: A cylinder has radius 7 cm and height 15 cm. Find CSA, TSA and volume.

To prove / find: CSA, TSA, V

Solution:
CSA = 2πrh = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 15 = 660 cm².
TSA = 2πr(r + h) = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 22 = 44 × 22 = 968 cm².
Volume = πr²h = 22/7 × 49 × 15 = 2310 cm³.

IntermediateQ4. A cone has base radius 7 cm and height 24 cm. Find slant height, CSA and volume.

Given: A cone has base radius 7 cm and height 24 cm. Find slant height, CSA and volume.

To prove / find: l, CSA, V

Solution:
l = √(r² + h²) = √(49 + 576) = √625 = 25 cm.
CSA = πrl = 22/7 × 7 × 25 = 550 cm².
Volume = ⅓πr²h = ⅓ × 22/7 × 49 × 24 = 1232 cm³.

HardQ5. A hemispherical bowl has inner radius 10.5 cm. Find its capacity in litres.

Given: A hemispherical bowl has inner radius 10.5 cm. Find its capacity in litres.

To prove / find: Capacity

Solution:
V = 2/3 πr³ = 2/3 × 22/7 × 10.5 × 10.5 × 10.5.
10.5³ = 1157.625.
V = (2 × 22 × 1157.625) ÷ (3 × 7) = 50935.5 ÷ 21 = 2425.5 cm³.
Since 1000 cm³ = 1 litre: capacity = 2.4255 litres.

HardQ6. A metallic sphere of radius 6 cm is melted and recast into a solid cylinder of radius 4 cm. Find the height of the cylinder.

Given: A metallic sphere of radius 6 cm is melted and recast into a solid cylinder of radius 4 cm. Find the height of the cylinder.

To prove / find: Height h

Solution:
Volume stays the same.
Volume of sphere = 4/3 π × 6³ = 4/3 π × 216 = 288π.
Volume of cylinder = π × 4² × h = 16πh.
16πh = 288π ⇒ h = 288 ÷ 16 = 18 cm.

❓ Short & Long Q&A

Q1. Difference between lateral and total surface area?

Answer: Lateral/curved area excludes top and bottom faces; total area includes all faces.

Q2. Write the formula for the diagonal of a cube of edge a.

Answer: √3 × a (from √(a² + a² + a²)).

Q3. How many cubes of edge 2 cm fit in a cube of edge 8 cm?

Answer: (8 ÷ 2)³ = 4³ = 64 cubes (512 ÷ 8).

Q4. How is a cone's volume related to a cylinder's?

Answer: With the same base radius and height, cone volume = ⅓ cylinder volume.

Q5. Derive the TSA of a hemisphere.

Answer: Curved surface = 2πr² (half of 4πr²). Add flat circular base πr². TSA = 3πr².

Q6. How many litres in 1 m³?

Answer: 1 m³ = 100 × 100 × 100 = 10,00,000 cm³ = 1000 litres.

Q7. What happens to the volume of a cube if its edge is doubled?

Answer: New volume = (2a)³ = 8a³, so it becomes 8 times.

Q8. Radii of two spheres are in ratio 1 : 2. Find ratio of surface areas and volumes.

Answer: SA ∝ r² ⇒ 1 : 4. Volume ∝ r³ ⇒ 1 : 8.

Q9. Find the slant height of a cone with r = 3 cm and h = 4 cm.

Answer: l = √(9 + 16) = √25 = 5 cm.

✅ MCQs

Q1. Volume of a cube of edge 5 cm:
  1. 25 cm³
  2. 125 cm³
  3. 150 cm³
  4. 100 cm³

Answer: B – 125 cm³. 5³ = 125.

Q2. CSA of a cylinder:
  1. πr²h
  2. 2πrh
  3. 2πr(r + h)
  4. πrl

Answer: B – 2πrh. Curved surface = circumference × height.

Q3. Volume of a cone:
  1. πr²h
  2. ⅓πr²h
  3. ⅔πr³
  4. 4/3πr³

Answer: B – ⅓πr²h. One-third of cylinder.

Q4. Surface area of a sphere of radius r:
  1. 2πr²
  2. 3πr²
  3. 4πr²
  4. 4/3πr³

Answer: C – 4πr². Standard formula.

Q5. TSA of a hemisphere:
  1. 2πr²
  2. 3πr²
  3. 4πr²
  4. πr²

Answer: B – 3πr². 2πr² + πr².

Q6. Slant height of a cone with r = 6, h = 8:
  1. 10
  2. 14
  3. 12
  4. 7

Answer: A – 10. √(36 + 64) = 10.

Q7. 1 litre equals:
  1. 100 cm³
  2. 1000 cm³
  3. 10 cm³
  4. 10000 cm³

Answer: B – 1000 cm³. Standard conversion.

Q8. If radius of a sphere doubles, volume becomes:
  1. 2 times
  2. 4 times
  3. 8 times
  4. 16 times

Answer: C – 8 times. V ∝ r³.
