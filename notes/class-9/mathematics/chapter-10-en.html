📌 Introduction: Two rays starting from a common point (the vertex) form an angle. In this chapter we study types of angles, linear pairs, vertically opposite angles, parallel lines and a transversal, and the angle sum property of a triangle.

1. Basic Terms

• Line segment: part with two end points  • Ray: one end point, infinite on the other side  • Line: infinite both ways.
• Collinear points: points lying on the same line.

2. Types of Angles

AngleMeasure
Acutemore than 0°, less than 90°
Right90°
Obtusemore than 90°, less than 180°
Straight180°
Reflexmore than 180°, less than 360°
Complementary angles: sum 90°; complement of x = 90° − x. Supplementary angles: sum 180°; supplement of x = 180° − x.

3. Linear Pair

Two adjacent angles whose non-common arms form a straight line are a linear pair. Linear Pair Axiom: the angles of a linear pair add up to 180°.

Animated Diagram 1: Linear pair

4. Vertically Opposite Angles

Theorem: If two lines intersect, the vertically opposite angles are equal.

Animated Diagram 2: Vertically opposite angles

5. Parallel Lines and a Transversal

If l ∥ m and a transversal t cuts them:
• corresponding angles are equal  • alternate interior angles are equal  • interior angles on the same side of t add up to 180°.
Converse: if corresponding or alternate angles are equal, or co-interior angles sum to 180°, the lines are parallel. Lines parallel to the same line are parallel to each other.

Animated Diagram 3: l ∥ m with transversal t

6. Angle Sum Property of a Triangle

The sum of the three angles of a triangle = 180°. Exterior angle property: an exterior angle of a triangle = the sum of the two interior opposite angles.

7. Solved Examples

Find: the complement and supplement of 65°.
Step 1: Complement = 90° − 65° = 25°
Step 2: Supplement = 180° − 65° = 115°.
Find: two complementary angles are in the ratio 2 : 3.
Step 1: Total parts 2 + 3 = 5, sum 90°
Step 2: One part = 18°
Angles: 36° and 54°.
Find x: a linear pair has angles (3x + 10)° and (2x − 10)°.
Step 1: 3x + 10 + 2x − 10 = 180 ⇒ 5x = 180
Step 2: x = 36
Angles: 118° and 62°.
Find x: vertically opposite angles ∠AOC = (5x − 20)° and ∠BOD = (3x + 10)°.
Step 1: Vertically opposite angles are equal ⇒ 5x − 20 = 3x + 10
Step 2: 2x = 30 ⇒ x = 15
∠AOC = 55°.
Find: l ∥ m, ∠1 = 110° (as in Diagram 3); find the other angles.
Step 1: ∠2 = 180° − 110° = 70° (linear pair)
Step 2: ∠3 = ∠1 = 110°, ∠4 = ∠2 = 70° (vertically opposite)
Step 3: By corresponding angles ∠5 = 110°, ∠6 = 70°, ∠7 = 110°, ∠8 = 70°.
Find: the angles of a triangle are in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3.
Step 1: Sum 180°, parts 1+2+3 = 6, one part = 30°
Angles: 30°, 60°, 90°.
Find: In ΔABC, ∠A = 40° and exterior angle ∠ACD = 110°. Find ∠B.
Step 1: Exterior angle = ∠A + ∠B
Step 2: 110° = 40° + ∠B
∠B = 70°.
Hard: The interior angles on one side of a transversal cutting parallel lines are in the ratio 3 : 2. Find them.
Step 1: Sum 180°, 5 parts, one part = 36°
Angles: 108° and 72°.
Hard — Prove: Vertically opposite angles are equal.
Step 1: Lines AB and CD meet at O. ∠AOC and ∠COB form a linear pair ⇒ ∠AOC + ∠COB = 180° …(i)
Step 2: ∠COB and ∠BOD form a linear pair ⇒ ∠COB + ∠BOD = 180° …(ii)
Step 3: From (i) and (ii): ∠AOC + ∠COB = ∠COB + ∠BOD
Step 4: Subtract ∠COB from both ⇒ ∠AOC = ∠BOD. ∎
Hard — Prove: The angles of a triangle add up to 180°.
Step 1: In ΔABC draw PQ through A parallel to BC.
Step 2: Alternate angles: ∠PAB = ∠B and ∠QAC = ∠C.
Step 3: ∠PAB + ∠BAC + ∠QAC = 180° (straight angle)
Step 4: ∠B + ∠A + ∠C = 180°. ∎

8. Q & A (click to see the answer)

Short Answer

Q. Complement of 40°?

Ans: 90° − 40° = 50°.

Q. Supplement of 100°?

Ans: 180° − 100° = 80°.

Q. What is a linear pair?

Ans: Two adjacent angles whose non-common arms form a straight line.

Q. Sum of the angles of a linear pair?

Ans: 180°.

Q. Relation between vertically opposite angles?

Ans: They are equal.

Q. Can both angles of a linear pair be acute?

Ans: No, their sum is 180°, so at least one is ≥ 90°.

Q. Corresponding angles for parallel lines?

Ans: Equal.

Q. Sum of co-interior angles for parallel lines?

Ans: 180°.

Q. Sum of the angles of a triangle?

Ans: 180°.

Q. State the exterior angle property.

Ans: An exterior angle = sum of the two interior opposite angles.

Q. What is a reflex angle?

Ans: An angle more than 180° and less than 360°.

Q. How many degrees is a right angle?

Ans: 90°.

Multiple Choice (MCQ)

Q. Supplement of 75°?
(a) 15° (b) 105° (c) 115° (d) 25°

Ans: (b) 180 − 75 = 105°.

Q. Sum of angles of a linear pair?
(a) 90° (b) 180° (c) 270° (d) 360°

Ans: (b) 180°.

Q. Two angles of a triangle are 50° and 60°. The third?
(a) 60° (b) 65° (c) 70° (d) 80°

Ans: (c) 180 − 110 = 70°.

Q. For parallel lines, alternate interior angles are:
(a) supplementary (b) equal (c) complementary (d) none

Ans: (b) equal.

Q. Complement of x is:
(a) 90° − x (b) 180° − x (c) x − 90° (d) 360° − x

Ans: (a) 90° − x.

Long Answer

Q. Prove that vertically opposite angles are equal.

Ans: See the hard example in Section 7: both linear pairs give ∠AOC = ∠BOD. ∎

Q. Prove that the angle sum of a triangle is 180°.

Ans: See the hard example in Section 7: draw a line through A parallel to BC and use alternate angles.

Q. The angles of a linear pair made by a transversal on parallel lines are in ratio 2 : 3. Find them.

Ans: Sum 180°, 5 parts, one part 36° ⇒ 72° and 108°.

Q. Prove that an exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of the two interior opposite angles.

Ans: In ΔABC extend BC to D. ∠ACD + ∠ACB = 180° (linear pair) and ∠A + ∠B + ∠ACB = 180°. Hence ∠ACD = ∠A + ∠B. ∎
