Class 9 Maths – Heron’s Formula

UP Board · Handwritten-style notes

📘 Notes

1. Area of a triangle – recap

  • Any triangle: Area = ½ × base × height (needs the height).
  • Right triangle: Area = ½ × product of the two perpendicular sides.
  • Equilateral triangle (side a): Area = (√3/4) a²; height = (√3/2) a.
  • Isosceles triangle (equal sides a, base b): Area = (b/4)√(4a² − b²).

When only the three sides are known and the height is not, we use Heron’s formula.

2. Heron’s formula

For a triangle with sides a, b, c:

s = (a + b + c) ÷ 2 (semi-perimeter)
Area = √[ s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c) ]

  • Valid for every triangle (scalene, isosceles, equilateral, right).
  • Area is in square units (cm², m²); all sides must be in the same unit.
  • A triangle exists only if the sum of any two sides is greater than the third, so s − a, s − b, s − c are all positive.

3. Steps and use

  1. Write the sides a, b, c.
  2. Find s = (a + b + c)/2.
  3. Find s − a, s − b, s − c.
  4. Multiply s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c) and take the square root.
  5. Write the answer with units.

Height on side a from area: h = 2 × Area ÷ a.

If the perimeter and a ratio of sides are given, find actual sides first (e.g. ratio 3 : 5 : 7 with perimeter 300 ⇒ 60, 100, 140).

4. Area of quadrilaterals

  • Draw a diagonal – it splits the quadrilateral into two triangles.
  • Find the area of each triangle (Heron’s formula, or ½ × b × h if it is right-angled) and add.
  • Rhombus with side a and one diagonal d: split into 2 congruent triangles with sides a, a, d; Area = 2 × (area of one triangle).
  • Parallelogram: one diagonal gives two congruent triangles.

✏️ Solved Examples

EasyQ1. Find the area of a triangle with sides 3 cm, 4 cm, 5 cm.

Given: Find the area of a triangle with sides 3 cm, 4 cm, 5 cm.

To prove / find: Area

Solution:
a = 3, b = 4, c = 5.
s = (3 + 4 + 5)/2 = 6.
Area = √[6 × (6 − 3) × (6 − 4) × (6 − 5)] = √(6 × 3 × 2 × 1) = √36 = 6 cm².

EasyQ2. Find the area of an equilateral triangle of side 8 cm (√3 = 1.73).

Given: Find the area of an equilateral triangle of side 8 cm (√3 = 1.73).

To prove / find: Area

Solution:
Area = (√3/4) a² = (√3/4) × 64 = 16√3.
= 16 × 1.73 = 27.68 cm² (approx).

IntermediateQ3. Find the area of a triangle with sides 13 cm, 14 cm, 15 cm and the height on the side 14 cm.

Given: Find the area of a triangle with sides 13 cm, 14 cm, 15 cm and the height on the side 14 cm.

To prove / find: Area and height

Solution:
s = (13 + 14 + 15)/2 = 21.
s − a = 8, s − b = 7, s − c = 6 (taking a = 13, b = 14, c = 15).
Area = √(21 × 8 × 7 × 6) = √7056 = 84 cm².
Height on 14 cm: h = 2 × 84 ÷ 14 = 12 cm.

IntermediateQ4. An isosceles triangle has equal sides 5 cm each and base 6 cm. Find its area.

Given: An isosceles triangle has equal sides 5 cm each and base 6 cm. Find its area.

To prove / find: Area

Solution:
Sides 5, 5, 6. s = (5 + 5 + 6)/2 = 8.
s − a = 3, s − b = 3, s − c = 2.
Area = √(8 × 3 × 3 × 2) = √144 = 12 cm².

HardQ5. In quadrilateral ABCD, AB = 3 cm, BC = 4 cm, CD = 12 cm, DA = 13 cm and ∠ABC = 90°. Find its area.

Given: In quadrilateral ABCD, AB = 3 cm, BC = 4 cm, CD = 12 cm, DA = 13 cm and ∠ABC = 90°. Find its area.

To prove / find: Area of ABCD

Solution:
Join AC. In right △ABC: AC² = 3² + 4² = 25 ⇒ AC = 5 cm.
Area of △ABC = ½ × 3 × 4 = 6 cm².
△ACD has sides 5, 12, 13. s = (5 + 12 + 13)/2 = 15.
Area = √(15 × 10 × 3 × 2) = √900 = 30 cm².
Area of ABCD = 6 + 30 = 36 cm².

HardQ6. A triangular park has sides in the ratio 3 : 5 : 7 and perimeter 300 m. Find its area (√3 = 1.73).

Given: A triangular park has sides in the ratio 3 : 5 : 7 and perimeter 300 m. Find its area (√3 = 1.73).

To prove / find: Area

Solution:
Let sides = 3x, 5x, 7x. 15x = 300 ⇒ x = 20.
Sides: 60 m, 100 m, 140 m. s = 150.
s − a = 90, s − b = 50, s − c = 10.
Area = √(150 × 90 × 50 × 10) = √6,750,000 = 1500√3.
= 1500 × 1.73 = 2595 m² (approx).

❓ Short & Long Q&A

Q1. State Heron’s formula.

Answer: Area = √[s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)], where s = (a + b + c)/2 and a, b, c are the sides.

Q2. When do we use Heron’s formula?

Answer: When the three sides of a triangle are known but its height is not.

Q3. Find the semi-perimeter of a triangle with sides 7, 8, 9 cm.

Answer: (7 + 8 + 9)/2 = 12 cm.

Q4. Write the area formula of an equilateral triangle of side a.

Answer: (√3/4) a².

Q5. Find the area of a triangle with sides 5, 12, 13.

Answer: s = 15; √(15 × 10 × 3 × 2) = √900 = 30 sq units.

Q6. Find the area of a rhombus of side 5 cm and one diagonal 8 cm.

Answer: Triangle with sides 5, 5, 8: s = 9; area = √(9 × 4 × 4 × 1) = 12. Rhombus = 2 × 12 = 24 cm².

Q7. Can a triangle have sides 2, 3, 6 cm?

Answer: No, 2 + 3 = 5 < 6. Then s − c would be negative, so Heron’s formula fails.

Q8. Area of an equilateral triangle is 9√3 cm². Find its perimeter.

Answer: (√3/4)a² = 9√3 ⇒ a² = 36 ⇒ a = 6. Perimeter = 3 × 6 = 18 cm.

Q9. How can you find a height of a triangle using Heron’s formula?

Answer: Find area by Heron’s formula, then h = 2 × Area ÷ base.
