✏️ Class 9 Maths · Chapter 8: Linear Equations in Two Variables (UP Board)

📌 Introduction: When two unknown quantities (like the prices of a pen and a notebook) are linked by a simple relation, we write it as a linear equation in two variables.

1. Definition

ax + by + c = 0, where a, b, c are real numbers and a, b are not both zero. The variables have power 1.

Example: 2x + 3y = 12 ⇒ 2x + 3y − 12 = 0.  x = 5 can also be written as 1·x + 0·y − 5 = 0.
Not linear: x² + y = 1, xy = 3, x + 1/y = 2.

2. Solution

An ordered pair (x, y) that satisfies the equation is a solution. A linear equation in two variables has infinitely many solutions.

E.g. solutions of x + y = 4: (0, 4), (1, 3), (2, 2), (4, 0), (−1, 5), …

3. Graph

• The graph of a linear equation is always a straight line.
• Every point on the line is a solution and every solution is a point on the line.
• Take at least 2 points to draw the line, 3 to check.

4. Special Lines

• Equation of the x-axis: y = 0; of the y-axis: x = 0
• x = a: line parallel to the y-axis; y = b: line parallel to the x-axis
• y = mx: line through the origin

5. Solved Examples

Write in the form ax + by + c = 0: 2x = 5y − 7
Step 1: Bring all terms to the left: 2x − 5y + 7 = 0
a = 2, b = −5, c = 7.
Check: Is (2, 3) a solution of 3x + 2y = 12?
Step 1: Put x = 2, y = 3.
Step 2: 3(2) + 2(3) = 6 + 6 = 12 = RHS
Yes, it is a solution.
Find y: In 3x + y = 10, x = 2.
Step 1: 3(2) + y = 10 ⇒ 6 + y = 10
y = 4.
Find k: (1, 2) is a solution of 3x + ky = 7.
Step 1: 3(1) + k(2) = 7
Step 2: 2k = 4
k = 2.
Draw the graph: y = 2x
Step 1: x = 0 ⇒ y = 0 ⇒ (0, 0)
Step 2: x = 1 ⇒ y = 2 ⇒ (1, 2); x = −1 ⇒ y = −2 ⇒ (−1, −2)
Step 3: Plot the three points and join. The line passes through the origin.
Write the equation: the line parallel to the x-axis through (3, −2).
Step 1: On a line parallel to the x-axis, y stays constant.
Step 2: At the given point y = −2.
y = −2.
Hard: A notebook costs twice as much as a pen. Write a linear equation.
Step 1: Notebook = ₹x, pen = ₹y.
Step 2: x = 2y
x − 2y = 0.
Hard: Find the equation of the line through (2, 3) and (4, 5).
Step 1: Both points fit y = x + 1 (3 = 2 + 1, 5 = 4 + 1).
Step 2: The equation is y = x + 1.
x − y + 1 = 0.
Hard: Where does 2x + 3y = 12 cut the axes, and what is the area of the triangle formed?
Step 1: x = 0 ⇒ 3y = 12 ⇒ y = 4, point (0, 4).
Step 2: y = 0 ⇒ 2x = 12 ⇒ x = 6, point (6, 0).
Step 3: Area = ½ × 6 × 4 = 12 square units.

6. Q & A (click to see the answer)

Short Answer

Q. What is a linear equation in two variables?

Ans: An equation of the form ax + by + c = 0 with a, b not both zero.

Q. How many solutions does it have?

Ans: Infinitely many.

Q. Is (1, 1) a solution of x + y = 2?

Ans: Yes, 1 + 1 = 2.

Q. What does the graph of a linear equation look like?

Ans: A straight line.

Q. Equation of the x-axis?

Ans: y = 0.

Q. Equation of the y-axis?

Ans: x = 0.

Q. Form of a line through the origin?

Ans: y = mx, e.g. y = 2x.

Q. Line parallel to the y-axis through (4, 5)?

Ans: x = 4.

Q. Is (1, 1) a solution of x + 2y = 3?

Ans: Yes, 1 + 2 = 3.

Q. Is 2x + 3 = 0 an equation in two variables?

Ans: Yes, it can be written as 2x + 0·y + 3 = 0.

Q. Minimum points needed to draw a line?

Ans: 2 (3 to check).

Q. What are the solutions of x = 5 in two variables?

Ans: (5, y) for any real y.

Multiple Choice (MCQ)

Q. A linear equation in two variables is:
(a) x²+y=1 (b) x+y=5 (c) xy=3 (d) x+1/y=2

Ans: (b) x + y = 5.

Q. (2, −1) is a solution of:
(a) x+y=1 (b) x−y=1 (c) 2x+y=1 (d) x+2y=1

Ans: (a) 2 − 1 = 1.

Q. The graph of y = 3x passes through:
(a) (0,3) (b) (1,3) (c) (0,0) (d) (3,1)

Ans: (c) at x=0, y=0.

Q. The graph of x = −2 is parallel to:
(a) x-axis (b) y-axis (c) y = x (d) none

Ans: (b) parallel to the y-axis.

Q. If (3, k) lies on 2x + y = 10, k = ?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 6

Ans: (c) 6 + k = 10 ⇒ k = 4.

Long Answer

Q. Draw the graph of 2x + y = 6.

Ans: Table: x=0 ⇒ y=6; x=1 ⇒ y=4; x=2 ⇒ y=2; x=3 ⇒ y=0. Plot (0,6), (1,4), (2,2), (3,0) and draw the straight line.

Q. Find k: x = 1, y = −2 is a solution of 2x + ky = 8.

Ans: 2(1) + k(−2) = 8 ⇒ 2 − 2k = 8 ⇒ k = −3.

Q. Show that (1,2), (2,4), (3,6) lie on the line y = 2x.

Ans: In y = 2x: x = 1 ⇒ 2, x = 2 ⇒ 4, x = 3 ⇒ 6. All three points satisfy the equation. ∎

Q. Find a if (a, 2a) lies on x + y = 9.

Ans: a + 2a = 9 ⇒ 3a = 9 ⇒ a = 3, point (3, 6).
