Class 9 Maths – Chapter 12: Quadrilaterals

UP Board · Handwritten-style notes

📘 Notes

1. Quadrilateral and Parallelogram

A quadrilateral has 4 sides, 4 angles, 4 vertices and 2 diagonals. Angle sum = 360° (a diagonal splits it into two triangles: 180° + 180°).

A parallelogram has both pairs of opposite sides parallel (AB ∥ DC, AD ∥ BC).

Properties:

  • A diagonal divides it into two congruent triangles.
  • Opposite sides are equal.
  • Opposite angles are equal.
  • Diagonals bisect each other.
  • Adjacent angles are supplementary (sum 180°).

2. Tests for a parallelogram

A quadrilateral is a parallelogram if:

  • both pairs of opposite sides are equal, or
  • both pairs of opposite angles are equal, or
  • its diagonals bisect each other, or
  • one pair of opposite sides is equal and parallel.

3. Rectangle, Rhombus, Square

  • Rectangle: parallelogram with one right angle (all angles 90°). Diagonals are equal.
  • Rhombus: parallelogram with all sides equal. Diagonals bisect each other at 90° and bisect the angles.
  • Square: rectangle + rhombus. All sides equal, all angles 90°, diagonals equal and perpendicular.

Converse: parallelogram with equal diagonals is a rectangle; with perpendicular diagonals is a rhombus.

4. Mid-point theorem

Theorem: The line segment joining the mid-points of two sides of a triangle is parallel to the third side and equal to half of it.

Converse: A line through the mid-point of one side, parallel to another side, bisects the third side.

Also: joining mid-points of the sides of any quadrilateral in order gives a parallelogram.

✏️ Solved Examples

EasyQ1. Angles of a quadrilateral are in ratio 1 : 2 : 3 : 4. Find each angle.

Given: Angles of a quadrilateral are in ratio 1 : 2 : 3 : 4. Find each angle.

To prove / find: All four angles

Solution:
Let angles = x, 2x, 3x, 4x. Sum = 360°.
10x = 360° ⇒ x = 36°.
Angles: 36°, 72°, 108°, 144°.

EasyQ2. In parallelogram ABCD, ∠A = 70°. Find ∠B, ∠C, ∠D.

Given: In parallelogram ABCD, ∠A = 70°. Find ∠B, ∠C, ∠D.

To prove / find: ∠B, ∠C, ∠D

Solution:
Opposite angles equal: ∠C = ∠A = 70°.
Adjacent angles supplementary: ∠B = 180° − 70° = 110°.
∠D = ∠B = 110°.

IntermediateQ3. Prove that opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal.

Given: Prove that opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal.

To prove / find: AB = DC and AD = BC

Solution:
Draw diagonal AC.
AB ∥ DC ⇒ ∠BAC = ∠DCA (alternate angles). AD ∥ BC ⇒ ∠BCA = ∠DAC.
In △ABC and △CDA: ∠BAC = ∠DCA, AC = CA (common), ∠BCA = ∠DAC.
△ABC ≅ △CDA (ASA) ⇒ AB = DC, BC = AD (CPCT).

IntermediateQ4. In rhombus ABCD, ∠A = 60° and AB = 8 cm. Find diagonal BD.

Given: In rhombus ABCD, ∠A = 60° and AB = 8 cm. Find diagonal BD.

To prove / find: BD

Solution:
All sides of a rhombus are equal: AB = AD = 8 cm.
In △ABD, AB = AD ⇒ ∠ABD = ∠ADB = x. x + x + 60° = 180° ⇒ x = 60°.
So △ABD is equilateral ⇒ BD = 8 cm.

HardQ5. Prove the mid-point theorem.

Given: Prove the mid-point theorem.

To prove / find: In △ABC, E, F mid-points of AB, AC ⇒ EF ∥ BC, EF = ½BC

Solution:
Extend EF to D so that FD = EF. Join CD.
In △AFE and △CFD: AF = CF (F mid-point), ∠AFE = ∠CFD (vertically opposite), EF = FD (construction).
△AFE ≅ △CFD (SAS) ⇒ AE = CD and ∠AEF = ∠CDF (CPCT). These are alternate angles ⇒ AB ∥ CD.
AE = EB, so EB = CD, and EB ∥ CD ⇒ EBCD is a parallelogram (one pair equal and parallel).
So ED ∥ BC and ED = BC. Since EF = ½ED, EF ∥ BC and EF = ½BC.

HardQ6. Prove that the diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles.

Given: Prove that the diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles.

To prove / find: AC ⊥ BD

Solution:
ABCD is a rhombus, diagonals meet at O. A rhombus is a parallelogram ⇒ OA = OC, OB = OD.
In △AOB and △COB: AB = CB (rhombus), OA = OC, OB = OB (common).
△AOB ≅ △COB (SSS) ⇒ ∠AOB = ∠COB (CPCT).
Linear pair: ∠AOB + ∠COB = 180° ⇒ 2∠AOB = 180°.
∠AOB = 90°, so AC ⊥ BD.

❓ Short & Long Q&A

Q1. Define a parallelogram.

Answer: A quadrilateral whose both pairs of opposite sides are parallel.

Q2. What is the angle sum of a quadrilateral? Why?

Answer: 360°. A diagonal divides it into two triangles, each with angle sum 180°.

Q3. State four conditions for a quadrilateral to be a parallelogram.

Answer: Opposite sides equal; opposite angles equal; diagonals bisect each other; one pair of opposite sides equal and parallel.

Q4. Difference between rectangle and rhombus?

Answer: Rectangle: all angles 90°, diagonals equal. Rhombus: all sides equal, diagonals perpendicular.

Q5. Is every square a rectangle? Is every rectangle a square?

Answer: Every square is a rectangle (right angles). Not every rectangle is a square (sides may be unequal).

Q6. Two adjacent angles of a parallelogram are in ratio 2 : 3. Find them.

Answer: 2x + 3x = 180° ⇒ x = 36°. Angles: 72° and 108°.

Q7. State the converse of the mid-point theorem.

Answer: A line drawn through the mid-point of one side of a triangle, parallel to another side, bisects the third side.

Q8. Sides of a triangle are 6, 8, 10 cm. Find perimeter of the triangle formed by joining mid-points.

Answer: Each new side = half of the corresponding side: 3, 4, 5. Perimeter = 12 cm.

Q9. Show that diagonals of a rectangle are equal.

Answer: In rectangle ABCD, △ABC and △DCB: AB = DC, BC = CB, ∠ABC = ∠DCB = 90°. SAS ⇒ AC = DB (CPCT).

✅ MCQs

Q1. Angle sum of a quadrilateral:
  1. 180°
  2. 270°
  3. 360°
  4. 540°

Answer: C – 360°. Two triangles.

Q2. In a parallelogram, adjacent angles are:
  1. Equal
  2. Supplementary
  3. Complementary
  4. Right

Answer: B – Supplementary. Sum = 180°.

Q3. Diagonals of a rhombus are:
  1. Equal
  2. Perpendicular bisectors
  3. Parallel
  4. Unequal and not bisecting

Answer: B – Perpendicular bisectors. They meet at 90° and bisect.

Q4. A parallelogram with equal diagonals is a:
  1. Rhombus
  2. Rectangle
  3. Trapezium
  4. Kite

Answer: B – Rectangle. Converse property.

Q5. If E, F are mid-points of AB, AC and BC = 10 cm, then EF = ?
  1. 5 cm
  2. 10 cm
  3. 20 cm
  4. 15 cm

Answer: A – 5 cm. EF = ½BC.

Q6. Each angle of a rectangle is:
  1. 60°
  2. 90°
  3. 120°
  4. 45°

Answer: B – 90°. By definition.

Q7. Which has all sides and all angles equal?
  1. Rhombus
  2. Rectangle
  3. Square
  4. Parallelogram

Answer: C – Square. Square.

Q8. In parallelogram ABCD, ∠A = 65°. ∠B = ?
  1. 65°
  2. 115°
  3. 125°
  4. 25°

Answer: B – 115°. 180° − 65°.
