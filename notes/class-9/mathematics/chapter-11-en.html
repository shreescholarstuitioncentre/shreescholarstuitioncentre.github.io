Class 9 Maths – Chapter 11: Triangles

UP Board · Handwritten-style notes

📘 Notes

1. Congruent figures

Two figures with exactly the same shape and size are congruent (symbol ≅). Place one over the other – they match completely.

If △ABC ≅ △PQR then A↔P, B↔Q, C↔R. Order of letters matters!

CPCT = Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles are equal. Use it after proving congruence.

2. Criteria of congruence

  • SAS: two sides + the included angle equal.
  • ASA: two angles + the included side equal.
  • AAS: two angles + any one non-included side equal.
  • SSS: three sides equal.
  • RHS: right angle, hypotenuse and one side equal (right triangles only).

⚠ AAA and SSA do not prove congruence.

3. Isosceles triangle

Theorem: Angles opposite equal sides are equal.
Converse: Sides opposite equal angles are equal.

Equilateral triangle: all sides equal, each angle = 60°. Angle sum of a triangle = 180°.

4. Inequalities in a triangle

  • Exterior angle > each interior opposite angle.
  • Larger side ↔ larger opposite angle (and converse).
  • Sum of any two sides > third side.
  • Of all segments from a point to a line, the perpendicular is shortest.

✏️ Solved Examples

EasyQ1. In △ABC, AB = AC and ∠B = 50°. Find ∠A.

Given: In △ABC, AB = AC and ∠B = 50°. Find ∠A.

To prove / find: ∠A

Solution:
AB = AC ⇒ ∠C = ∠B = 50° (angles opposite equal sides).
∠A = 180° − 50° − 50° = 80°.

EasyQ2. Can a triangle have sides 3 cm, 4 cm, 8 cm?

Given: Can a triangle have sides 3 cm, 4 cm, 8 cm?

To prove / find: Possible or not

Solution:
3 + 4 = 7, but 7 < 8. Sum of two sides must exceed the third. Not possible.

IntermediateQ3. AB and CD bisect each other at O. Prove AC = BD and AC ∥ BD.

Given: AB and CD bisect each other at O. Prove AC = BD and AC ∥ BD.

To prove / find: AC = BD

Solution:
Step 1: In △AOC and △BOD: AO = OB (given), CO = OD (given).
Step 2: ∠AOC = ∠BOD (vertically opposite).
Step 3: △AOC ≅ △BOD (SAS).
Step 4: AC = BD and ∠CAO = ∠OBD (CPCT). These are alternate angles, so AC ∥ BD.

IntermediateQ4. In isosceles △ABC (AB = AC), D is the midpoint of BC. Prove AD ⊥ BC.

Given: In isosceles △ABC (AB = AC), D is the midpoint of BC. Prove AD ⊥ BC.

To prove / find: ∠ADB = 90°

Solution:
In △ABD and △ACD: AB = AC, BD = DC, AD = AD (common).
△ABD ≅ △ACD (SSS) ⇒ ∠ADB = ∠ADC (CPCT).
They form a linear pair: ∠ADB + ∠ADC = 180° ⇒ 2∠ADB = 180°.
∠ADB = 90°.

HardQ5. Prove: angles opposite equal sides of a triangle are equal.

Given: Prove: angles opposite equal sides of a triangle are equal.

To prove / find: ∠B = ∠C when AB = AC

Solution:
Draw AD, the bisector of ∠A, meeting BC at D.
In △BAD and △CAD: AB = AC (given), ∠BAD = ∠CAD (bisector), AD = AD (common).
△BAD ≅ △CAD (SAS) ⇒ ∠B = ∠C (CPCT).

HardQ6. In △ABC, altitudes BE and CF are equal. Prove AB = AC.

Given: In △ABC, altitudes BE and CF are equal. Prove AB = AC.

To prove / find: AB = AC

Solution:
In right △BEC and △CFB: ∠BEC = ∠CFB = 90°, BC = CB (common hypotenuse), BE = CF (given).
△BEC ≅ △CFB (RHS) ⇒ ∠C = ∠B (CPCT).
Sides opposite equal angles are equal ⇒ AB = AC.

❓ Short & Long Q&A

Q1. What are congruent triangles?

Answer: Triangles with equal corresponding sides and angles – same shape and size.

Q2. State the SAS rule.

Answer: If two sides and the included angle of one triangle equal those of another, the triangles are congruent.

Q3. Why is SSA not a congruence rule?

Answer: The equal angle is not between the equal sides, so two different triangles can be drawn.

Q4. What does CPCT mean?

Answer: Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles – they are equal. Used after proving congruence.

Q5. State RHS rule.

Answer: Two right triangles are congruent if hypotenuse and one side of one equal those of the other.

Q6. Find each angle of an equilateral triangle.

Answer: All angles equal, 3x = 180° ⇒ x = 60°.

Q7. In △ABC, ∠A = 70°, ∠B = 60°. Which side is longest?

Answer: ∠C = 50°. Largest angle is A (70°), so side BC (opposite A) is longest.

Q8. Prove that the perpendicular is the shortest segment from a point to a line.

Answer: Let PM ⊥ l and PN be any other segment. In right △PMN, ∠M = 90° is the largest angle, so PN (opposite) is longer than PM.

✅ MCQs

Q1. Which is NOT a congruence rule?
  1. SAS
  2. SSS
  3. AAA
  4. RHS

Answer: C – AAA. Equal angles only give similar triangles.

Q2. AB = AC and ∠A = 40°. ∠B = ?
  1. 40°
  2. 70°
  3. 100°
  4. 140°

Answer: B – 70°. (180 − 40)/2 = 70°.

Q3. Sides 5, 7, 12 form a triangle?
  1. Yes
  2. No
  3. Right triangle
  4. Isosceles

Answer: B – No. 5 + 7 = 12, not greater.

Q4. Each angle of an equilateral triangle:
  1. 30°
  2. 45°
  3. 60°
  4. 90°

Answer: C – 60°. 180° ÷ 3.

Q5. Longest side is opposite to the:
  1. Smallest angle
  2. Largest angle
  3. Right angle only
  4. None

Answer: B – Largest angle. Larger angle ↔ larger side.

Q6. RHS applies to:
  1. Any triangle
  2. Isosceles only
  3. Right triangles
  4. Equilateral

Answer: C – Right triangles. Right angle–Hypotenuse–Side.

Q7. Exterior angle of a triangle equals:
  1. Sum of interior opposite angles
  2. Adjacent angle
  3. 180°
  4. Difference

Answer: A – Sum of interior opposite angles. Exterior angle property.

Q8. △ABC ≅ △DEF, then BC = ?
  1. DE
  2. EF
  3. DF
  4. AB

Answer: B – EF. B↔E, C↔F.
