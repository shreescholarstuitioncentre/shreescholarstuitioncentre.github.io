✏️ Class 9 Maths · Chapter 6: Remainder Theorem & Factor Theorem (UP Board)

📌 Introduction: These two theorems let us find the remainder without long division and test whether a linear expression is a factor of a polynomial.

1. Remainder Theorem

Statement: If p(x) is a polynomial of degree ≥ 1 and is divided by (x − a), the remainder = p(a).
Variants:
• Divide by (x + a): remainder p(−a)
• Divide by (ax + b): remainder p(−b/a)
• Divide by (b − ax): remainder p(b/a)

Proof

By the division algorithm, p(x) = (x − a)·q(x) + r (r constant). Put x = a: p(a) = 0·q(a) + r = r. So the remainder r = p(a). ∎

2. Factor Theorem

Statement: (x − a) is a factor of p(x) ⇔ p(a) = 0.
Meaning: a zero remainder means the division is exact.

Uses: (i) testing a factor, (ii) finding an unknown k, (iii) factorising cubic polynomials. To test, try factors of the constant term (±1, ±2, ±3, …).

3. Solved Examples

Remainder: x³ + 3x² + 3x + 1 divided by (x + 1).
Step 1: x + 1 = x − (−1) ⇒ a = −1
Step 2: p(−1) = −1 + 3 − 3 + 1 = 0
Remainder = 0.
Remainder: 2x³ − 5x² + x − 3 divided by (2x − 1).
Step 1: 2x − 1 = 0 ⇒ x = 1/2
Step 2: p(1/2) = 2/8 − 5/4 + 1/2 − 3 = 1/4 − 5/4 + 1/2 − 3
Step 3: = −1 + 1/2 − 3 = −7/2
Remainder = −7/2.
Check: Is (x − 3) a factor of x³ − 4x² + x + 6?
Step 1: p(3) = 27 − 36 + 3 + 6
Step 2: = 0
Yes, it is a factor.
Find k: (x + 2) is a factor of 2x³ + kx² − 5x − 6.
Step 1: p(−2) = 0
Step 2: 2(−8) + 4k + 10 − 6 = 0 ⇒ 4k − 12 = 0
k = 3.
Factorise: x³ − 7x + 6
Step 1: p(1) = 1 − 7 + 6 = 0 ⇒ (x−1) is a factor.
Step 2: Dividing gives x² + x − 6.
Step 3: x² + x − 6 = (x+3)(x−2).
(x−1)(x+3)(x−2).
Prove: (x − a) is a factor of xⁿ − aⁿ.
Step 1: Let p(x) = xⁿ − aⁿ.
Step 2: p(a) = aⁿ − aⁿ = 0.
Step 3: By the Factor Theorem, (x − a) is a factor. ∎
Hard: x³ + ax² + 3x − 5 and x² + ax + 3 leave the same remainder when divided by (x − 2). Find a.
Step 1: p(2) = 8 + 4a + 6 − 5 = 4a + 9
Step 2: q(2) = 4 + 2a + 3 = 2a + 7
Step 3: 4a + 9 = 2a + 7 ⇒ 2a = −2
a = −1.
Hard: (x − 2) is a factor of x³ + ax² + bx + 6, and the remainder on dividing by (x − 3) is 3. Find a, b.
Step 1: p(2) = 0 ⇒ 8 + 4a + 2b + 6 = 0 ⇒ 2a + b = −7 …(i)
Step 2: p(3) = 3 ⇒ 27 + 9a + 3b + 6 = 3 ⇒ 3a + b = −10 …(ii)
Step 3: (ii) − (i): a = −3, then b = −7 + 6 = −1.
a = −3, b = −1.

4. Q & A (click to see the answer)

Short Answer

Q. Remainder on dividing by (x + a)?

Ans: p(−a).

Q. Remainder on dividing by (ax + b)?

Ans: p(−b/a).

Q. Remainder of x² − 3x + 2 ÷ (x − 1)?

Ans: 1 − 3 + 2 = 0.

Q. Is (x − 1) a factor of xⁿ − 1?

Ans: Yes, p(1) = 1 − 1 = 0.

Q. Remainder of x⁵ + 1 ÷ (x + 1)?

Ans: p(−1) = −1 + 1 = 0.

Q. Is (x − 2) a factor of x³ − 8?

Ans: Yes, p(2) = 0.

Q. Remainder of 2x² − 3 ÷ (x − 2)?

Ans: 8 − 3 = 5.

Q. Is (x + 1) a factor of x² + 1?

Ans: No, p(−1) = 2 ≠ 0.

Q. Remainder of x³ + x² + x + 1 ÷ (x − 1)?

Ans: 1+1+1+1 = 4.

Q. Remainder of 3x + 7 ÷ (x + 1)?

Ans: p(−1) = 4.

Q. Degree of the remainder for a linear divisor?

Ans: 0 (the remainder is a constant).

Q. What is the converse of the Factor Theorem?

Ans: If p(a) = 0 then (x − a) is a factor.

Multiple Choice (MCQ)

Q. Remainder of x² + x + 1 ÷ (x − 1)?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 0

Ans: (c) p(1) = 3.

Q. (x − 2) is a factor of:
(a) x²+4 (b) x²−4 (c) x²−2x+1 (d) x²+2

Ans: (b) 4 − 4 = 0.

Q. Remainder of 2x³ − x ÷ (2x + 1)?
(a) 1/4 (b) −1/4 (c) 0 (d) 1/2

Ans: (a) p(−1/2) = −1/4 + 1/2 = 1/4.

Q. If (x − a) is a factor of p(x):
(a) p(a) = 0 (b) p(a) = 1 (c) p(−a) = 0 (d) p(0) = a

Ans: (a) p(a) = 0.

Q. Remainder of x³ − 3x² + 5 ÷ (x + 1)?
(a) 1 (b) −1 (c) 5 (d) 0

Ans: (a) −1 − 3 + 5 = 1.

Long Answer

Q. Prove the Remainder Theorem.

Ans: See the proof in Theorem 1 — put x = a in p(x) = (x−a)q(x) + r to get p(a) = r. ∎

Q. Factorise: 2x³ + x² − 13x + 6.

Ans: p(2) = 16 + 4 − 26 + 6 = 0 ⇒ (x−2). Quotient: 2x² + 5x − 3 = (2x−1)(x+3). Answer: (x−2)(2x−1)(x+3).

Q. Find a if ax³ + 3x² − 3 and 2x³ − 5x + a leave the same remainder on division by (x − 4).

Ans: p(4) = 64a + 48 − 3 = 64a + 45; q(4) = 128 − 20 + a = 108 + a. Equal: 63a = 63 ⇒ a = 1.

Q. Factorise: x³ + x² − 4x − 4.

Ans: p(2) = 8+4−8−4 = 0 ⇒ (x−2). Quotient: x² + 3x + 2 = (x+1)(x+2). Answer: (x−2)(x+1)(x+2).
