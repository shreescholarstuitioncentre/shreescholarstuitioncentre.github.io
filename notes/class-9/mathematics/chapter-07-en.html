📌 Introduction: To fix the position of a point in a plane we use two perpendicular number lines. This is the Cartesian system (named after René Descartes).

1. The Cartesian System

• The horizontal line X′OX is the x-axis; the vertical line Y′OY is the y-axis.
• The point O where they meet is the origin, with coordinates (0, 0).
• The axes divide the plane into four parts called quadrants.

2. Coordinates of a Point

In a point (x, y), x = abscissa: distance from the y-axis (right +, left −); y = ordinate: distance from the x-axis (up +, down −).

• Point on the x-axis: (a, 0)  • Point on the y-axis: (0, b)  • (a, b) ≠ (b, a) when a ≠ b (ordered pair).
• Two ordered pairs are equal if their corresponding coordinates are equal.

3. Solved Examples

Name the quadrant: (−3, 5), (4, −2), (−1, −6), (0, 3)
Step 1: (−3, 5): x negative, y positive ⇒ II
Step 2: (4, −2): (+, −) ⇒ IV
Step 3: (−1, −6): (−, −) ⇒ III
Step 4: (0, 3): x = 0 ⇒ on the y-axis, in no quadrant.
Write: the abscissa and ordinate of (−5, 7).
Abscissa = −5, ordinate = 7.
Plot: A(2, 3)
Step 1: Start at the origin O and move 2 units right along the x-axis.
Step 2: From there move 3 units up.
Step 3: The point reached is A(2, 3), in quadrant I.
Write the coordinates: the point 4 units left of the y-axis and 3 units above the x-axis.
Step 1: Left ⇒ x = −4
Step 2: Above ⇒ y = +3
(−4, 3).
Write: the point with abscissa −2 lying on the x-axis.
On the x-axis the ordinate is 0 ⇒ (−2, 0).
Find x, y: (x + 2, y − 3) = (5, 1)
Step 1: x + 2 = 5 ⇒ x = 3
Step 2: y − 3 = 1 ⇒ y = 4
x = 3, y = 4.
Fourth vertex: Three vertices of a rectangle are (1, 1), (4, 1), (4, 3).
Step 1: (1,1) and (4,1) form a horizontal side (y = 1).
Step 2: (4,1) and (4,3) form a vertical side (x = 4).
Step 3: The fourth vertex has x = 1 and y = 3.
(1, 3).
Hard: Find the reflections of (3, −2) in the x-axis, y-axis and origin.
Step 1: In the x-axis the sign of y changes ⇒ (3, 2).
Step 2: In the y-axis the sign of x changes ⇒ (−3, −2).
Step 3: In the origin both signs change ⇒ (−3, 2).
Hard: If (a + b, 2) = (5, a − b), find a and b.
Step 1: a + b = 5 …(i)
Step 2: a − b = 2 …(ii)
Step 3: Adding: 2a = 7 ⇒ a = 7/2, then b = 5 − 7/2 = 3/2.
a = 7/2, b = 3/2.

4. Q & A (click to see the answer)

Short Answer

Q. After whom is the Cartesian system named?

Ans: René Descartes.

Q. Coordinates of the origin?

Ans: (0, 0).

Q. What is the abscissa?

Ans: The x-coordinate of a point, i.e. its distance from the y-axis.

Q. What is the ordinate?

Ans: The y-coordinate of a point, i.e. its distance from the x-axis.

Q. Which quadrant has (−2, −3)?

Ans: III.

Q. Ordinate of a point on the x-axis?

Ans: 0.

Q. Abscissa of a point on the y-axis?

Ans: 0.

Q. Are (2, 3) and (3, 2) the same point?

Ans: No, the order in an ordered pair matters.

Q. How many quadrants are there?

Ans: Four.

Q. Signs in quadrant IV?

Ans: (+, −).

Q. Where does (0, −5) lie?

Ans: On the negative y-axis.

Q. Where does (4, 0) lie?

Ans: On the positive x-axis.

Multiple Choice (MCQ)

Q. (−4, 6) lies in which quadrant?
(a) I (b) II (c) III (d) IV

Ans: (b) II: (−, +).

Q. Abscissa of (5, −3)?
(a) 5 (b) −3 (c) 2 (d) 8

Ans: (a) 5.

Q. (0, −7) lies on:
(a) x-axis (b) positive y-axis (c) negative y-axis (d) quadrant III

Ans: (c) negative y-axis.

Q. Which point is in quadrant IV?
(a) (−1,2) (b) (3,−4) (c) (−2,−2) (d) (1,1)

Ans: (b) (+, −).

Q. Distance of (3, 4) from the x-axis?
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 7

Ans: (b) ordinate = 4.

Long Answer

Q. Plot A(2,1), B(5,1), C(5,4), D(2,4) and identify the figure.

Ans: Plotting gives AB = 3 (horizontal), BC = 3 (vertical), CD = 3, DA = 3 and all angles 90° ⇒ a square of side 3.

Q. Explain the quadrants of the Cartesian system and the signs of coordinates in them.

Ans: See the table in Section 1: I (+,+), II (−,+), III (−,−), IV (+,−); points on the axes lie in no quadrant.

Q. If (2a + 1, b − 3) = (5, 2), find a and b.

Ans: 2a + 1 = 5 ⇒ a = 2; b − 3 = 2 ⇒ b = 5.

Q. Find the distances of (−5, 3) from both axes and name its quadrant.

Ans: Distance from the x-axis = |3| = 3, from the y-axis = |−5| = 5; signs (−, +) ⇒ quadrant II.
