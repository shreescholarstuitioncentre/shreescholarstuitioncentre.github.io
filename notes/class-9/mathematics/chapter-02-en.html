📌 Introduction: When the same number is multiplied repeatedly we write it compactly using an exponent. E.g. 2×2×2×2×2 = 2⁵ = 32.

1. What is an Exponent?

aⁿ = a × a × a × … (n times). Here a = base, n = exponent (index), and aⁿ is the n-th power of a.

Examples: 3⁴ = 3×3×3×3 = 81; (−2)³ = −8; (−2)⁴ = 16 (a negative base with an even power is positive).

3. Rational Exponents

a^(1/n) = ⁿ√a  |  a^(m/n) = (ⁿ√a)ᵐ = ⁿ√(aᵐ)  |  a^(−m/n) = 1/a^(m/n)

The same laws hold, but the base must be a positive real number (a > 0).

4. Solved Examples

Evaluate: 8^(2/3)
Step 1: 8 = 2³
Step 2: 8^(2/3) = (2³)^(2/3) = 2^(3×2/3)
Step 3: = 2² = 4.
Evaluate: (27/8)^(−2/3)
Step 1: Invert to remove the negative power: = (8/27)^(2/3)
Step 2: 8/27 = (2/3)³
Step 3: ((2/3)³)^(2/3) = (2/3)² = 4/9.
Simplify: 16^(3/4) ÷ 8^(−1/3)
Step 1: 16^(3/4) = (2⁴)^(3/4) = 2³ = 8
Step 2: 8^(−1/3) = (2³)^(−1/3) = 2⁻¹ = 1/2
Step 3: 8 ÷ (1/2) = 16.
Find x: 2^(x+1) = 32
Step 1: 32 = 2⁵ ⇒ 2^(x+1) = 2⁵
Step 2: Same base, so exponents are equal: x + 1 = 5
Step 3: x = 4.
Prove: (xᵃ/xᵇ)^(a+b) · (xᵇ/xᶜ)^(b+c) · (xᶜ/xᵃ)^(c+a) = 1
Step 1: xᵃ/xᵇ = x^(a−b), and similarly for the others.
Step 2: LHS = x^((a−b)(a+b)) · x^((b−c)(b+c)) · x^((c−a)(c+a))
Step 3: = x^(a²−b²) · x^(b²−c²) · x^(c²−a²)
Step 4: Add exponents: a²−b²+b²−c²+c²−a² = 0
Step 5: = x⁰ = 1 = RHS. ∎

5. Q & A (click to see the answer)

Short Answer

Q. What is an exponent?

Ans: The number of times the base is multiplied by itself; in 2⁵ it is 5.

Q. Value of a⁰?

Ans: 1 (a ≠ 0).

Q. Difference between (−3)² and −3²?

Ans: (−3)² = 9 while −3² = −9 (the power applies only to 3).

Q. Value of 2⁻⁴?

Ans: 1/2⁴ = 1/16.

Q. Value of 25^(1/2)?

Ans: √25 = 5.

Q. Value of 27^(2/3)?

Ans: (3³)^(2/3) = 3² = 9.

Q. Value of (3²)³?

Ans: 3⁶ = 729.

Q. Value of 4^(−1/2)?

Ans: 1/4^(1/2) = 1/2.

Q. Value of 2³ × 5³?

Ans: (2×5)³ = 1000.

Q. (1/2)⁻³ = ?

Ans: 2³ = 8.

Q. Value of 81^(3/4)?

Ans: (3⁴)^(3/4) = 3³ = 27.

Q. If 3ˣ = 81, find x.

Ans: 81 = 3⁴ ⇒ x = 4.

Multiple Choice (MCQ)

Q. 2⁰ + 3⁰ = ?
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) 2 (d) 5

Ans: (c) 1 + 1 = 2.

Q. (64)^(1/6) = ?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 8 (d) 32

Ans: (a) 64 = 2⁶ ⇒ 2.

Q. aᵐ × aⁿ = ?
(a) aᵐⁿ (b) aᵐ⁻ⁿ (c) aᵐ⁺ⁿ (d) (2a)ᵐ⁺ⁿ

Ans: (c) aᵐ⁺ⁿ.

Q. (16)^(−3/4) = ?
(a) 8 (b) 1/8 (c) 1/12 (d) 12

Ans: (b) 16^(3/4)=8, invert to get 1/8.

Q. (5^(1/2))⁴ = ?
(a) 5² (b) 25 (c) 10 (d) both a and b

Ans: (d) 5^(4/2) = 5² = 25.

Long Answer

Q. Simplify: (2^(1/3) × 2^(1/4)) ÷ 2^(1/12).

Ans: Exponent: 1/3 + 1/4 − 1/12 = (4+3−1)/12 = 1/2 ⇒ √2.

Q. Prove: 1/(1+xᵃ⁻ᵇ) + 1/(1+xᵇ⁻ᵃ) = 1.

Ans: Multiply numerator and denominator of the second term by xᵃ⁻ᵇ: xᵃ⁻ᵇ/(xᵃ⁻ᵇ+1). Adding gives (1+xᵃ⁻ᵇ)/(1+xᵃ⁻ᵇ) = 1. ∎

Q. Evaluate: (2⁻¹ + 3⁻¹)⁻¹.

Ans: 2⁻¹+3⁻¹ = 1/2+1/3 = 5/6; its reciprocal = 6/5.

Q. Solve: 3^(2x−1) = 27.

Ans: 27 = 3³ ⇒ 2x−1 = 3 ⇒ x = 2.
