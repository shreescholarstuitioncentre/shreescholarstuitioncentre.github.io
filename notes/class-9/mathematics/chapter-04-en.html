✏️ Class 9 Maths · Chapter 4: Algebraic Identities (UP Board)

📌 Introduction: An identity is an equation true for all values of the variables (an equation holds only for some values). Identities make multiplication, factorisation and big calculations quick.

1. Main Identities

No.Identity
1(a+b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
2(a−b)² = a² − 2ab + b²
3a² − b² = (a+b)(a−b)
4(x+a)(x+b) = x² + (a+b)x + ab
5(a+b+c)² = a²+b²+c²+2ab+2bc+2ca
6(a+b)³ = a³+b³+3ab(a+b)
7(a−b)³ = a³−b³−3ab(a−b)
8a³+b³ = (a+b)(a²−ab+b²)
9a³−b³ = (a−b)(a²+ab+b²)
10a³+b³+c³−3abc = (a+b+c)(a²+b²+c²−ab−bc−ca)
Special: If a+b+c = 0 then a³+b³+c³ = 3abc.

2. Animated Diagrams (Area Method)

Figure 1: (a+b)²

A square of side a+b splits into four parts: a², ab, ab, b². Their sum = a²+2ab+b².

Figure 2: (x+a)(x+b)

A rectangle of length (x+a) and width (x+b): x² + ax + bx + ab = x² + (a+b)x + ab.

3. Solved Examples

Expand: (3x + 2y)²
Step 1: Use (a+b)² with a = 3x, b = 2y.
Step 2: a² = 9x², 2ab = 2×3x×2y = 12xy, b² = 4y².
Answer: 9x² + 12xy + 4y²
Evaluate: 103²
Step 1: 103 = 100 + 3.
Step 2: (100+3)² = 10000 + 600 + 9 = 10609.
Evaluate: 99 × 101
Step 1: = (100−1)(100+1).
Step 2: = 100² − 1² = 10000 − 1 = 9999.
Find: If a+b = 10 and ab = 21, find a²+b².
Step 1: a²+b² = (a+b)² − 2ab.
Step 2: = 100 − 42 = 58.
Factorise: x² + 7x + 12
Step 1: Find two numbers with sum 7 and product 12: 3 and 4.
Step 2: x² + 3x + 4x + 12 = x(x+3) + 4(x+3)
Answer: (x+3)(x+4)
Expand: (2a − 3b)³
Step 1: In (a−b)³ = a³ − 3a²b + 3ab² − b³ put a→2a, b→3b.
Step 2: 8a³ − 3(4a²)(3b) + 3(2a)(9b²) − 27b³
Answer: 8a³ − 36a²b + 54ab² − 27b³
Factorise: 8x³ + 27y³
Step 1: = (2x)³ + (3y)³ ⇒ use a³+b³.
Step 2: (2x+3y)(4x² − 6xy + 9y²).
Answer: (2x+3y)(4x² − 6xy + 9y²)
Hard: If x + 1/x = 5, find x³ + 1/x³.
Step 1: (x+1/x)³ = x³ + 1/x³ + 3(x+1/x).
Step 2: 125 = x³+1/x³ + 15.
Answer: 110
Hard: Evaluate 28³ + (−15)³ + (−13)³.
Step 1: 28 − 15 − 13 = 0, so a³+b³+c³ = 3abc.
Step 2: 3 × 28 × (−15) × (−13) = 3 × 28 × 195 = 16380.

4. Q & A (click to see the answer)

Short Answer

Q. Formula of (a+b)²?

Ans: a² + 2ab + b².

Q. 101² = ?

Ans: (100+1)² = 10000+200+1 = 10201.

Q. 98 × 102 = ?

Ans: (100−2)(100+2) = 10000−4 = 9996.

Q. (x+2)(x+3) = ?

Ans: x² + 5x + 6.

Q. Factors of a² − b²?

Ans: (a+b)(a−b).

Q. Formula of (a−b)³?

Ans: a³ − 3a²b + 3ab² − b³.

Q. If a+b+c = 0, a³+b³+c³ = ?

Ans: 3abc.

Q. Factors of 8x³ − 1?

Ans: (2x−1)(4x²+2x+1).

Q. (2x+3)² = ?

Ans: 4x² + 12x + 9.

Q. 51² − 49² = ?

Ans: (51+49)(51−49) = 100×2 = 200.

Q. 997 × 1003 = ?

Ans: (1000−3)(1000+3) = 10⁶ − 9 = 999991.

Q. If x−y = 3, xy = 5, find x²+y².

Ans: (x−y)² + 2xy = 9 + 10 = 19.

Multiple Choice (MCQ)

Q. (x+5)(x−5) = ?
(a) x²+25 (b) x²−10x+25 (c) x²−25 (d) x²+10x

Ans: (c) by a²−b², x²−25.

Q. 105² = ?
(a) 11025 (b) 10525 (c) 11005 (d) 10025

Ans: (a) 10000 + 1000 + 25 = 11025.

Q. a³+b³ = (a+b)(?)
(a) a²+ab+b² (b) a²−ab+b² (c) a²−b² (d) (a−b)²

Ans: (b) a²−ab+b².

Q. If a+b = 5, ab = 6, a²+b² = ?
(a) 11 (b) 13 (c) 25 (d) 30

Ans: (b) 25 − 12 = 13.

Q. x³ − 8 = ?
(a) (x−2)(x²−2x+4) (b) (x−2)(x²+2x+4) (c) (x+2)(x²−2x+4) (d) (x−2)³

Ans: (b) by the a³−b³ identity.

Long Answer

Q. Factorise: 27a³ − 64b³.

Ans: (3a)³ − (4b)³ = (3a−4b)(9a² + 12ab + 16b²).

Q. Expand: (2x − y + 3z)².

Ans: 4x² + y² + 9z² − 4xy − 6yz + 12zx (identity 5).

Q. If x − 1/x = 4, find x³ − 1/x³.

Ans: (x−1/x)³ = x³−1/x³ − 3(x−1/x) ⇒ 64 = x³−1/x³ − 12 ⇒ 76.

Q. Prove: a³+b³+c³−3abc = (a+b+c)(a²+b²+c²−ab−bc−ca).

Ans: Multiply a, b, c in turn with the second bracket: a³+ab²+ac²−a²b−abc−a²c, and similar terms for b and c; adding, the mixed terms cancel leaving a³+b³+c³−3abc. ∎
