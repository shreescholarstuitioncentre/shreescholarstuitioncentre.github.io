Class 10 Chitrakala - Chapter 5: Technical / Geometric Art (Pravaidhik Kala) - Notes

1. Introduction

Technical (Geometric) Art is drawing with instruments (ruler, compass, set-squares, protractor, divider, pencil) using geometric rules so that every figure is accurate, neat and measurable. It is the base of engineering drawing, architecture, design, rangoli, jaali and carpet patterns. Basic elements: point, line, angle, triangle, circle, polygon.

2. Instruments & their use

  • HB/2H pencil - thin, light construction lines.
  • Compass - arcs and circles.
  • Set-squares (30-60-90, 45-45-90) - 30,45,60,90 degree lines, parallels, perpendiculars.
  • Protractor - measures/draws angles (0-180).
  • Divider - transfers or divides lengths.
  • Scale/Ruler - straight lines and measuring.

3. Types of Lines

Horizontal (sleeping), Vertical (standing), Oblique (slanting), Curved, Parallel (never meet), Perpendicular (meet at 90 degree), Zig-zag, Wavy. Angles: acute (<90), right (=90), obtuse (>90, <180), straight (180), reflex (>180).

4. Bisecting a Line

Steps: 1) Draw line AB. 2) Take compass opening more than half of AB. 3) Centre A, draw arcs above and below. 4) With same opening, centre B, cut them at P and Q. 5) Join PQ; it meets AB at M. AM = MB and PQ is perpendicular to AB.

5. Bisecting an Angle

Steps: 1) Draw angle AOB. 2) Centre O, draw an arc cutting arms at C and D. 3) Centres C and D, equal arcs cutting at R. 4) Join OR - it divides the angle into two equal parts.

6. Regular Hexagon in a Circle

Steps: 1) Draw circle, centre O. 2) Mark A on circle. 3) Radius = side of hexagon: with compass on radius, centre A cut circle at B, then from B to C, D, E, F. 4) Join A-B-C-D-E-F. Six equal sides, each angle 120 degree. Used in honeycomb, nuts and floor designs.

7. Equilateral Triangle & Other Constructions

Draw base AB. With centre A and B, radius AB, cut arcs at C. Join AC, BC: all sides equal, all angles 60. Perpendicular at a point: cut equal arcs both sides of the point, then bisect that segment. Parallel lines: slide a set-square along a ruler. Square: erect 90 degree at both ends of base, cut equal heights, join.

8. Importance & Precautions

Gives accuracy, base for design/architecture/engineering. Keep pencil sharp, use light lines, keep compass tight, never rub paper hard, mark points clearly with letters, keep drawing clean.

Short Questions & Answers

Q1. Define technical art.
Art made with geometric instruments and rules for accurate, measurable drawings.
Q2. Name four drawing instruments.
Compass, divider, set-square, protractor (also scale, pencil).
Q3. What is a perpendicular bisector?
A line that cuts a segment into two equal parts at 90 degree.
Q4. How many degrees is each angle of a regular hexagon?
120 degree; sum of angles = 720 degree.
Q5. What is the side of a hexagon in a circle?
Equal to the radius of the circle.
Q6. Why are construction lines drawn light?
So they can be erased and final figure stands out.
Q7. Difference between acute and obtuse angle?
Acute is less than 90 degree; obtuse is between 90 and 180 degree.
Q8. Uses of set-squares?
Draw 30, 45, 60, 90 degree angles, parallel and perpendicular lines.

Q4. Write the importance of technical art in daily life.
