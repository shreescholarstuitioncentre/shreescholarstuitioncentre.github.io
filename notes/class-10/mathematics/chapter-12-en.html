2. Combination of Solids

When two solids are joined, the new solid's volume = sum of volumes, but its total surface area ≠ sum of the TSAs: the faces that are joined are hidden. So add only the visible curved/flat surfaces.

A cone sits on a hemisphere of the same radius r. The joined circular base is hidden, so TSA = CSA of cone + CSA of hemisphere = πrl + 2πr². Volume = ⅓πr²h + ⅔πr³. Examples: toys, capsules, tent (cylinder + cone), ice-cream cone, test tube.

3. Conversion of a Solid into Another Shape

When a solid is melted and recast, its volume stays the same, though its shape and surface area change. So Volume of old solid = Volume of new solid (or n × volume of one small solid).

Typical uses: number of small spheres = V(big)/V(small); water flowing through a pipe: Volume = (cross-section area) × (speed) × (time); rise of water level when an object is dropped in a vessel: volume of object = base area × rise.

4. Frustum of a Cone

If a small cone is cut off from the top of a cone by a plane parallel to the base, the remaining bottom part is a frustum. It has two circular ends: radii r₁ (top) and r₂ (bottom) , height h and slant height l. Buckets, glasses and lampshades have this shape.

Volume = ⅓πh(r₁² + r₁r₂ + r₂²)
Slant height l = √(h² + (r₂ − r₁)²)
Curved surface area = πl(r₁ + r₂)
Total surface area = πl(r₁ + r₂) + πr₁² + πr₂²

Q & A – Practice

(Take π = 22/7 unless stated.)

MCQs (NCERT / Balaji / Rajeev / UP Board style)

Total surface area of a cube of side 5 cm is (a) 25 cm² (b) 100 cm² (c) 150 cm² (d) 125 cm²
(c) 150 cm²: 6 × 25.
Volume of a cone of radius 7 cm and height 6 cm is (a) 154 cm³ (b) 308 cm³ (c) 924 cm³ (d) 462 cm³
(b) 308 cm³: ⅓ × 22/7 × 49 × 6.
Volume of a sphere of radius 3 cm is (a) 12π (b) 36π (c) 27π (d) 108π cm³

(b) 3πr²: CSA 2πr² + base πr².

Short Answer

Find CSA and volume of a cylinder of radius 7 cm and height 10 cm.
CSA = 2 × 22/7 × 7 × 10 = 440 cm². Volume = 22/7 × 49 × 10 = 1540 cm³.
A cone has radius 7 cm and slant height 25 cm. Find its height, volume and CSA.
h = √(625 − 49) = 24 cm. Volume = ⅓ × 22/7 × 49 × 24 = 1232 cm³. CSA = 22/7 × 7 × 25 = 550 cm².
Find the surface area and volume of a sphere of radius 21 cm.
SA = 4 × 22/7 × 441 = 5544 cm². V = (4/3) × 22/7 × 9261 = 38808 cm³.
A metallic sphere of radius 4.2 cm is melted and recast into a cylinder of radius 6 cm. Find the height of the cylinder.
(4/3)π(4.2)³ = π(6)²h ⇒ 98.784 = 36h ⇒ h = 2.744 cm.
Find the volume of a hemisphere of radius 21 cm.
⅔ × 22/7 × 9261 = 19404 cm³.

Long Answer

A toy is a cone mounted on a hemisphere of radius 3.5 cm. The total height of the toy is 15.5 cm. Find its total surface area.
Cone height = 15.5 − 3.5 = 12 cm. l = √(12² + 3.5²) = √156.25 = 12.5 cm. CSA cone = 22/7 × 3.5 × 12.5 = 137.5 cm². CSA hemisphere = 2 × 22/7 × 3.5² = 77 cm². TSA = 214.5 cm².
A solid toy is a hemisphere surmounted by a cone of height 2 cm and base radius 1 cm (hemisphere radius 1 cm). Find its volume.
Cone = ⅓π(1)²(2) = 2π/3. Hemisphere = ⅔π(1)³ = 2π/3. Total = 4π/3 ≈ 4.19 cm³.
A bucket is in the form of a frustum of height 24 cm with radii 15 cm (top) and 5 cm (bottom). Find its capacity and the metal sheet needed.
Capacity = ⅓πh(r₁² + r₁r₂ + r₂²) = ⅓ × 22/7 × 24 × (225 + 75 + 25) = 8171.43 cm³ (≈ 8.17 litres). l = √(24² + 10²) = 26 cm. CSA = 22/7 × 26 × 20 = 1634.29 cm²; bottom = 22/7 × 25 = 78.57 cm². Sheet = 1712.86 cm².
A cuboid of 4.4 m × 2.6 m × 1 m is melted and recast into a hollow cylindrical pipe of internal radius 30 cm and thickness 5 cm. Find the length of the pipe.
Volume = 440 × 260 × 100 = 11,440,000 cm³. Outer R = 35, inner r = 30. Cross-section = 22/7 × (1225 − 900) = 22/7 × 325. Length = 11,440,000 × 7/(22 × 325) = 11,200 cm = 112 m.
A medicine capsule is a cylinder with two hemispherical ends. Its total length is 14 mm and diameter 5 mm. Find its surface area.
r = 2.5 mm. Cylinder length = 14 − 5 = 9 mm. SA = 2πr × 9 + 4πr² = 22/7 × (45 + 25) = 220 mm².
