Chapter 11: Sewing Kit and Garment Making Art

Class 10 · Home Science · UP Board / NCERT style notes

1. Meaning and Importance of Sewing

Sewing is joining pieces of cloth with needle and thread (by hand or machine) to make garments and household articles.

  • Saves money, since stitching at home costs less than buying readymade.
  • Garments fit properly and match personal choice.
  • Old clothes can be altered and reused, so nothing is wasted.
  • Can be a hobby and a source of income (tailoring, boutique).
  • Develops creativity, patience and skill.

2. Sewing Kit (Sewing Box)

A sewing kit keeps all small tools together in one box so that they are always ready and safe.

3. Parts and Care of a Sewing Machine

PartFunction
Spool pinHolds the thread reel
Tension discControls tightness of upper thread
Take-up leverMoves thread up and down to form a stitch
NeedleCarries thread through cloth
Presser footHolds cloth flat while stitching
Throat plate and feed dogTeeth that push cloth forward
Bobbin and bobbin caseLower thread supply
Hand wheelTurned by hand to start and move the needle
Stitch regulatorSets stitch length

Care: oil regularly at oiling points, clean lint with a brush, cover after use, do not run it without cloth, keep the needle up and lift the presser foot after work, use correct needle and thread, and keep it in a dry place.

5. Taking Body Measurements

  • Chest/bust: around fullest part, under arms.
  • Waist: around natural waistline.
  • Hip: around fullest part of hips.
  • Shoulder: across back from one shoulder tip to the other.
  • Sleeve length: shoulder tip to wrist (or required length).
  • Garment length: shoulder or waist to desired length.
  • Neck: around the base of the neck.

Rules: take measurements over thin clothes; keep tape flat and snug, not tight; stand straight; note them in a notebook; measure again to check.

6. Fabric Preparation and Cutting

  • Grain: warp (lengthwise threads) and weft (crosswise threads). Cut and stitch along the straight grain so the garment hangs well.
  • Selvedge: firm finished edge of fabric; bias is the diagonal direction that stretches.
  • Shrink and straighten the fabric (wash/soak and press) before cutting.
  • Make or choose a paper pattern; pin it on cloth along the grain.
  • Allow seam allowance (about 1–1.5 cm) and hem allowance.
  • Mark with chalk, then cut with long smooth strokes of sharp shears.

7. Basic Hand Stitches and Seams

StitchUse
RunningPlain seams, gathers; stitches of equal size
Tacking (basting)Long temporary stitches; removed later
Back stitchStrong stitch, like a machine stitch
HemmingFixing folded edge or hem with slanting stitches
Slip stitchAlmost invisible hem
OvercastingOver raw edge to stop fraying
Blanket / buttonholeDecorative edge and buttonholes

Seams: plain seam (most common), French seam (raw edges hidden, for fine fabrics), flat-fell seam (strong, for jeans and shirts).

8. Steps of Garment Making and Precautions

  1. Take correct measurements.
  2. Prepare paper pattern or draft.
  3. Prepare and cut the cloth.
  4. Mark with chalk or tracing wheel.
  5. Tack the pieces and try for fitting.
  6. Stitch on machine and finish seams.
  7. Make hems, add buttons, hooks, zips.
  8. Press with iron and check the garment.

Precautions: use sharp scissors carefully; keep needles and pins in a pin cushion; do not put pins in the mouth; keep good light; sit straight; press at each stage.

Short Answer Questions

Q1. What is a sewing kit?

A box that keeps all small sewing tools together and ready for use.

Q2. Write the use of tailor's chalk.

It is used for marking lines on cloth before cutting and stitching.

Q3. What is seam allowance?

Extra cloth (about 1–1.5 cm) left beyond the stitching line for the seam.

Q4. What is the function of feed dog?

Its teeth push the cloth forward as each stitch is made.

Q5. Why does the upper thread break?

Because of wrong threading, tight tension or a blunt needle.

Q6. Name four basic hand stitches.

Running, tacking, back stitch and hemming.

Long Answer Questions

Q1. Describe the tools of a sewing kit and their uses.

The kit holds measuring tape for measurements, shears for cutting, small scissors for threads, pinking shears for zig-zag edges, needles and thread for sewing, pins and pin cushion for holding pieces, thimble to protect the finger, chalk for marking, seam ripper for undoing stitches, tracing wheel for marks and an iron for pressing.

Q2. Name the parts of a sewing machine and explain care of the machine.

Main parts are spool pin, tension disc, take-up lever, needle, presser foot, throat plate and feed dog, bobbin case, hand wheel and stitch regulator. Care: oil at oiling points, clean lint, cover after use, do not run without cloth, keep the needle up, use right needle and thread and keep in a dry place.

Q3. Explain the steps of garment making.

Take measurements, prepare a paper pattern, straighten and cut the cloth with seam allowance, mark with chalk, tack the pieces and check fit, stitch on the machine and finish the seams, make hems and add fastenings, then press the finished garment with an iron.
