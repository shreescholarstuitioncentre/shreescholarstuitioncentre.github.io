Footprints Without Feet ✍️

Class 10 · English · Footprints Without Feet (Supplementary) · Author: H.G. Wells (adapted from The Invisible Man) · Chapter 24 as per your list

1. Story in short

GenreScience fiction / adventure.

Griffin is a brilliant scientist who finds a way to make himself invisible. He first tests it, then uses it on himself.

Invisibility looks like a gift, but it soon becomes a curse. He is hungry and cold in the London winter, cannot walk freely, and has to steal clothes.

He covers his face with bandages, wears dark goggles and a hat, and moves to the village of Iping, where he rents a room at an inn.

The villagers become suspicious of the stranger who hides his face, refuses to be disturbed, and works with strange bottles at night.

Things get worse: strange things happen in the village, and footprints appear in the snow with no feet to make them (the title!).

His secret is revealed and he is chased by police and villagers. He becomes angry and violent, and the invisible man turns dangerous.

In the end he is hunted down, and as he dies his body slowly becomes visible again.

4. How invisibility works (animated)

Idea: an object is seen because light reflects, refracts or is absorbed by it. If it does none of these, light passes straight through, so it cannot be seen.

5. The clue: footprints in snow

6. Themes & message

  • Misuse of science brings ruin, because power without responsibility is dangerous.
  • Invisibility is a gift but also a curse: he is lonely and cut off from society.
  • Society fears what it does not understand.
  • Selfishness and anger lead to downfall.
  • Even an invisible person cannot hide his traces (footprints).

8. Important Q & A

Q1. Why did Griffin cover himself with bandages and goggles?

To hide his invisibility and look like a normal (injured) person among the villagers.

Q2. Why is the title apt?

The only proof of his presence was footprints made by unseen feet.

Q3. How did the villagers react?

First with curiosity, then fear and anger, and they tried to capture him.

Q4. What is the message?

Science without ethics is dangerous; selfishness leads to ruin.
