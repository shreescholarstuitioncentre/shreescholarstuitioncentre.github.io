2. Summary in Simple Words

Gavin Maxwell was a naturalist who loved wild animals. He brought home a young otter from the marshes of southern Iraq, whom he named Mijbil, or 'Mij' for short. Mij was a playful, curious and lively otter, and Maxwell took him all the way to London by aeroplane in a box, which Mij found very uncomfortable.

During the journey Mij grew restless. He was a young animal who was used to freedom, not to being locked up in a box. Maxwell worried that he might escape or become frightened, and had to take special care of him during the flight and the journey. His job was to calm Mij and keep him safe.

In London, Mij settled in Maxwell's flat. Maxwell watched how the otter explored every corner with great curiosity, how he moved like a flowing creature, and how he loved to play. Mij was fascinated by water. He liked to play with a ball, roll and dive, and splash in the bath. He could even work out how to turn on the tap, to the surprise of Maxwell.

Maxwell learned that an otter must be understood on its own terms. He was patient, did not force Mij and gave him freedom, and a close bond developed between them. The lesson shows the author's love for Mij and his respect for a wild animal, and it shows how a human and an animal can become good companions.

    5. Part-wise Explanation

    Part 1 — Meeting Mij

    Maxwell brings a young otter from Iraq and names him Mij. He is a playful and curious animal and the author is deeply fond of him.

    Key point: love for animals and wildlife.

    Part 2 — The journey

    Mij is carried in a box on an aeroplane. He is not used to captivity and becomes restless. Maxwell has to be careful and patient.

    Key point: wild animals need freedom and care.

    Part 3 — Life in London

    Mij settles into Maxwell's flat and explores everything. His movements are graceful and full of energy.

    Key point: close observation of animal behaviour.

    Part 4 — Mij and water

    Mij's love of water is shown in his play in the bath, with a ball and by turning on taps. Water is the centre of an otter's life.

    Key point: an animal's nature must be understood.

    Part 5 — Friendship

    Maxwell does not force Mij to do anything. His patient treatment creates trust and a lasting companionship.

    Key point: trust and kindness build relationships.

    6. Character Sketches

    Gavin Maxwell

    A caring and patient naturalist. He observes animals closely and respects their nature. He is humorous and affectionate towards Mij, treating him almost like a child. He puts the animal's comfort and freedom first.

    Mijbil (Mij)

    A playful, curious, energetic and intelligent young otter. He loves water, rolling and games. He is restless in captivity but becomes trusting and affectionate when treated kindly.

    7. Themes and Message

    Bond between humans and animals

    Patience and love can build trust between a human and a wild animal.

    Respect for nature

    Animals have their own nature; we should understand and respect it.

    Curiosity and playfulness

    Mij's curiosity and playfulness make the story lively.

    Responsibility

    Having a pet means caring for its needs and freedom.

    Moral

    Love, patience and understanding can make friends even of wild creatures.

    11. Long Answer Questions

    Q1. Describe Mij's journey from Iraq to London.
    Maxwell brought Mij, a young otter, from the marshes of southern Iraq. He travelled with him by aeroplane in a box. Mij was used to freedom and became restless. Maxwell took great care to keep him calm and safe throughout the journey. After reaching London, Mij settled in his flat and began to explore.
    Q2. Describe Mij's playful behaviour in London.
    In London, Mij was curious about everything. He loved water and played in the bathtub, diving and rolling. He enjoyed playing with a ball and even learned to turn on the taps. His movements were quick and graceful. His lively nature made the home cheerful and showed how intelligent otters are.
    Q3. What does the story tell us about Maxwell's relationship with Mij?
    Maxwell treated Mij with love, patience and respect. He studied the otter's habits and did not force him to do anything. As a result, Mij trusted him and the two became close companions. The story shows that kindness and understanding can create a strong bond between humans and animals.
