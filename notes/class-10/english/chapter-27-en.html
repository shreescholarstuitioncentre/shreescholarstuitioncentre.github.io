Bholi

Class 10 • Interesting Prose • Chapter 27 (Supplementary) • by K. A. Abbas

✏️ About the Author & Story

K. A. (Khwaja Ahmad) Abbas (1914–1987) was an Indian writer, journalist and filmmaker. Bholi is a story of a shy, stammering girl who, through education and a kind teacher, finds the courage to say “No” to injustice and the dowry system.

📖 Summary (Step by Step)

  1. Sulochana, youngest child of Ramlal, the village Tehsildar, was called Bholi (simpleton). As a baby she fell and hurt her head, so she was slow to speak and stammered. Smallpox later left her face pockmarked.
  2. Her elder sisters were healthy and married well. Bholi was neglected by the family and seen as a burden.
  3. When a new school opened in the village, her mother insisted that Bholi be sent. Her father agreed, thinking education might help her find a husband.
  4. On the first day Bholi was terrified. The kind teacher spoke gently, gave her the proper name Sulochana and told her not to be afraid.
  5. Slowly Bholi became confident; she learnt to read, write and speak without fear. The teacher’s patience changed her life.
  6. Years later a wealthy middle-aged shopkeeper, Bishambar Nath, was chosen as her groom. The marriage was arranged because of her looks and her family’s fear of finding a match.
  7. At the wedding the groom’s greed and demand for dowry upset everyone. Bholi, now brave, refused to marry him and called him greedy and a coward.
  8. Her father admitted she was right and supported her. Bholi decided to become a teacher in the village school and take care of her parents.

📈 Plot Diagram (Animated)

🦋 Transformation: Before: stammering, scared, silent → After: confident, independent, outspoken. Education was the bridge.

💡 Themes

  • Power of education: it gives confidence and independence.
  • Women empowerment: Bholi chooses her own path.
  • Evil of dowry: greedy demands are rejected firmly.
  • Importance of kindness: the teacher’s love healed Bholi.
  • Courage and self-respect: saying NO is sometimes right.

❓ Important Q & A

Q1. Why was Bholi called a simpleton?
She was slow to speak, stammered and was seen as dull, so everyone called her Bholi.
Q2. How did the teacher help her?
She gave Bholi love, patience and encouragement, so Bholi overcame her fear.
Q3. Why did Bholi refuse the marriage?
The groom was greedy and demanded dowry, so she refused to marry such a man.
Q4. What is the message of the story?
Education makes a girl bold and independent; dowry should be rejected.
