Class 10 Maths – Ch 8: Introduction to Trigonometry

1. Trigonometric Ratios

Trigonometry = measurement of triangles. In a right triangle, for an acute angle θ, the sides are named: Hypotenuse (opposite the right angle, longest), Opposite (facing θ), Adjacent (next to θ, not the hypotenuse).

In the 3-4-5 triangle the angle θ is at P. The side facing θ is 3 (opposite), the side touching it is 4 (adjacent), and the hypotenuse is 5.

sin θ = Opposite/Hypotenuse   cos θ = Adjacent/Hypotenuse   tan θ = Opposite/Adjacent
cosec θ = 1/sin θ   sec θ = 1/cos θ   cot θ = 1/tan θ
tan θ = sin θ / cos θ,   cot θ = cos θ / sin θ
Remember: SOH-CAH-TOA (Sine = Opp/Hyp, Cosine = Adj/Hyp, Tangent = Opp/Adj).
  • Ratios depend only on the angle, not on the size of the triangle (similar triangles).
  • sin θ and cos θ are always between 0 and 1 (for acute angles); sec θ and cosec θ are always ≥ 1; tan θ and cot θ can be any positive value.

2. Ratios of Specific Angles

Two special triangles give all values. The 45° triangle has sides 1, 1, √2. Cutting an equilateral triangle of side 2 in half gives the 30°-60°-90° triangle with sides 1, √3, 2.

3. Complementary Angles

sin(90° − θ) = cos θ   cos(90° − θ) = sin θ
tan(90° − θ) = cot θ   cot(90° − θ) = tan θ
sec(90° − θ) = cosec θ   cosec(90° − θ) = sec θ
Eg sin 25° = cos 65°; tan 26° × tan 64° = tan 26° × cot 26° = 1.

4. Trigonometric Identities

On the unit circle the hypotenuse is 1, so the base is cos θ and the height is sin θ. By Pythagoras: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1. (Here 0.6² + 0.8² = 0.36 + 0.64 = 1.)

sin²θ + cos²θ = 1
1 + tan²θ = sec²θ (divide first by cos²θ)
1 + cot²θ = cosec²θ (divide first by sin²θ)
Proving identities: start with the more complicated side, convert everything into sin and cos, simplify using the identities until it equals the other side.

Long Answer

If 3 cot A = 4, check whether (1 − tan²A)/(1 + tan²A) = cos²A − sin²A.
tan A = 3/4. LHS = (1 − 9/16)/(1 + 9/16) = (7/16)/(25/16) = 7/25. Hypotenuse = 5 ⇒ cos A = 4/5, sin A = 3/5. RHS = 16/25 − 9/25 = 7/25. LHS = RHS ✓.
Prove (sin θ + cosec θ)² + (cos θ + sec θ)² = 7 + tan²θ + cot²θ.
LHS = sin²θ + 2 + cosec²θ + cos²θ + 2 + sec²θ = (sin²θ + cos²θ) + 4 + (1 + cot²θ) + (1 + tan²θ) = 1 + 4 + 2 + tan²θ + cot²θ = 7 + tan²θ + cot²θ. ∎
Prove sec A (1 − sin A)(sec A + tan A) = 1.
LHS = (1/cos A)(1 − sin A)(1 + sin A)/cos A = (1 − sin²A)/cos²A = cos²A/cos²A = 1. ∎
Prove (cosec θ − cot θ)² = (1 − cos θ)/(1 + cos θ).
LHS = ((1 − cos θ)/sin θ)² = (1 − cos θ)²/(1 − cos²θ) = (1 − cos θ)²/[(1 − cos θ)(1 + cos θ)] = (1 − cos θ)/(1 + cos θ). ∎

cos 65° = sin 25°, sin 65° = cos 25°. Expression = sin²25° + cos²25° = 1.
