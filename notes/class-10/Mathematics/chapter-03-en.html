Class 10 Maths – Ch 3: Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables

1. Linear Equation in Two Variables

Form: ax + by + c = 0 (a, b not both zero). Its graph is a straight line; every point on the line is a solution.
A pair of linear equations: a₁x + b₁y + c₁ = 0 and a₂x + b₂y + c₂ = 0. A solution must satisfy both.

2. Graphical Method

Steps: (1) Make a table of 2–3 points for each equation. (2) Plot and join them to get two lines. (3) The point of intersection is the solution.

Watch the two lines being drawn: x + y = 5 passes through (0,5), (5,0), (2,3); x − y = 1 passes through (1,0), (0,−1), (3,2). They meet at (3, 2): 3 + 2 = 5 ✓, 3 − 2 = 1 ✓.

Three possible cases

RatiosLinesSolutionsType
a₁/a₂ ≠ b₁/b₂IntersectingExactly oneConsistent
a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ ≠ c₁/c₂ParallelNoneInconsistent
a₁/a₂ = b₁/b₂ = c₁/c₂CoincidentInfinitely manyDependent consistent

3. Algebraic Methods

(a) Substitution: express one variable from one equation, put it in the other.
Eg x + y = 14, x − y = 4: x = 14 − y → 14 − y − y = 4 → y = 5, x = 9.
(b) Elimination: make coefficients of one variable equal, add/subtract to eliminate it.
Eg 3x + 2y = 11, 2x + 3y = 4 → ×3 and ×2: 9x + 6y = 33, 4x + 6y = 8 → 5x = 25 → x = 5, y = −2.
(c) Cross-multiplication:
x/(b₁c₂ − b₂c₁) = y/(c₁a₂ − c₂a₁) = 1/(a₁b₂ − a₂b₁), valid when a₁b₂ − a₂b₁ ≠ 0.

4. Equations Reducible to Linear Form

Put u = 1/x, v = 1/y. Eg 2/x + 3/y = 13 becomes 2u + 3v = 13. Solve for u, v, then x = 1/u, y = 1/v.

5. Word Problems – How to Solve

  • Read, assume two variables x and y for the unknowns.
  • Translate each statement into an equation.
  • Solve by any method, then check with the problem.
  • Common types: ages, numbers/digits, fractions, speed–distance–time (upstream x − y, downstream x + y), geometry, money.

Q & A – Practice

MCQs (NCERT / Balaji / Rajeev / UP Board style)

The pair x + 2y − 4 = 0 and 2x + 4y − 12 = 0 has (a) unique solution (b) no solution (c) infinitely many (d) two solutions
(b) no solution. 1/2 = 2/4 ≠ 4/12, so lines are parallel.
kx + 2y = 5 and 3x + y = 1 have a unique solution if (a) k = 6 (b) k ≠ 6 (c) k = 3 (d) k ≠ 3
(b) k ≠ 6, since k/3 ≠ 2.
Solution of x + y = 5 and x − y = 1 is (a) (2,3) (b) (3,2) (c) (4,1) (d) (1,4)
(b) (3, 2).
2x + 3y = 5 and 4x + 6y = 10 are (a) inconsistent (b) consistent dependent (c) intersecting (d) none
(b) consistent dependent: 2/4 = 3/6 = 5/10.
Graph of x = 3 is a line (a) parallel to x-axis (b) parallel to y-axis (c) through origin (d) none
(b) parallel to y-axis.
If x = a, y = b solves 2x + 3y = 13 and 5x − 2y = 4, then a + b = (a) 4 (b) 5 (c) 6 (d) 7
(b) 5. Solving gives x = 2, y = 3.

Short Answer

Solve x + y = 14, x − y = 4 by substitution.
x = 14 − y; 14 − 2y = 4; y = 5; x = 9.
Solve 3x + 2y = 11, 2x + 3y = 4 by elimination.
x = 5, y = −2. Check: 2(5) + 3(−2) = 4 ✓.
For what k does 2x + 3y = 7, (k − 1)x + (k + 2)y = 3k have infinitely many solutions?
2/(k−1) = 3/(k+2) ⇒ 2k + 4 = 3k − 3 ⇒ k = 7. Check: 7/21 = 1/3 ✓.
Is 2x − 3y = 8, 4x − 6y = 9 consistent?
1/2 = 1/2 ≠ 8/9: parallel lines, no solution, inconsistent.
Solve 2/x + 3/y = 13, 5/x − 4/y = −2.
u = 1/x, v = 1/y: 2u + 3v = 13, 5u − 4v = −2 ⇒ u = 2, v = 3. x = 1/2, y = 1/3.

Long Answer

Solve 2x + 3y = 12 and 3x − y = 7 by cross-multiplication.
a₁=2, b₁=3, c₁=−12; a₂=3, b₂=−1, c₂=−7. b₁c₂−b₂c₁ = −33; c₁a₂−c₂a₁ = −22; a₁b₂−a₂b₁ = −11. x = −33/−11 = 3, y = −22/−11 = 2.
Ten years ago a father was 12 times as old as his son; ten years hence he will be 2 times as old. Find their ages.
Father x, son y. x − 10 = 12(y − 10) ⇒ x − 12y = −110. x + 10 = 2(y + 10) ⇒ x − 2y = 10. Subtract: −10y = −120 ⇒ y = 12, x = 34. Father 34 yrs, son 12 yrs.
A boat goes 30 km upstream and 44 km downstream in 10 h, and 40 km upstream and 55 km downstream in 13 h. Find boat and stream speeds.
u = 1/(x−y), v = 1/(x+y): 30u + 44v = 10; 40u + 55v = 13 ⇒ v = 1/11, u = 1/5. x − y = 5, x + y = 11. Boat 8 km/h, stream 3 km/h.
Sum of numerator and denominator of a fraction is 12. If denominator is increased by 3, fraction becomes 1/2. Find it.
x + y = 12; x/(y+3) = 1/2 ⇒ 2x = y + 3. Solving: x = 5, y = 7. Fraction 5/7.
Solve graphically x + y = 5, x − y = 1.
Table 1: (0,5), (2,3), (5,0). Table 2: (1,0), (3,2), (0,−1). Lines meet at (3, 2), so x = 3, y = 2.
