Class 10 Maths – Ch 2: Polynomials

1. Polynomial: Basic Terms

Polynomial in x: p(x) = aₙxⁿ + … + a₁x + a₀, where powers are whole numbers and aᵢ are real numbers. Degree = highest power of x.
Eg 3x² − 5x + 2 (degree 2). √x + 1, 1/x are NOT polynomials.
DegreeNameGeneral formMax zeroes
1Linearax + b1
2Quadraticax² + bx + c2
3Cubicax³ + bx² + cx + d3
Zero of a polynomial: k is a zero if p(k) = 0. Eg p(x) = x² − 3x + 2, p(1) = 1 − 3 + 2 = 0, so 1 is a zero. A polynomial of degree n has at most n zeroes. Constant polynomial has no zero (non-zero constant); zero polynomial has degree undefined.

2. Geometrical Meaning of Zeroes

Zeroes of p(x) are the x-coordinates of the points where the graph y = p(x) meets the x-axis.

Linear: graph is a straight line; it meets the x-axis at exactly one point, x = −b/a.

Watch the parabola draw itself. It cuts the x-axis at x = −1 and x = 3, so these are the zeroes. Check: (−1)² − 2(−1) − 3 = 0 and 3² − 6 − 3 = 0 ✓. Factorised: (x + 1)(x − 3).

Three possibilities for a quadratic

  • Graph cuts x-axis at 2 points → 2 distinct real zeroes.
  • Graph just touches the x-axis → 1 zero (repeated).
  • Graph lies wholly above (or below) the axis → no real zero.

Cubic polynomial

A cubic can cut the axis at 1, 2 or 3 points, never more than 3. Here x³ − 4x = x(x − 2)(x + 2).

3. Relation between Zeroes and Coefficients

Quadratic ax² + bx + c, zeroes α, β:
α + β = −b/a   and   αβ = c/a
Cubic ax³ + bx² + cx + d, zeroes α, β, γ:
α + β + γ = −b/a;   αβ + βγ + γα = c/a;   αβγ = −d/a
Example: x² − 2x − 3 (a=1, b=−2, c=−3) has zeroes −1, 3. Sum = 2 = −(−2)/1 ✓. Product = −3 = c/a ✓.
Forming a polynomial: if sum = S and product = P, then polynomial = k[x² − Sx + P]. Eg S = 4, P = 3 → x² − 4x + 3.

Useful identities: α² + β² = (α+β)² − 2αβ; 1/α + 1/β = (α+β)/αβ; (α−β)² = (α+β)² − 4αβ.

4. Division Algorithm for Polynomials

For polynomials p(x) and g(x) (g ≠ 0) there exist q(x) and r(x) with p(x) = g(x)·q(x) + r(x), where r(x) = 0 or deg r(x) < deg g(x).
Steps: (1) Write both in descending powers. (2) Divide the leading term of dividend by leading term of divisor → first quotient term. (3) Multiply it with divisor, subtract. (4) Repeat on the remainder until its degree < divisor's degree. Uses: finding other zeroes when some zeroes are known.

Q & A – Practice

MCQs (NCERT / Balaji / Rajeev / UP Board style)

The degree of 5x³ − 4x² + 7 is (a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 5 (d) 7
(b) 3, highest power of x.
Number of zeroes of a quadratic polynomial is at most (a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
(b) 2.
Zeroes of x² − 9 are (a) 3 only (b) −3 only (c) ±3 (d) 9
(c) ±3, since (x−3)(x+3).
If sum of zeroes is 5 and product is 6, the polynomial is (a) x²+5x+6 (b) x²−5x+6 (c) x²−6x+5 (d) x²+6x+5
(b) x² − 5x + 6.
If graph of p(x) does not meet the x-axis, p(x) has (a) 1 zero (b) 2 zeroes (c) no real zero (d) 3 zeroes
(c) no real zero.
Product of zeroes of ax³+bx²+cx+d is (a) −b/a (b) c/a (c) −d/a (d) d/a
(c) −d/a.

Short Answer

Find zeroes of x² − 2x − 8 and verify the relation.
x²−2x−8=(x−4)(x+2); zeroes 4, −2. Sum 2 = −(−2)/1 ✓; product −8 = c/a ✓.
Form a quadratic polynomial whose sum and product of zeroes are −3 and 2.
x² − (−3)x + 2 = x² + 3x + 2.
If α, β are zeroes of 2x² − 7x + 3, find α² + β².
α+β=7/2, αβ=3/2. α²+β²=(7/2)²−2(3/2)=49/4−3=37/4.
If 2 is a zero of x² − 3x + k, find k.
4 − 6 + k = 0 ⇒ k = 2.
Find zeroes of 6x² − 3 − 7x.
6x²−7x−3=(3x+1)(2x−3); zeroes −1/3, 3/2. Sum 7/6 ✓, product −1/2 ✓.

Long Answer

Divide 3x³ + x² + 2x + 5 by x² + 2x + 1.
3x³÷x² = 3x; 3x(x²+2x+1)=3x³+6x²+3x; subtract → −5x²−x+5. Next −5x²÷x² = −5; −5(x²+2x+1)=−5x²−10x−5; subtract → 9x+10 (degree 1 < 2, stop). Quotient 3x−5, remainder 9x+10. Check: (x²+2x+1)(3x−5)+9x+10 = 3x³+x²+2x+5 ✓.
Verify that 1/2, 1, −2 are zeroes of 2x³ + x² − 5x + 2 and verify the relations.
p(1/2)=1/4+1/4−5/2+2=0; p(1)=0; p(−2)=−16+4+10+2=0 ✓. Sum = −1/2 = −b/a ✓. Pair sum = 1/2 −2 −1 = −5/2 = c/a ✓. Product = −1 = −d/a ✓.
Find a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes sum to 1/4 and product is −1.
k[x² − x/4 − 1]; with k = 4: 4x² − x − 4.
Given √2 and −√2 are zeroes of 2x⁴ − 3x³ − 3x² + 6x − 2, find all zeroes.
(x−√2)(x+√2)=x²−2 is a factor. Dividing: quotient 2x²−3x+1, remainder 0. 2x²−3x+1=(2x−1)(x−1). Zeroes: √2, −√2, 1/2, 1.
If sum of zeroes of kx² + 2x + 3k equals their product, find k.
Sum = −2/k, product = 3k/k = 3. −2/k = 3 ⇒ k = −2/3.
