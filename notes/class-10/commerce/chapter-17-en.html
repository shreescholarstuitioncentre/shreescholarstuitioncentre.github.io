Chapter 17: Commercial Banks and Co-operative Banks

Class 10 – Commerce (Banking)

1. Commercial Bank – Meaning

A commercial bank = an institution that accepts deposits and gives loans with the aim of earning profit. It meets the banking needs of trade, industry and the public.

Easy example: opening an account or taking a loan from SBI or HDFC ✔

2. Types of Commercial Banks (animated diagram)

📝 Explanation: By ownership, commercial banks are public, private and foreign. Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) are another type.

3. Functions of Commercial Banks

FunctionExamples

General utilityDrafts, lockers, ATM, credit cards

4. Co-operative Bank – Meaning

A co-operative bank = a bank formed by members for mutual help and service. It follows "one member – one vote". It gives cheap loans to farmers, artisans and small traders.

5. Three-tier Co-operative Structure (animated diagram)

📝 Explanation: At the bottom, village primary agricultural credit societies lend to farmers. Above them are district and state co-operative banks. Loans flow down (orange dot) and deposits flow up (green dot).

6. Features of Co-operative Banks

  • Service motive, not only profit
  • Democratic management (one member one vote)
  • Loans at low interest
  • Serve rural and urban areas
  • Limited liability
