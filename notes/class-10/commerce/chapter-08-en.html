Chapter 8: Means of Communication

Class 10 – Commerce (Business Methods)

1. Meaning

Communication = exchange of information, ideas or messages between two or more persons. In business, details of goods, prices, payments and orders travel this way.

Easy example: a shopkeeper phoning a wholesaler to order goods = communication ✔

2. Importance

  • Smooth running and coordination of business
  • Good relations with customers and suppliers
  • Helps in taking correct decisions
  • Saves time and money
  • Raises employee morale

3. Communication Process (animated diagram)

📝 Explanation: The sender prepares a message and sends it through a medium (phone, letter, e-mail). The receiver understands it and replies – this is feedback. Without feedback, communication is incomplete. Orange dot = journey of the message.

6. Postal & Telecom Services

  • Registered Post – safe, with receipt
  • Speed Post – fast delivery
  • Money Order – sending money
  • VPP / COD – payment collected on delivery
  • Telephone/Mobile, E-mail, Internet – instant contact

7. Barriers to Communication

  • Language difficulty
  • Noise / technical faults
  • Unclear message
  • Inattention or wrong interpretation

8. Qualities of Good Communication (5 C)

Clear, Concise, Correct, Complete, Courteous
