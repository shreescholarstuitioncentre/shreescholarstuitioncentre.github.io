Chapter 9: Time and Labour-Saving Appliances

Class 10 – Commerce (Business Methods)

1. Meaning

Time and labour-saving appliances = office equipment that completes work in less time, with less effort and more accuracy.

Easy example: adding 100 figures by hand takes hours, but with a calculator/computer only minutes ✔

2. Need and Importance

  • Increases speed of work
  • Fewer mistakes
  • Saves labour and cost
  • Better quality and neatness
  • More work with fewer staff

3. Time Saving (animated diagram)

📝 Explanation: For the same job, the red bar (manual) fills slowly, while the green bar (machine) fills quickly. So machines save both time and effort.
