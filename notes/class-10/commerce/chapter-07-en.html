Chapter 7: Indexing (Business Methods)

Class 10 – Commerce

1. Meaning

Indexing = arranging records/documents in a systematic list (alphabetical, numerical or subject-wise) so that they can be found quickly when needed.

Easy example: a book's "contents page" or your phone's "contact list" = an index ✔

2. Importance / Advantages

  • Saves time – files found fast
  • Accuracy and efficiency in work
  • Less risk of losing records
  • New staff can find records easily
  • Saves labour and cost

3. Essentials of a Good Indexing System

  • Simple – easy to understand
  • Economical – low cost
  • Flexible – new names can be added
  • Quick & accurate search
  • Compact and clear
  • Keeps confidentiality

5. Types of Index

TypeDescriptionBest for
Page (Book) IndexBound book; separate page for each letterSmall offices, few names
Card IndexSeparate cards in a drawer with guide cardsLarge numbers, frequent changes
Strip IndexStrips fixed in a frameDirectory-type lists
Visible IndexEdges of cards always visibleRecords needed at a glance
