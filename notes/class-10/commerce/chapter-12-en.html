Chapter 12: Retail Trade

Class 10 – Commerce (Business Methods)

1. Meaning

Retail trade = selling goods in small quantities directly to the final consumer. The retailer is the last link in the chain of distribution.

Easy example: a neighbourhood grocer selling 1 kg of sugar = retail trade ✔

2. Features

  • Sales in small quantities
  • Customers = consumers
  • Can start with little capital
  • Higher profit per unit
  • Cash or credit sales
  • Direct contact with customers

3. Role of the Retailer (animated diagram)

📝 Explanation: The retailer buys from suppliers (orange dots) and sells in small lots to many consumers (green dots). Consumers get many goods at one place.

4. Types of Retail Trade (animated diagram)

📝 Explanation: Retail trade can be grouped into three – sellers who move around, small fixed shops and large-scale stores.
