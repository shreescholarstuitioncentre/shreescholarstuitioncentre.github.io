Chapter 18: State Bank of India (SBI)

Class 10 – Commerce (Banking)

1. Introduction

The State Bank of India (SBI) is India's largest public-sector commercial bank. Its head office is in Mumbai, with thousands of branches in India and abroad.

Easy example: the SBI branch and ATM you see in villages and cities alike ✔

2. Origin and Growth (animated diagram)

📝 Explanation: The three Presidency Banks of Bengal, Bombay and Madras joined in 1921 to form the Imperial Bank (orange dots). In 1955 it became the SBI (green dot). In 2017 the associate banks were merged into SBI.

4. Functions of SBI (animated diagram)

📝 Explanation: Besides normal banking, SBI is banker to the government and agent of the RBI, and helps in rural development and financial inclusion.

5. Main Functions

  • Accepting deposits and giving loans
  • Cheques, drafts, online banking and ATM services
  • Banker to the government (tax collection, pension payment)
  • Acts as RBI's agent where RBI has no branch
  • Loans to agriculture, small industry and rural areas
  • Foreign exchange business

6. Importance

  • Banking facilities in rural and remote areas
  • Promotes savings and capital formation
  • Implements government schemes
  • Creates employment
