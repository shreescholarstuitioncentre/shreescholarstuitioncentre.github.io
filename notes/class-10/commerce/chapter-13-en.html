Chapter 13: Invoice and Statement of Account

Class 10 – Commerce (Business Methods)

1. Invoice – Meaning

An invoice = a document prepared by the seller when goods are sold, showing details of goods, quantity, price and terms. It is sent to the buyer along with the goods.

Easy example: the "bill" you get when you buy goods from a shop = invoice ✔

2. Contents of an Invoice

  • Invoice number and date
  • Names and addresses of seller and buyer
  • Description, quantity and rate of goods
  • Trade discount
  • Total amount (with tax)
  • Terms of payment and transport
  • E&OE (Errors and Omissions Excepted)

4. Statement of Account – Meaning

A statement sent by the seller to the buyer at the end of a fixed period (usually a month) showing all transactions (sales, returns, payments) and the balance due.

📝 Explanation: Goods and invoice go against an order, the buyer pays, and at month end the statement is sent to remind of any balance. Orange dot = journey of documents.

7. Invoice vs Statement of Account

BasisInvoiceStatement of Account
ContentOne saleAll transactions of the period
SentWith goodsAt the end of the period
PurposeTells price of goodsTells amount due
⚡ Quick Revision: Invoice = bill of a sale | Statement = monthly account | Balance = opening balance + sales − payments/returns
