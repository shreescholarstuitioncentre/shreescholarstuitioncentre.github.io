Chapter 10: Home Trade / Inland Trade

Class 10 – Commerce (Business Methods)

1. Meaning

Home trade = buying and selling of goods and services within the boundaries of a country. Also called inland / internal trade.

Easy example: goods made in a Kanpur factory sold in a Delhi shop = home trade ✔

2. Types

  • Wholesale trade – selling in large quantities (to retailers)
  • Retail trade – selling in small quantities directly to consumers

6. Services of the Wholesaler

  • To manufacturer: bulk orders, storage, sharing risk, market information
  • To retailer: credit, variety of goods, advice

7. Services of the Retailer

  • Goods available near the consumer's home
  • Credit and home delivery
  • Passes consumer tastes to the producer

8. Types of Retail Trade
