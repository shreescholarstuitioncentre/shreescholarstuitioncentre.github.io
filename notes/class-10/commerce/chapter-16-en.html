Chapter 16: Central Bank / Reserve Bank of India

Class 10 – Commerce (Banking)

1. Central Bank – Meaning

A central bank = the apex bank of a country's banking and monetary system. It issues notes, controls other banks and acts as banker to the government. It works for the nation's welfare, not for profit.

Easy example: as a principal controls all of a school, the RBI controls all banks ✔

2. Reserve Bank of India (RBI) – Introduction

  • Established: 1 April 1935
  • Nationalised: 1 January 1949
  • Headquarters: Mumbai
  • Head: Governor

3. Role of the RBI (animated diagram)

📝 Explanation: The RBI is banker to the government (orange dot), lends to banks when needed (green dot) and issues notes for the public (blue dot).

📝 Explanation: When bank rate/repo rate rises, loans become costly and less money flows in the market (inflation falls). When rates fall, loans become cheap. Under CRR and SLR, banks must keep part of their deposits aside.
