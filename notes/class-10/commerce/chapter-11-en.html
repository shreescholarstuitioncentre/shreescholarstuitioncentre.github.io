Chapter 11: Wholesale Trade

Class 10 – Commerce (Business Methods)

1. Meaning

Wholesale trade = buying goods in large quantities from producers and selling them to retailers or industries (not directly to consumers). The person doing this is a wholesaler.

Easy example: a trader buys hundreds of soap cartons from a factory and sells them to grocery shops = wholesale trade ✔

2. Features

  • Large-scale dealings
  • Needs more capital
  • Customers = retailers / industries
  • Mostly credit sales
  • Needs big warehouses
  • Low profit per unit, high total profit

📝 Explanation: Many producers send goods to the wholesaler (orange dots). He stores them in a warehouse and supplies many retailers (green dots). So the producer need not visit every shop.

📝 Explanation: Merchant wholesalers buy and own the goods; agents only arrange deals and earn commission.

5. Functions of a Wholesaler

  • Collects goods and buys in bulk
  • Storage, grading and packing
  • Arranges transport
  • Finance / credit
  • Bears risk (price fall, spoilage)
