1. What is Cyber Crime?

Cyber crime is any illegal activity done using a computer, mobile or the Internet – either as a tool to commit the crime or as the target of it. The study of protecting systems and data is called cyber security.
  • Computer as target: hacking, malware, DoS attack.
  • Computer as tool: phishing, online fraud, cyberbullying, identity theft.
Cyber crimeMeaning
HackingIllegally entering a computer or network to steal or change data
PhishingFake emails, SMS or websites that steal passwords and card details
Identity theftUsing someone’s personal data (Aadhaar, PAN, card) to cheat
Online fraudFake shops, lottery or job offers, UPI/OTP scams
Cyberbullying / stalkingHarassing, threatening or following a person online
RansomwareLocks your files and demands money to unlock them
DoS / DDoS attackFloods a website with requests so that it stops working
PiracyIllegal copying of software, movies or music
Deepfake / fake newsFake videos, images or rumours made to mislead

Black hatBreaks in to steal or damage – illegal

Red flags

  • Urgent threats (“account blocked in 1 hour!”).
  • Unknown sender or strange email address.
  • Spelling mistakes; fake-looking link.
  • Asks for OTP, PIN, CVV or password.

C. Strong Password

  • At least 8 characters with capital, small letters, digits and symbols.
  • Do not use name, birth date or “12345”. Use a different password for each account.
  • Turn on 2-step verification.

6. If You Become a Victim

  • Stay calm; do not delete messages. Save screenshots as evidence.
  • Change passwords; inform the bank to block cards/accounts.
  • Tell your parents or teacher.
  • Report at cybercrime.gov.in; for money fraud call 1930 quickly.

7. Safe Online Behaviour (Netiquette)

  • Be polite; do not bully or spread rumours.
  • Think before you post; photos stay online for long.
  • Do not share personal details with strangers.
  • Respect copyright; give credit.

8. Remember

  • Cyber crime = crime using computers or the Internet.
  • Malware: virus, worm, trojan, ransomware, spyware.
  • Never share OTP, PIN or password; check links before clicking.
  • Firewall filters traffic; encryption hides data.
  • Report cyber crime: cybercrime.gov.in, helpline 1930.
