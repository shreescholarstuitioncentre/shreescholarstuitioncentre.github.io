1. E-Commerce

E-Commerce (Electronic Commerce) means buying and selling of goods and services over the Internet, along with the online payment of money. Examples: Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra, IRCTC ticket booking.

Features

  • Shops are open 24×7; no need to visit a market.
  • Wide choice, easy price comparison, home delivery.
  • Payment is done electronically.
  • Reaches customers all over the world.

4. Online Payment and Safety

MethodHow it works

UPIInstant transfer from bank account using a UPI app/ID or QR code

Safe online shopping

  • Use trusted websites with https and a padlock.
  • Never share your OTP, PIN or CVV with anyone.
  • Beware of phishing (fake links and messages).
  • Avoid public Wi-Fi for payments; keep the invoice.

5. E-Governance

E-Governance (Electronic Governance) is the use of ICT (computers, Internet, mobile) by the government to give services and information to citizens, businesses and departments quickly and transparently.
  • Aim: SMART governance – Simple, Moral, Accountable, Responsive, Transparent.
  • India: National e-Governance Plan (2006) and the Digital India mission (2015).
  • Benefits: less paperwork, less corruption, saves time and travel.

7. Advantages and Limitations of E-Governance

Advantages

  • Services 24×7; saves time, money and travel.
  • Transparency and less corruption.
  • Paperless and faster work.

Limitations

  • Digital divide: not everyone has internet or skills.
  • Risk to privacy and data security.
  • High setup cost; needs trained staff.

9. Remember

  • E-commerce = buying/selling online; models B2B, B2C, C2C, C2B, B2G.
  • Order cycle: browse → cart → payment → shipping → delivery.
  • Never share OTP, PIN or CVV.
  • E-governance = government services through ICT; models G2C, G2B, G2G, G2E.
  • Digital India (2015) promotes e-governance.
