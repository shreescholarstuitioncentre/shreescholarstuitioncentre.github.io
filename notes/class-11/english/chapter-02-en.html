✎ We're Not Afraid to Die… if We Can All Be Together

Class 11 English (Hornbill) · Ch.2 · Gordon Cook & Alan East · UP Board

1. Introduction

A true, first-person adventure story told by the father (Gordon Cook). A family sails round the world on their boat Wavewalker to retrace Captain James Cook's voyage. In a terrible ocean storm they fight for life. Theme: courage, teamwork, family bond, hope.

3. Summary (Detailed)

(a) The Family & Voyage

The narrator (37, businessman), wife Mary, daughter Suzanne (Sue, 13) and son Jonathan (Jon, 6) left Plymouth, England in 1976 on Wavewalker, planning to follow Captain Cook's route round the world. All were fond of sailing.

(b) Storm Begins

Far at sea, on the way to Australia, a gale started. Wind rose, waves grew huge. The narrator lowered sails and put out drag ropes to keep the boat steady, then went below to rest. He felt the danger growing.

(c) The Giant Wave

A giant wave hit, capsizing the boat. Water rushed into the cabin. The boat tilted and the mast and rigging were damaged, the engine failed. Mary was thrown about; Sue was hurt in the cabin. The narrator was flung out and held by his safety line.

(d) Struggle for Survival

Everyone worked: the narrator and Mary pumped out water and made repairs; Sue bore pain silently. Children stayed calm and brave. The boat would not hold long enough to reach land.

(e) Jon's Words

Jon asked, “Daddy, are we going to die?” Then said they were not afraid to die if all of them were together. These words gave the father new strength. Sue also gave a card saying she loved her parents.

(f) Rescue

After days of cold, hunger and exhaustion, a ship spotted them and rescued the family; all survived, though Wavewalker was lost.

8. Long Q&A

L1. Describe the storm and the family's struggle.A gale raised mountainous waves; a giant wave capsized the boat. The engine and mast failed, water flooded in, Sue was hurt. They pumped water, repaired and endured cold, hunger and fatigue for days. Teamwork and courage kept them alive until rescue.
L2. Character sketch of the narrator.He was a brave and responsible father: he prepared the boat for the storm, led repairs, did not give up even when hurt. He was inspired by his son's words and drew on his family's strength, showing leadership and love.
L3. Significance of the title.The title is Jon's statement. It shows that family togetherness removes the fear of death and gives hope; it sums up the chapter's central message.
