✎ The Adventure

Class 11 English (Hornbill) · Ch.5 · Jayant Narlikar · UP Board

1. About the Author

Jayant Vishnu Narlikar (1938–2023) was a renowned Indian astrophysicist, cosmologist and science-fiction writer. In this science-fiction / alternate-history story he blends history, physics and imagination.

2. Animated Diagram: The Two Histories of Panipat (1761)

Labels: Fork at Panipat → two possible histories. Gaitonde, through a strange experience, travels to the second one.

3. Summary (Detailed)

(a) Professor Gaitonde

Professor Gangadharpant Gaitonde was a history professor who was to give a talk on the Third Battle of Panipat. He travels by train; his mind is busy with the lecture and the battle's result.

(b) The Strange Journey

During the journey he finds the surroundings oddly different: station, people, rules and newspapers seem unfamiliar. He is puzzled but believes it is a real place.

(c) The Alternate World

He learns that in this world the Marathas won the Battle of Panipat against Abdali. The East India Company continued and helped Maratha expansion, so Indian history, politics and society are different (no British Raj as in our world).

(d) At the Lecture

When he starts to speak about the Maratha defeat at Panipat, the audience rejects his “wrong” version and he is physically removed from the dais. He now doubts his own sanity.

(e) Rajendra's Explanation

His friend Rajendra (a scientist) tells him: “You neither travelled to the past nor the future. You were in the present experiencing a different world.” Using the idea of parallel worlds / quantum possibilities, he says a different history can exist side by side. Gaitonde's experience was a “fantastic, or more correctly, a catastrophic experience”.

7. Short Q&A

Q1. Who was Professor Gaitonde?A history professor, full name Gangadharpant Gaitonde.
Q2. Which battle did he speak about?The Third Battle of Panipat (1761).
Q3. What did he find different in the alternate world?The Marathas had won Panipat, and the East India Company helped their expansion, changing India's history.
Q4. Why was he removed from the dais?His account of the Maratha defeat contradicted what the people believed in that world.
Q5. Who explained the experience to him?His friend Rajendra.
Q6. What did Rajendra say?That he neither travelled to the past nor the future, but was in the present experiencing a different world.

8. Long Q&A

L1. Describe Gaitonde's adventure.He is a historian going to lecture on Panipat. During the train journey he enters a world where details differ. He learns the Marathas won at Panipat, and when he states the real defeat at the lecture, he is thrown off the stage. Rajendra later explains it as experiencing a parallel world.
L2. Explain Rajendra's idea of the experience.Rajendra says the experience is not time travel. A different possible world exists in the present; Gaitonde glimpsed one where history took another course. It was “catastrophic” because it shook his beliefs about reality.
L3. How does Narlikar mix science and history?He takes a real event (Panipat), imagines a different result (alternate history) and explains it using scientific ideas like parallel universes and quantum possibilities.
