✎ The Ailing Planet: The Green Movement's Role

Class 11 English (Hornbill) · Ch.4 · Nani Palkhivala · UP Board

1. Introduction

An essay / non-fiction prose by Nani Palkhivala, Indian jurist and economist. It warns that Earth is "ailing" because of human actions (pollution, deforestation, overpopulation) and explains why the Green Movement and a holistic, ecological view of life are needed. There are no story characters.

2. Animated Diagram: Cause → Effect → Cure

3. Summary (Detailed)

(a) The Earth is Ailing

Palkhivala says modern life has damaged the planet. Land is eroding, forests are cut, air and water are polluted and the climate is changing. Humans act as if the earth belongs only to them.

(b) The Green Movement

The Green Movement tries to protect nature. It stands for a holistic (whole) and ecological view: every living thing and element is connected, so harming one harms all.

(c) Main Problems

Deforestation – trees cut for fuel and land; in India people cut trees for cooking fuel. Pollution – industries and vehicles. Overpopulation – more people need more resources. Global warming & ozone damage – harmful gases harm climate.

(d) Role & Message

He urges sustainable development: progress that does not destroy resources for future generations. Governments, industries and individuals must act together. Saving Earth is everyone's duty.

7. Short Q&A

Q1. Which movement is Palkhivala talking about?The Green Movement.
Q2. Why is the planet called "ailing"?Because human activities like pollution and deforestation have made the earth unhealthy.
Q3. How do many Indians get cooking fuel?By cutting trees and collecting firewood from forests.
Q4. What is a holistic view?Looking at nature as a connected whole, not as separate parts.
Q5. What is sustainable development?Development that meets present needs without harming future generations.
Q6. Who is Nani Palkhivala?An eminent Indian jurist, economist and thinker.

8. Long Q&A

L1. Explain the title "The Ailing Planet".Like a sick person, Earth shows symptoms: eroded soil, vanishing forests, polluted air and water, warming climate. Human greed caused this illness. The title warns that unless we treat the planet through the Green Movement, it will worsen.
L2. What role does the Green Movement play?It spreads awareness, opposes harmful exploitation, promotes tree planting, clean energy and sustainable development, and urges people and governments to protect nature for future generations.
L3. What is the message of the essay?Humans are part of nature, not its master. We must change habits, limit waste, control population and work together; saving Earth is a shared, urgent duty.
