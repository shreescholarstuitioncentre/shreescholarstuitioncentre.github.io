Handwritten-style notes • UP Board / NCERT

1. Coordinate Axes in 3D

In a plane, 2 axes fix a point. In space we need 3 mutually perpendicular lines meeting at a point O (origin).

  • X'OX = x-axis, Y'OY = y-axis, Z'OZ = z-axis.
  • Positive directions: OX, OY, OZ (right-handed system: turn from OX to OY, a right-handed screw moves along OZ).
  • Origin O = (0,0,0).
Any point on x-axis: (a,0,0) | y-axis: (0,b,0) | z-axis: (0,0,c)

4. Coordinates of a Point

From P draw a perpendicular PQ to the XY-plane, then QA ⟂ OX and QB ⟂ OY. Then x = OA, y = OB, z = PQ, written P(x,y,z).

5. Distance Between Two Points

PQ = √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)² + (z₂−z₁)²]
OP = √(x²+y²+z²)

Proof: Draw a parallelepiped with PQ as diagonal and faces ∥ coordinate planes. Its edges are |x₂−x₁|, |y₂−y₁|, |z₂−z₁|. Right triangle on the base gives (base diagonal)² = Δx²+Δy²; then PQ² = (base diagonal)² + Δz². ∎

Uses: collinearity (a+b=c), isosceles / equilateral / right triangle, square, parallelogram, locus of equidistant points.

Solved Examples (step by step)

EasyFind the distance between (2,3,5) and (4,3,1).Given: P(2,3,5), Q(4,3,1). Formula: PQ=√(Δx²+Δy²+Δz²).
Δx=2, Δy=0, Δz=−4.
PQ=√(4+0+16)=√20=2√5.
EasyIn which octant is (−3,2,5)?x<0, y>0, z>0 ⇒ Octant II.
EasyFind distance of (3,4,5) from the XY-plane and from the x-axis.From XY-plane = |z| = 5. From x-axis = √(y²+z²)=√(16+25)=√41.
MediumShow that (0,7,−10), (1,6,−6), (4,9,−6) form an isosceles right triangle.AB²=1+1+16=18, BC²=9+9+0=18, CA²=16+4+16=36.
AB=BC ⇒ isosceles. AB²+BC²=36=CA² ⇒ right-angled at B. ∎
MediumShow that P(−2,3,5), Q(1,2,3), R(7,0,−1) are collinear.PQ=√(9+1+4)=√14; QR=√(36+4+16)=2√14; PR=√(81+9+36)=3√14.
PQ+QR=PR ⇒ collinear. ∎
HardFind the locus of P equidistant from A(3,4,5) and B(−1,3,−7).PA²=PB²: (x−3)²+(y−4)²+(z−5)² = (x+1)²+(y−3)²+(z+7)².
LHS: x²+y²+z²−6x−8y−10z+50. RHS: x²+y²+z²+2x−6y+14z+59.
Subtract: −8x−2y−24z−9=0 ⇒ 8x+2y+24z+9=0.
HardShow A(0,4,1), B(2,3,−1), C(4,5,0), D(2,6,2) are vertices of a square.AB²=BC²=CD²=DA²=9 (all sides 3). AC²=16+1+1=18, BD²=0+9+9=18.
Equal sides and equal diagonals ⇒ square. ∎
