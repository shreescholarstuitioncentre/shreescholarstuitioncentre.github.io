Class 11 Maths – Ch 19: Statistics – Measures of Dispersion

Handwritten-style notes • UP Board / NCERT

1. Dispersion – Why?

Two data sets can have the same mean but different spread (e.g. 4,5,6 and 1,5,9). A measure of dispersion tells how scattered the observations are. Main measures: Range, Quartile Deviation, Mean Deviation, Standard Deviation.

2. Range

Simplest measure, uses only the two extreme values.

Range = Largest value − Smallest value
Coefficient of range = (L − S)/(L + S)
Grouped data: Range = upper limit of highest class − lower limit of lowest class.

3. Mean Deviation

Average of absolute deviations from a central value a (mean or median).

MD(a) = Σ|xᵢ − a| / n    (ungrouped)
MD(a) = Σ fᵢ|xᵢ − a| / N    (grouped, N = Σf)

For continuous classes take xᵢ = class mark (mid-value). Coefficient of MD = MD / a. Mean deviation is least about the median.

4. Variance and Standard Deviation

Variance σ² = mean of squared deviations from the mean; SD σ = +√variance.

σ² = Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² / n = Σxᵢ²/n − x̄²

Proof: Σ(x−x̄)² = Σx² − 2x̄Σx + n x̄² = Σx² − 2n x̄² + n x̄² = Σx² − n x̄². Divide by n. ∎

Grouped data

σ² = Σfx²/N − (Σfx/N)²
Step-deviation: u=(x−A)/h ⇒ σ² = h²[Σfu²/N − (Σfu/N)²]

Properties

  • Adding a constant to every value: mean changes, SD unchanged.
  • Multiplying every value by k: mean×k, SD×|k|, variance×k².
Coefficient of Variation CV = (σ / x̄) × 100
Smaller CV ⇒ more consistent (more stable) data.

5. Ungrouped Data – Steps

  1. Find mean x̄ (or median).
  2. Find deviations xᵢ − x̄.
  3. MD: take absolute values, add, divide by n.
  4. SD: square deviations, add, divide by n, take root.

6. Grouped Data – Steps

  1. Make a table: class, mid-value x, frequency f.
  2. Find N and mean (use assumed mean A, step h for large numbers).
  3. Compute f|x−x̄| (MD) or fu², fu (SD).
  4. Apply formula; take square root for SD.

Short & Long Questions

Q1. Define dispersion.Spread or scatter of observations around a central value.
Q2. Write the formula of range and coefficient of range.Range=L−S; coefficient=(L−S)/(L+S).
Q3. About which average is MD minimum?Median.
Q4. Define variance and SD.Variance=mean of squared deviations from mean; SD=its positive square root.
Q5. Effect of adding a constant on SD?None. SD is independent of change of origin.
Q6. Effect of multiplying by k?SD becomes |k|σ, variance k²σ².
Q7. Define coefficient of variation.CV=(σ/x̄)×100; used to compare consistency.
Q8. Why is SD preferred over MD? (long)Squares remove signs without absolute values, it is algebraically manageable and used in further statistics (correlation, tests).
Q9. Derive step-deviation formula for variance.Put x=A+hu; x̄=A+hū and x−x̄=h(u−ū) ⇒ σ²=h²σᵤ²=h²[Σfu²/N−(Σfu/N)²].
