Class 12 Maths — Chapter 22: 3D Geometry (Straight Lines and Plane)

1. Coordinates of a Point in Space

P(x, y, z): x, y, z are distances from the YZ, ZX, XY planes

Three mutually perpendicular axes OX, OY, OZ meet at the origin O and divide space into 8 octants. Origin = (0,0,0); a point on the x-axis = (x,0,0); a point in the XY-plane = (x,y,0).

2. Distance, Direction Ratios and Direction Cosines

PQ = √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)² + (z₂−z₁)²]
DRs of PQ: x₂−x₁, y₂−y₁, z₂−z₁
DCs: l = (x₂−x₁)/PQ, m = (y₂−y₁)/PQ, n = (z₂−z₁)/PQ

3. Equation of a Line

Vector: r = a + λb (a = point on the line, b = parallel vector)
Cartesian: (x−x₁)/a = (y−y₁)/b = (z−z₁)/c
Through two points: (x−x₁)/(x₂−x₁) = (y−y₁)/(y₂−y₁) = (z−z₁)/(z₂−z₁)

Here a, b, c are the direction ratios of the line.

4. Parallel, Intersecting and Skew Lines

Parallel: b₁ = λb₂
Skew: not parallel and do not intersect (not coplanar)
Coplanar: [a₂−a₁ b₁ b₂] = 0

Lines in a plane either intersect or are parallel. Skew lines exist only in 3D.

5. Shortest Distance Between Lines

Skew: d = |(a₂−a₁)·(b₁×b₂)| / |b₁×b₂|
Parallel: d = |b×(a₂−a₁)| / |b|
Lines intersect ⇔ d = 0

6. Equation of a Plane

Vector: r·n = q (n = normal)
Cartesian: ax + by + cz + d = 0
Through (x₁,y₁,z₁): a(x−x₁)+b(y−y₁)+c(z−z₁) = 0
Through 3 points: | x−x₁ y−y₁ z−z₁ ; x₂−x₁ y₂−y₁ z₂−z₁ ; x₃−x₁ y₃−y₁ z₃−z₁ | = 0
Intercept form: x/p + y/q + z/r = 1

The coefficients a, b, c of the plane are the direction ratios of its normal.

7. Angles

Two lines: cosθ = |b₁·b₂| / (|b₁||b₂|) (perpendicular: a₁a₂+b₁b₂+c₁c₂ = 0)
Two planes: cosθ = |n₁·n₂| / (|n₁||n₂|)
Line and plane: sinθ = |b·n| / (|b||n|)

8. Distance of a Point from a Plane

D = |ax₁ + by₁ + cz₁ + d| / √(a²+b²+c²)
Vector form: D = |a·n − q| / |n|

Solved Examples (Given / To Find / To Prove / Solution)

Ex 1 (Easy). Distance between P(1,2,3) and Q(3,4,4).
PQ = √(4+4+1) = 3.
Ex 2 (Easy). DRs and DCs of the line PQ.
DRs: 2, 2, 1. DCs: 2/3, 2/3, 1/3.
Ex 3 (Easy). Line through (1,2,3) parallel to 2i+j−2k.
Vector: r = (i+2j+3k) + λ(2i+j−2k).
Cartesian: (x−1)/2 = (y−2)/1 = (z−3)/(−2).
Ex 4 (Easy). Line through A(1,2,3) and B(3,4,4).
DRs of AB: 2, 2, 1.
(x−1)/2 = (y−2)/2 = (z−3)/1.
Ex 5 (Medium). Angle between lines with DRs (1,2,2) and (2,3,6).
b₁·b₂ = 2+6+12 = 20; |b₁| = 3; |b₂| = 7.
cosθ = 20/21 ⇒ θ = cos⁻¹(20/21).
Ex 6 (Hard). Shortest distance between r = (i+j)+λ(2i−j+k) and r = (2i+j−k)+μ(3i−5j+2k).
b₁×b₂ = i(−2+5) − j(4−3) + k(−10+3) = 3i−j−7k; |b₁×b₂| = √59.
a₂−a₁ = i − k; dot = 3 + 0 + 7 = 10.
d = 10/√59.
Ex 7 (Medium). Plane through (1,2,3) with normal 2i+3j−k.
2(x−1) + 3(y−2) − (z−3) = 0 ⇒ 2x + 3y − z − 5 = 0.
Ex 8 (Medium). Plane through A(1,0,0), B(0,1,0), C(0,0,1).
Intercepts 1, 1, 1 ⇒ x/1 + y/1 + z/1 = 1 ⇒ x + y + z = 1.
Ex 9 (Medium). Angle between planes 2x+y−2z = 5 and 3x−6y−2z = 7.
n₁·n₂ = 6−6+4 = 4; |n₁| = 3; |n₂| = 7.
cosθ = 4/21.
Ex 10 (Medium). Distance of (1,2,3) from 2x−y+2z+3 = 0.
D = |2−2+6+3| / √(4+1+4) = 9/3 = 3.
Ex 11 (Proof). Show (x−5)/7 = (y+2)/(−5) = z/1 and x/1 = y/2 = z/3 are perpendicular.
a₁a₂ + b₁b₂ + c₁c₂ = 7(1) + (−5)(2) + 1(3) = 7−10+3 = 0 ∎

Short & Long Questions

Q1. How many octants are there?
Eight.
Q2. Distance of P(x,y,z) from the origin?
√(x²+y²+z²).
Q3. Vector equation of a line?
r = a + λb.
Q4. What are skew lines?
Lines that are neither parallel nor intersecting.
Q5. Condition for lines to intersect?
Shortest distance = 0.
Q6. Vector equation of a plane?
r·n = q.
Q7. Angle between two planes is the angle between …?
Their normals.
Q8. Distance of a point from a plane?
|ax₁+by₁+cz₁+d| / √(a²+b²+c²).
