Chapter 10: Tangents and Normals

UP Board / NCERT style notes · Step-by-step · Animated diagrams

1. Slope of a Tangent

A tangent to a curve at a point P touches the curve at P and has the same direction as the curve there. Its slope equals the value of the derivative at P. If the tangent makes angle θ with the positive x-axis, slope = tan θ.

Slope of tangent at P(x₁, y₁): m = [dy/dx](x₁, y₁)
Parametric curve x = φ(t), y = ψ(t): m = (dy/dt)/(dx/dt) at t = t₁.
Implicit curve: differentiate both sides w.r.t. x, solve for dy/dx, then put (x₁, y₁).

2. Equation of a Tangent to a Curve

Tangent at P(x₁, y₁): y − y₁ = m (x − x₁), where m = [dy/dx] at P.

Special cases: if m = 0 the tangent is y = y₁ (horizontal). If dy/dx is infinite (vertical tangent) the tangent is x = x₁.

3. Slope of a Normal & 4. Equation of a Normal

The normal at P is the line through P perpendicular to the tangent at P. Since the product of slopes of perpendicular lines is −1:

Slope of normal = −1/m = −1/[dy/dx]
Normal at P(x₁, y₁): y − y₁ = −(1/m)(x − x₁)  or  (x − x₁) + m (y − y₁) = 0.
If m = 0: normal is x = x₁. If tangent is vertical: normal is y = y₁.

Fig. 1: At P the tangent (red) and normal (green) are perpendicular (90°).

5. Tangents Parallel to the Coordinate Axes

Parallel to x-axis ⇔ slope = 0 ⇔ dy/dx = 0.
Parallel to y-axis ⇔ slope infinite ⇔ dx/dy = 0 (i.e. 1/(dy/dx) = 0).
Parallel to line y = mx + c: dy/dx = m. Perpendicular to it: dy/dx = −1/m.

6. Normals Parallel to the Coordinate Axes

Normal ∥ x-axis ⇔ tangent ∥ y-axis (dx/dy = 0).
Normal ∥ y-axis ⇔ tangent ∥ x-axis (dy/dx = 0).

Fig. 2: On a circle, the tangent is horizontal at the top and bottom and vertical at the left and right. Every normal passes through the centre.

7. Tangents and Normals at a Given Point

Steps: ① Check the point lies on the curve. ② Find dy/dx. ③ Substitute the point to get m. ④ Tangent: y − y₁ = m(x − x₁). ⑤ Normal: y − y₁ = −(1/m)(x − x₁). ⑥ Simplify into the form ax + by + c = 0.

8. Problems Based on Tangents and Normals

Lengths (bonus, for UP Board): with m = dy/dx at (x, y):
Sub-tangent = |y/m|; Sub-normal = |y·m|; Length of tangent = |(y/m)√(1+m²)|; Length of normal = |y√(1+m²)|.
If the point is not given: take a general point (x₁, y₁) on the curve, use the given condition (parallel/perpendicular/passing through a point) to get equations, then solve.

Solved Examples (step by step)

Easy Ex 1. Find the slope of the tangent to y = x² − 3x + 2 at (1, 0).
dy/dx = 2x − 3. At x = 1: m = 2 − 3 = −1.
Easy Ex 2. Find the tangent and normal to y = x²/2 at (2, 2).
dy/dx = x ⇒ m = 2.Tangent: y − 2 = 2(x − 2) ⇒ 2x − y − 2 = 0.Normal slope = −1/2: y − 2 = −½(x − 2) ⇒ x + 2y − 6 = 0. (Fig. 1)
Easy Ex 3. Tangent and normal to y = x³ at (1, 1).
dy/dx = 3x² = 3 at x = 1.Tangent: y − 1 = 3(x − 1) ⇒ 3x − y − 2 = 0.Normal: y − 1 = −⅓(x − 1) ⇒ x + 3y − 4 = 0.
Medium Ex 4. Tangent and normal to x² + y² = 25 at (3, 4).
2x + 2y dy/dx = 0 ⇒ dy/dx = −x/y = −3/4.Tangent: y − 4 = −¾(x − 3) ⇒ 3x + 4y = 25.Normal slope 4/3: y − 4 = (4/3)(x − 3) ⇒ 4x − 3y = 0 (passes through the centre).
Medium Ex 5. For x = at², y = 2at, find tangent and normal at t = 1.
dy/dt = 2a, dx/dt = 2at ⇒ dy/dx = 1/t = 1. Point = (a, 2a).Tangent: y − 2a = x − a ⇒ x − y + a = 0.Normal: y − 2a = −(x − a) ⇒ x + y − 3a = 0.
Medium Ex 6. Find points on y = x³ − 3x where the tangent is parallel to the x-axis.
dy/dx = 3x² − 3 = 0 ⇒ x = ±1.y(1) = −2, y(−1) = 2. Points: (1, −2) and (−1, 2); tangents y = −2 and y = 2.
Medium Ex 7. For (x − 1)² + y² = 4 find points where tangent is parallel to (a) y-axis, (b) x-axis.
2(x − 1) + 2y y′ = 0 ⇒ y′ = −(x − 1)/y.(a) Parallel to y-axis: y = 0 ⇒ (x − 1)² = 4 ⇒ x = 3 or −1: points (3, 0), (−1, 0).(b) Parallel to x-axis: x = 1 ⇒ y² = 4: points (1, 2), (1, −2).
Medium Ex 8. Find the tangent to y = x² − 2x + 7 parallel to 2x − y + 9 = 0.
Line slope = 2. dy/dx = 2x − 2 = 2 ⇒ x = 2; y = 4 − 4 + 7 = 7.Tangent: y − 7 = 2(x − 2) ⇒ 2x − y + 3 = 0.
Hard Ex 9. Find the normals to y = x² that pass through (0, 3/2).
Point (t, t²), slope of tangent 2t, normal: y − t² = −(1/2t)(x − t).Put (0, 3/2): 3/2 − t² = −(1/2t)(−t) = 1/2 ⇒ t² = 1 ⇒ t = ±1.t = 1: x + 2y − 3 = 0; t = −1: x − 2y + 3 = 0.
Hard Ex 10. Show that for xy = c² the triangle formed by any tangent and the axes has constant area.
y = c²/x ⇒ dy/dx = −y₁/x₁ at (x₁, y₁). Tangent: y − y₁ = −(y₁/x₁)(x − x₁).Put y = 0: x = 2x₁. Put x = 0: y = 2y₁.Area = ½ · 2x₁ · 2y₁ = 2x₁y₁ = 2c², a constant. ∎
Hard Ex 11. Find a, b so that y² = ax³ + b touches y = 4x − 5 at (2, 3).
Point on curve: 9 = 8a + b … (i).2y y′ = 3ax² ⇒ y′ = 3ax²/(2y) = 12a/6 = 2a at (2, 3). Slope of line = 4 ⇒ a = 2.From (i): b = 9 − 16 = −7. So a = 2, b = −7.
Medium Ex 12. For y = x² at (1, 1) find sub-tangent, sub-normal and lengths of tangent and normal.
m = 2, y = 1.Sub-tangent = |y/m| = 1/2; sub-normal = |y m| = 2.Tangent length = (1/2)√5 = √5/2; normal length = 1·√5 = √5.

Q & A Practice (tap to open)

Short Questions

Q1. Write the equation of the tangent at (x₁, y₁).y − y₁ = (dy/dx)(x₁, y₁) (x − x₁).
Q2. What is the slope of the normal?−1/(dy/dx) at the point, since normal ⟂ tangent.
Q3. When is the tangent parallel to the x-axis?When dy/dx = 0.
Q4. When is the tangent parallel to the y-axis?When dx/dy = 0 (dy/dx not finite).
Q5. Slope of the tangent to y = x² at x = 3?2x = 6.
Q6. If the tangent is vertical, what is the normal?Horizontal: y = y₁.

Long Questions

Q7. Find the tangent and normal to y² = 4ax at (at², 2at).2y y′ = 4a ⇒ y′ = 2a/y = 1/t. Tangent: ty = x + at². Normal slope −t: y − 2at = −t(x − at²) ⇒ y = −tx + 2at + at³.
Q8. Show that the tangent to x² + y² = r² at (x₁, y₁) is xx₁ + yy₁ = r².y′ = −x₁/y₁. Tangent: y − y₁ = −(x₁/y₁)(x − x₁) ⇒ yy₁ − y₁² = −xx₁ + x₁² ⇒ xx₁ + yy₁ = x₁² + y₁² = r². ∎
Q9. Find the tangent to y = x³ − x that is perpendicular to the line x + 2y = 0.Line slope −1/2, so tangent slope 2: 3x² − 1 = 2 ⇒ x = ±1. Points (1, 0), (−1, 0). Tangents: y = 2(x − 1) and y = 2(x + 1).
