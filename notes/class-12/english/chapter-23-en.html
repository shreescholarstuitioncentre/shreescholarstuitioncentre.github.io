1. NOTICE

A notice is a formal written announcement for a group of people (students, residents, staff). It is written in an impersonal style, in a box, within 50 words.

Points to remember

  • Write the issuing body's name (school / society) at the top.
  • Write the word NOTICE in capitals.
  • Date (below the heading, on the left) and a short, catchy Heading.
  • Body covers the 5 W's: What, When, Where, Who, Why, with contact detail if needed.
  • End with the writer's name, designation and signature. No "Dear" or "Yours".
  • Use passive voice, e.g. "All students are informed…".

ST. MARY'S INTER COLLEGE, LUCKNOW
NOTICE
12 March 2026

All students are informed that the Annual Sports Day will be held on 20 March 2026 at 9 a.m. in the school ground. Interested students must give their names to the sports teacher by 15 March. Parents are cordially invited.
Rohan Verma
Sports Captain

2. INVITATION

An invitation is a written request to attend an event. Formal (printed, third person, e.g. wedding, inauguration) and Informal (to friends, first person, personal tone).

Parts of a formal invitation

  1. Host's name / "Mr. & Mrs. … request the pleasure of your company"
  2. Name of the guest
  3. Occasion
  4. Date, Time, Venue
  5. R.S.V.P. / contact
Mr. & Mrs. A. K. Singh① Host
request the pleasure of the company of Mr. …② Guest
on the wedding of their daughter③ Occasion
on 5 Dec 2026 at 7 p.m.④ Date & time
Venue: Grand Lawn, Kanpur⑤ Venue
R.S.V.P.: 98xxxxxx01⑥ Reply

Reply to an invitation: Accepting — express thanks and confirm attendance. Declining — express regret with the reason.

4. REPORT WRITING

A report is a factual account of an event that has already happened, written in the past tense, passive voice, third person, within 120 words.

Heading (catchy)① Title
By-line: Reporter's name② By-line
Place, Date③ Place & date
Opening: what, where, when④ Para 1
Details, chief guest, events⑤ Para 2
Result / conclusion⑥ Para 3
Sample:
BLOOD DONATION CAMP HELD AT SCHOOL
By Neha Gupta
Lucknow, 14 Feb: A blood donation camp was organised at St. Mary's Inter College yesterday. The camp was inaugurated by the Principal at 10 a.m. Nearly 80 students and teachers donated blood. Doctors from the City Hospital supervised it. The camp ended at 4 p.m. with a vote of thanks.

5. SHORT Q&A (click to see answer)

Q1. What is a notice?A notice is a formal announcement given in writing to inform a group of people about an event or matter.
Q2. What is the word limit of a notice?50 words.
Q3. Which tense is used in a report?Past tense (passive voice mostly).
Q4. Difference between "Yours faithfully" and "Yours sincerely"?"Yours faithfully" is used when the receiver is unknown (Sir/Madam); "Yours sincerely" when we know the name.
Q5. What is R.S.V.P.?French "Répondez s'il vous plaît" meaning "please reply".
