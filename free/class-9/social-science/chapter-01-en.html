History

1. The French Revolution

  • 1789: Bastille stormed on 14 July; Louis XVI was king, Estates General summoned in May 1789.
  • Three estates: clergy, nobility, Third Estate (peasants, workers, middle class). Third Estate bore taxes like taille and tithe.
  • Ideas: liberty, equality, fraternity; Rousseau, Montesquieu, Locke. Declaration of the Rights of Man and Citizen (1789).
  • 1792 republic; Louis XVI guillotined 1793; Reign of Terror under Robespierre; Napoleon took power 1799.

2. Socialism in Europe and the Russian Revolution

  • Liberals, radicals, conservatives; socialists wanted collective ownership; Marx and Engels.
  • 1905 revolution; February Revolution 1917 ended Tsar Nicholas II's rule.
  • October Revolution 1917: Lenin and Bolsheviks seized power; April Theses.
  • Civil war (Reds vs Whites); Collectivisation under Stalin; USSR formed 1922.

3. Nazism and the Rise of Hitler

  • Germany's defeat in WWI; Treaty of Versailles (1919) and Weimar Republic's weakness.
  • Great Depression (1929) helped Hitler; he became Chancellor in 1933; Enabling Act gave dictatorial power.
  • Racial ideology: Jews persecuted; Holocaust; Gestapo, SS and concentration camps.

4. Forest Society and Colonialism

  • Deforestation: railways, ships, agriculture expansion, plantations (tea, coffee, rubber).
  • Dietrich Brandis first Inspector General of Forests; Indian Forest Act 1865 and 1878; reserved forests.
  • Bastar rebellion 1910; Samin movement in Java; Kalang community.

5. Pastoralists in the Modern World

  • Pastoralists: Gujjar Bakarwals (J&K), Gaddis (Himachal), Dhangars (Maharashtra), Maasai (Africa).
  • Colonial rule: Waste Land Rules, Forest Acts, Criminal Tribes Act 1871, grazing tax.
  • Maasai lost land to colonial borders and game reserves; drought hit herds.
Geography

6. India – Size and Location

  • Lies in northern and eastern hemispheres; latitudes 8°4′N–37°6′N; longitudes 68°7′E–97°25′E.
  • Tropic of Cancer (23°30′N) divides India into two halves. Standard Meridian: 82°30′E (IST).
  • Area 3.28 million sq km (7th largest); about 2.4% of world area. Land boundary 15,200 km; coast 7,516.6 km. Suez Canal shortened sea route.

7. Physical Features of India

  • Himalayas (Himadri, Himachal, Shiwaliks), Northern Plains, Peninsular Plateau, Indian Desert, Coastal Plains, Islands.
  • Highest peak Kanchenjunga (India); Mt Everest in Nepal. Northern plains: bhabar, terai, bhangar, khadar.
  • Deccan Plateau is older, bounded by Western and Eastern Ghats; Western Ghats higher (Anai Mudi).

8. Drainage

  • Himalayan rivers (Indus, Ganga, Brahmaputra) are perennial; Peninsular rivers are seasonal.
  • Ganga: Gangotri → Devprayag (Alaknanda + Bhagirathi). Brahmaputra is Tsangpo in Tibet.
  • West-flowing: Narmada and Tapi (rift valley) → estuaries. East-flowing: Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri → deltas.

9. Climate

  • Tropical monsoon climate. Factors: latitude, altitude, pressure, winds, distance from sea, relief.
  • Four seasons: cold weather, hot weather, advancing monsoon (June–Sept), retreating monsoon (Oct–Nov).
  • Loo, mango showers, western disturbances; monsoon is the unifying bond.

10. Natural Vegetation and Wildlife

  • Forest types: tropical evergreen, deciduous, thorn, montane, mangrove.
  • Wildlife: Project Tiger (1973), Project Elephant; Gir lion, one-horned rhino, Asiatic lion.
  • Conservation: national parks, sanctuaries, biosphere reserves.

11. Population

  • Census 2011: 121 crore; second most populous country. Most populous state: Uttar Pradesh.
  • Density 382 per sq km; sex ratio 943; literacy 74%.
  • Adolescents 10–19 years; National Population Policy 2000.
Political Science (Civics)

12. What Is Democracy? Why Democracy?

  • Democracy is rule by the people; features: free and fair elections, one person one vote, rule of law, rights.
  • Arguments for: accountable government, improves quality of decisions, resolves conflicts, dignity of citizens.
  • Broader meaning: Abraham Lincoln – government of the people, by the people, for the people.

13. Constitutional Design

  • South Africa: apartheid; Mandela; 1996 Constitution. Constitution = rules, guarantee, vision.
  • Indian Constituent Assembly: Dr B.R. Ambedkar (Drafting Committee), Rajendra Prasad (President). Adopted 26 Nov 1949; in force 26 Jan 1950.
  • Preamble: sovereign, socialist, secular, democratic, republic; justice, liberty, equality, fraternity.

14. Electoral Politics

  • Elections: choose representatives; Lok Sabha seats 543. Reserved constituencies for SC/ST.
  • Election Commission conducts polls; Model Code of Conduct; voter list; EVM.
  • Free and fair: universal adult franchise (18+), independent EC, right to contest.

15. Working of Institutions

  • Legislature: Parliament (Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha). Executive: President, PM, Council of Ministers. Judiciary: Supreme Court.
  • PM is leader of majority party in Lok Sabha; Mandal Commission case; judicial review; PIL.

16. Democratic Rights

  • Fundamental Rights: Equality, Freedom, Against Exploitation, Freedom of Religion, Cultural and Educational, Constitutional Remedies.
  • Right to Constitutional Remedies: Article 32; writs; NHRC (1993).
Economics

17. The Story of Village Palampur

  • Four factors of production: land, labour, physical capital, human capital.
  • Green Revolution: HYV seeds, fertilisers; multiple cropping. Land is fixed, so sustainability matters.
  • Non-farm activities: dairy, small manufacturing, transport; farm labourers are poor.

18. People as Resource

  • Human capital: education, health, training raise productivity. Sectors: primary, secondary, tertiary.
  • Economic vs non-economic activities; unemployment types: seasonal, disguised, educated.

19. Poverty as a Challenge

  • Poverty line: 2400 kcal rural, 2100 kcal urban. Social exclusion and vulnerability.
  • Schemes: MGNREGA 2005, PMRY, SGSY; causes: low growth, inequality, population.

20. Food Security in India

  • Food security: availability, accessibility, affordability. Buffer stock by FCI; PDS ration shops.
  • Minimum Support Price; Antyodaya Anna Yojana; National Food Security Act 2013.
