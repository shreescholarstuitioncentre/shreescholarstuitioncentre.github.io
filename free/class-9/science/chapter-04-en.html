🧊Chapter 1: Matter in Our Surroundings

Kelvin from CelsiusK = °C + 273
Celsius from Kelvin°C = K − 273
Densityρ = mass ÷ volume (kg/m³)
Water freezes / boils0°C = 273 K ; 100°C = 373 K
Evaporation rises with↑ Temp, ↑ Surface area, ↑ Wind, ↓ Humidity
💡 Trick: Latent heat = hidden heat: temperature stays same during change of state!

🧪Chapter 2: Is Matter Around Us Pure?

SolutionSolution = Solute + Solvent
Mass % of solution(Mass of solute ÷ Mass of solution) × 100
Volume % of solution(Vol. of solute ÷ Vol. of solution) × 100
Mass by volume %(Mass of solute ÷ Vol. of solution) × 100
Rf value (chromatography)Rf = Distance by solute ÷ Distance by solvent
💡 Trick: Colloid scatters light (Tyndall effect), true solution does not.

⚛️Chapter 3: Atoms and Molecules

Conservation of massMass of reactants = Mass of products
Law of constant proportionsWater: H : O = 1 : 8 (by mass)
Water formation2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O
Avogadro numberNₐ = 6.022 × 10²³ per mole
Moles from massn = Given mass ÷ Molar mass
Moles from particlesn = No. of particles ÷ 6.022×10²³
Moles from gas volumen = Volume at STP ÷ 22.4 L
Molecular massSum of atomic masses of all atoms
💡 Trick: Criss-cross valency: Al³⁺ + SO₄²⁻ → Al₂(SO₄)₃

🔬Chapter 4: Structure of the Atom

Neutral atomProtons (Z) = Electrons
Mass numberA = Protons + Neutrons
Neutronsn = A − Z
Max electrons in a shell2n² (K=2, L=8, M=18, N=32)
ValencyOuter e⁻ (if ≤4) or 8 − outer e⁻
Average atomic massΣ (isotope mass × % abundance) ÷ 100
Notationᴬ_Z X (e.g. ³⁵₁₇Cl)
💡 Trick: Isotopes: same Z, different A. Isobars: same A, different Z.

🏃Chapter 7: Motion

Speedv = distance ÷ time
Velocityv = displacement ÷ time
Accelerationa = (v − u) ÷ t
1st equationv = u + at
2nd equations = ut + ½at²
3rd equationv² = u² + 2as
Average velocity (uniform a)(u + v) ÷ 2
Circular motionv = 2πr ÷ t
GraphsSlope of s-t = speed ; Slope of v-t = a ; Area of v-t = distance
💡 Trick: Remember: u = initial, v = final, a = acceleration, t = time, s = distance.

🚀Chapter 8: Force and Laws of Motion

Momentump = m × v
Newton's 2nd lawF = m × a
Force from momentumF = (mv − mu) ÷ t
Unit of force1 N = 1 kg·m/s²
Conservation of momentumm₁u₁ + m₂u₂ = m₁v₁ + m₂v₂
Gun recoilv(gun) = − (m(bullet) ÷ M(gun)) × v(bullet)
Newton's 3rd lawAction = − Reaction
💡 Trick: 1st law = Inertia (more mass, more inertia).

🌍Chapter 9: Gravitation

Universal lawF = G m₁m₂ ÷ d²
Value of G6.67 × 10⁻¹¹ N·m²/kg²
Acceleration due to gravityg = GM ÷ R² ≈ 9.8 m/s²
WeightW = m × g
Moon's gravityg(moon) = g(earth) ÷ 6
Free fallv = gt ; h = ½gt² ; v² = 2gh
PressureP = Thrust ÷ Area
Buoyant force= Weight of liquid displaced
Relative densityDensity of substance ÷ Density of water
💡 Trick: Mass stays same everywhere; weight changes with g.

⚡Chapter 10: Work and Energy

WorkW = F × s (J)
Work at angleW = F s cosθ
Kinetic energyKE = ½ m v²
Potential energyPE = m g h
PowerP = W ÷ t (1 W = 1 J/s)
Energy conservationKE + PE = constant
Work-energy theoremW = ΔKE
Commercial unit1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J
Horse power1 HP = 746 W
💡 Trick: Work = 0 if no displacement (pushing a wall!).

Min. distance for echo≈ 17.2 m (0.1 s, 344 m/s)

SONAR depthd = (v × t) ÷ 2

Infra / Ultra sound< 20 Hz / > 20 kHz
