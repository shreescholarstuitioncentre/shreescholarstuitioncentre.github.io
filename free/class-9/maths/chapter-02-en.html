Three terms(a+b+c)² = a²+b²+c²+2ab+2bc+2ca

Cube of difference(a − b)³ = a³ − b³ − 3ab(a − b)
Sum of cubesa³ + b³ = (a + b)(a² − ab + b²)
Difference of cubesa³ − b³ = (a − b)(a² + ab + b²)
Specialx³+y³+z³−3xyz = (x+y+z)(x²+y²+z²−xy−yz−zx)

💡 Trick: Signs in a³+b³ and a³−b³: first bracket SAME, second bracket flips middle sign.
