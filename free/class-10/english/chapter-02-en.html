🔀 PART 3: REORDERING OF SENTENCES

Steps: 1) Find the part with the subject, it starts the sentence. 2) Add the verb phrase. 3) Add the object or complement. 4) Add place, time and extra parts. 5) Read the sentence once to check meaning.
Example: P. went to the market   Q. every morning   R. The old man   → R–P–Q: The old man went to the market every morning.

💬 EXPLAINED Q&A

A. Concept Questions (12)

Q1. What is a tense?
The form of a verb that shows the time (past, present, future) and kind of an action.
Q2. How many tenses are there?
Twelve: three times (past, present, future) × four kinds (simple, continuous, perfect, perfect continuous).
Q3. Difference between Simple Present and Present Continuous?
Simple present: habits and facts (She sings daily). Continuous: action going on now (She is singing now).
Q4. Present Perfect vs Simple Past?
Present perfect links a past action to now, with no fixed time (I have eaten). Simple past needs a finished time (I ate at eight yesterday).
Q5. When do we use since and for?
Since goes with a point of time (since 2010, since Monday); for goes with a period (for two hours, for five years).
Q6. What is an article and what are its kinds?
A word placed before a noun to limit it. Indefinite: a, an. Definite: the. Sometimes no article is used (zero article).
Q7. When do we use an?
Before a singular countable noun that begins with a vowel SOUND: an apple, an hour, an MLA.
Q8. Difference between a and the?
a/an means any one, not specific. the means a particular, known or unique thing.
Q9. Is the used before languages, meals and games?
No. We say: She speaks Hindi. I had lunch. He plays cricket.
Q10. What is reordering of sentences?
Arranging jumbled words or parts into a meaningful, grammatically correct sentence.
Q11. How do we solve a reordering question?
Start with the subject part, then the verb, then the object, then place/time parts; read once to check meaning.
Q12. What are common errors in tenses and articles?
Using V1 or V2 after has/have/had (use V3); mixing tenses; using a before a vowel sound; using the before general plural nouns.

B. Structure, Use and Example (12)

Q13. Write the structure, use and an example of Simple Present.
Structure: S + V1 (s/es)
Use: habits, facts, universal truths
Example: He plays cricket daily.
Signal words: always, usually, every day, never
Q14. Write the structure, use and an example of Present Continuous.
Structure: S + is/am/are + V-ing
Use: action going on now
Example: She is reading a book now.
Signal words: now, at present, at the moment
Q15. Write the structure, use and an example of Present Perfect.
Structure: S + has/have + V3
Use: finished action with present result
Example: I have finished my work.
Signal words: already, just, yet, ever, so far
Q16. Write the structure, use and an example of Present Perfect Continuous.
Structure: S + has/have been + V-ing
Use: action started in the past and still continuing
Example: They have been working for two hours.
Signal words: since, for, how long, all day
Q17. Write the structure, use and an example of Simple Past.
Structure: S + V2
Use: action finished in the past
Example: He went to Delhi yesterday.
Signal words: yesterday, ago, last week, in 1990
Q18. Write the structure, use and an example of Past Continuous.
Structure: S + was/were + V-ing
Use: action in progress at a past time
Example: I was sleeping at nine.
Signal words: while, when, at that time
Q19. Write the structure, use and an example of Past Perfect.
Structure: S + had + V3
Use: earlier of two past actions
Example: The train had left before I reached.
Signal words: before, after, by the time, already
Q20. Write the structure, use and an example of Past Perfect Continuous.
Structure: S + had been + V-ing
Use: continuous action up to a past moment
Example: She had been waiting for an hour when I came.
Signal words: for, since, before, when
Q21. Write the structure, use and an example of Simple Future.
Structure: S + will/shall + V1
Use: future action, promise, decision
Example: I will call you tomorrow.
Signal words: tomorrow, next week, soon
Q22. Write the structure, use and an example of Future Continuous.
Structure: S + will be + V-ing
Use: action in progress at a future time
Example: He will be travelling at this time tomorrow.
Signal words: at this time tomorrow
Q23. Write the structure, use and an example of Future Perfect.
Structure: S + will have + V3
Use: action completed before a future time
Example: They will have finished by Monday.
Signal words: by, by the time, before
Q24. Write the structure, use and an example of Future Perfect Continuous.
Structure: S + will have been + V-ing
Use: continuous action up to a future time
Example: By June, I will have been teaching for ten years.
Signal words: by next year, for ten years by then
