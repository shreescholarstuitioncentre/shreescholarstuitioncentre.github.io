1. Voice (Active & Passive)

Voice shows whether the subject does the action (Active) or receives it (Passive).

Active: Ram writes a letter.   Passive: A letter is written by Ram.

Steps: (1) Find Subject, Verb, Object. (2) Object becomes the new subject. (3) Use the correct form of 'be' + V3 (past participle). (4) Add 'by' + old subject (pronouns change: I→me, he→him, we→us, they→them).

Tense-wise formula (passive)

Imperative: Open the door → Let the door be opened. (Request: Please open → You are requested to open.)

Questions: Who wrote this? → By whom was this written?   Do you know him? → Is he known to you?

Modals: modal + be + V3 → The work can be done.

Don't change voice of intransitive verbs (no object): He sleeps.

Exam tip: The agent (by…) may be dropped if it is unknown or unimportant: English is spoken here.

2. Narration (Direct & Indirect Speech)

Direct speech gives the exact words of the speaker inside inverted commas. Indirect speech reports the meaning in our own words.

Direct: He said, "I am happy."   Indirect: He said that he was happy.

3 rules: (1) Remove commas, add a connector. (2) Change the tense if the reporting verb is past. (3) Change pronouns (person) and time/place words.

Tense back-shift

Time & place words

Pronouns: 1st person → follows the subject of reporting verb; 2nd person → follows the object; 3rd person → no change.

Interrogative: said to → asked. Wh-question → keep the wh-word; yes/no question → if / whether. Use normal word order, no '?'.

Imperative: said to → ordered / requested / advised / forbade + to-infinitive. (Don't → not to.)

Exclamatory: exclaimed with joy / sorrow / surprise that… (Hurrah → joy; Alas → sorrow; Wow → surprise).

No tense change when: the reporting verb is present/future, or the speech is a universal truth, habit or historical fact.

3. Punctuation

Punctuation marks make writing clear, like traffic signals for a reader. Tap or hover over a mark below.

Capital letters are used for: first word of a sentence, the pronoun I, proper nouns, days, months, and the first word of a quotation.

Quick revision

Passive = be + V3.

Past reporting verb → back-shift the tense.

Reported speech has no quotes and no '?' or '!'.

End every sentence with ., ? or !.
