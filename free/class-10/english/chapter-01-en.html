Sentences and Their Kinds

A sentence is a group of words that makes complete sense. It begins with a capital letter and ends with a full stop, question mark or exclamation mark.

AssertiveStates a fact or opinion.She plays tennis.
InterrogativeAsks a question.Where do you live?
ImperativeCommand, request or advice.Please open the door.
ExclamatoryStrong feeling.What a beautiful day!
OptativeWish or prayer.May you succeed!

Punctuation

Full stop ( . )Ends a statement. He is a doctor.
Comma ( , )Separates items or short pauses. I bought milk, eggs and bread.
Question mark ( ? )Ends a question. Who are you?
Exclamation mark ( ! )Shows strong feeling. Hurrah! We won!
Apostrophe ( ’ )Shows possession or a missing letter. Ravi’s book; don’t.
Colon ( : )Introduces a list or explanation. You need three things: pen, paper, ink.
Semicolon ( ; )Joins two related sentences. It rained; we stayed home.
Quotation marks ( “ ” )Enclose spoken words. He said, “Come in.”
Hyphen ( - )Joins words. Well-known, mother-in-law.
